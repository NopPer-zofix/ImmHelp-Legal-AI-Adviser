## The Capital Gains Tax Law of 1980 (52/1980)

## Short title.
1.- This Law may be cited as the Capital Gains Taxation Law of 1980.

*(52/1980)*

## Interpretation
2.- (1) In this Law, unless otherwise stated in the text―
"immovable property" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Immovable Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Act;
"restructuring" means the direct or indirect disposal and transfer of immovable property, as well as the transfer of rights under a deed of sale filed with the Department of Land and Survey, between one or more borrowers and/or debtors and/or guarantors regarding the same credit facility or grant or debt and one or more lenders, which is carried out within eleven (11) years from the effective date of the Tax Capital Gains (Amendment) (No. 4) Law of 2015 and aims to reduce or repay credit facilities or grants or debts given to borrowers by one or more lenders:
Provided that, the disposal and transfer of immovable property, as well as the transfer of rights pursuant to a sale deed filed with the Department of Cadastre and Land Surveying, may be carried out to a lender and/or to a non-"connected person", as this term is interpreted in accordance with the provisions of this Law:
It is further provided that the disposition and transfer of real property, as well as the transfer of rights pursuant to a deed of sale filed with the Department of Cadastre and Land Surveying to a non-"connected person", excluding the lender, falls within this definition only in relation to the reduction or repayment of those credit facilities or loans or debts arising in the context of a written agreement with the lender:
It is further provided that the above provisions apply to non-performing loans (NPLs) on or before December 31, 2015, as this term is interpreted by the European Banking Authority:
It is further provided that the disposal and transfer of immovable property to the Cyprus Asset Management Company Ltd with registration number HE 387704 or to its subsidiary company, which is carried out in the context of the "RENT AGAINST INSTALLMENT" plan, is also considered a restructuring, and this provision applies to non-performing credit facilities on December 31, 2021 and on December 31 2022, as defined in the "RENT AGAINST INSTALLMENT" plan;
"Reorganisation" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Income Tax Act;
"adjusted acquisition value" means the amount of the market value of the property at the time of its acquisition reduced by the amount of capital gain, which arose in the past and for which, according to the provisions of this Law, no tax has been paid;
"reverse finance lease" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Finance Lease Law;
"simple finance lease" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Finance Lease Law;
"borrower" means a person who entered into an agreement with the lender "and includes a person whose credit facility was purchased in accordance with the provisions of the Purchase and Sale of Credit Facilities and Related Matters Law and was either taken over by or transferred to a public body or company that is licensed and who maintains or to whom or to which loans from a credit institution that are in arrears have been transferred and/or have become overdue under the technical standards of the European Banking Authority, which define the criteria for classifying a grant as non-performing and/or regulated as established by the European Commission through Executive Regulation 2015/227:
Provided that, any related person of the first borrower is also considered a borrower, as the term "related person" is interpreted in accordance with the provisions of article 33 of the Income Tax Law, provided that the disposal and transfer of immovable property, as well as the transfer of rights pursuant to a sale deed filed with the Department of Land and Surveying, is carried out for the benefit of the lender;
"lender" means a licensed credit institution, as defined in article 2 of the Law on Operations of Credit Institutions and includes the credit and financial institutions operating in the Republic pursuant to the provisions of article 10A thereof, as well as their subsidiaries or a credit acquisition company, as defined in accordance with the provisions of article 2 of the Law on Buying and Selling Credit Facilities and Related Matters, as well as its subsidiaries or a public body or company that is licensed and which holds or to which or to which have been transferred from a credit institution loans that are in arrears and/or have become overdue by virtue of the technical standards of the European Banking Authority, which determine the criteria for classifying a loan as non-performing and/or regulated as established by the European Commission through the Executive of Regulation 2015/227;
"public company" has the meaning assigned to that term in the Income Tax Act:
It is provided that companies that were considered public companies before the entry into force of this Law are considered public companies for a period of three years from the entry into force of this Law, regardless of the fact that they do not meet all the conditions of this definition;
"Director" means the Director of the Inland Revenue Department;
"lessor" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Finance Leases Act;
"company" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Income Tax Act;
"Commissioner" means the Commissioner of Taxation as defined under the provisions of the Taxation Department Act and includes any officer of the Taxation Department duly authorized by him;
"property" means –
(i) immovable property situated in the Republic;
(ii) shares of companies whose property also consists of immovable property located in the Republic,
(iii) shares of companies that directly or indirectly participate in a company or companies that own immovable property located in the Republic and at least twenty percent (20%) of the market value of such shares is derived from the market value of the immovable property located in the Republic, and
(iv) agreement to sell property located in the Republic:
Provided that, in calculating whether the value of the immovable property represents at least twenty percent (20%) of the value of the shares, any liabilities shall not be taken into account;
"profit" means the profit of any person which arises after the effective date of this Law due to the disposition of property and which is not a profit falling within the provisions of the Income Tax Laws in force from time to time and includes profit which is exempt from tax in accordance with the provisions of the Income Tax Law.
"Base Sovereign Area" means the British Sovereign Base Area of Akrotiri as well as the British Sovereign Base Area of Dekelia, as defined in Article 1 of the Treaty Establishing the Republic, signed in Nicosia on August 16, 1960;
"lease" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Finance Lease Act;
"lessee" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Finance Leases Act;
"family" for the purposes of this Law includes spouses and persons up to the third degree of consanguinity, whether they are unmarried or married;
"organization of persons" [Deleted];
"person" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Income Tax Act;
"regulated market" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Investment Services and Activities and Regulated Markets Act;
"connected person" means a connected person as provided by virtue of the provisions of article 33 of the Income Tax Law;
"Grant Scheme" means the Grant Scheme for Existing Apartment Buildings in Government IDP Housing Settlements which was approved on April 7, 2023 by the Council of Ministers, by decision no. 94,640∙
"restructuring price" means the price at which immovable property is transferred in the context of restructuring, and which is determined in a written agreement entered into in this context;
"tax" means the tax imposed under this Act;
"finance lease" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Finance Lease Law.

## Director
3.- (1) The Director is responsible for the good and faithful implementation of this Law, however, he may not take any action that he deems necessary or appropriate for the implementation of the provisions of this Law.
(2) The exercise of powers and responsibilities referred to the implementation of this Law may be delegated by the Director to another or other officials of the Department of Internal Revenue.

*(52/1980)*

## Imposition of the tax
4.- Subject to the provisions of this Law, and subject to the exceptions contained in this Law, on all profit due to the disposal of property, a tax at a rate of twenty percent is imposed and paid on such profit.

*(52/1980)*

## Exceptions
5.-(1) No tax shall be paid when the total profit resulting from the disposition or dispositions of property held by a natural person does not exceed the amount of thirty thousand euros (€30,000) or in the case of the disposition of agricultural land held by a natural person whose main occupation is agriculture does not exceed the amount of fifty thousand euros (€50,000), whether this occurred during the same year or any other year.
(2) No tax is paid in the event that the profit arising from the disposal of a main residence, which is used by the owner for a total period of at least five years exclusively for owner-occupation purposes and is located on an area of land up to one and a half decares, does not exceed the amount of one hundred and fifty thousand euros (€150,000):
Provided that:
(i) In case the profit exceeds the amount of one hundred and fifty thousand euros (€150,000), tax is paid on the amount that exceeds one hundred and fifty thousand euros (€150,000);
(ii) in case the residence is situated on an area of land in excess of one and a half acres, a tax is levied on the proportion of the gain arising from the disposal of the land in excess of one and a half acres;
(iii) in the case of a second or more disposal of a residence, the total period of use by the owner exclusively for owner-occupation purposes is increased to a minimum of ten years;
(iv) in case of disposal of the residence after the lapse of at least one year from the cessation of ownership, no exception will be granted;
(n) no person is entitled to both the exemptions granted under this subsection and subsection (1) of this section, but is entitled to only one of the two exemptions, whichever is the greater.
(3) No tax shall be paid on the profit arising from the disposal of shares listed on a regulated market of a recognized Stock Exchange.
(3A) No tax is paid on the profit, which arises to a person from the disposal of shares listed on a non-regulated market of a recognized Stock Exchange, if the total value of all disposals made within the same calendar year does not cumulatively exceed the amount of fifty thousand euros (€50,000):
Provided that, if the total value of all dispositions made by a person within the same calendar year cumulatively exceeds the amount of fifty thousand euros (€50,000), then a tax is imposed and paid on the profit of each disposition in accordance with the provisions of this Law on the date when the total value of said dispositions exceeded the amount of fifty thousand euros (€50,000):
It is further provided that, regardless of the total value of the transaction, no tax shall be paid on the profit that arises to a person from the disposal of shares listed on a non-regulated market of a recognized Stock Exchange, which the person in question held on 31h December 2025 and which on that date were listed on a non-regulated market of a recognized Stock Exchange.
(4) (a) No tax shall be paid for profit arising from the disposal of the main residence used by the owner exclusively for owner-occupation purposes, in cases where the proceeds of such disposal do not exceed the amount of four hundred and fifty thousand euros (€450,000) and the disposal is carried out-
(i) Regardless of the provisions of paragraph (b) of this subsection, in the context of restructuring:
It is provided that, in the event that any part of the disposal product is returned to the disposer, then on the date of the return, the tax exempted pursuant to this subsection shall be paid up to the amount of the amount returned:
It is further provided that, the validity of the provisions of this subsection is suspended, from the date of entry into force of the Capital Gains Taxation (Amendment) (No. 4) Law of 2015 until the date of its expiry:
Provided further that, regardless of the provisions of article 2, for the purposes of this subparagraph-
"restructuring" means the disposal and transfer or the transfer of rights pursuant to a deed of sale filed with the Department of Lands and Surveys, principal residence, by a borrower and/or guarantor, regarding the same credit facility, to a lender or non-"related person", which is intended to reduce or pay off the credit facilities granted to the borrower by the lender and which relate to non-performing sponsorships (MEX) on or before December 31, 2020, as this term is interpreted by the European Banking Authority;
"borrower" means a person contracted with the lender and includes a person whose credit facility was acquired in accordance with the provisions of the Purchase and Sale of Credit Facilities and Related Matters Law and was either taken over by or transferred to a public body or company that is licensed and which holds or to which loans from a credit institution that are in arrears have been transferred and/or have become overdue under the technical standards of the European Banking Authority, which define the criteria for classifying a grant as non-performing and/or regulated as established by the European Commission through Executive Regulation 2015/227;
"lender" means a licensed credit institution, as defined in article 2 of the Law on Operations of Credit Institutions and includes the credit and financial institutions operating in the Republic pursuant to the provisions of article 10A thereof, as well as their subsidiaries or a credit acquisition company, as defined in accordance with the provisions of article 2 of the Law on Buying and Selling Credit Facilities and Related Matters, as well as its subsidiaries or a public body or company that is licensed and which holds or to which or to which have been transferred from a credit institution loans that are in arrears and/or have become overdue by virtue of the technical standards of the European Banking Authority, which determine the criteria for classifying a loan as non-performing and/or regulated as established by the European Commission through the Executive Regulation 2015/227.
(ii) in the context of a compromise or scheme of arrangement approved by the Court or by the Official Receiver or Trustee acting in accordance with a Bankruptcy Order made under the provisions of the Bankruptcy Law:
It is provided that, in the event that an undistributed part of the bankruptcy estate is returned to the bankrupt, then on the date of the return, the tax exempted pursuant to this subsection shall be paid up to the amount or value of the property returned to the bankrupt:
Provided further that, in the event that-
(aa) the Court sets aside a compromise or scheme of arrangement on the ground that the Court's approval of such compromise or scheme of arrangement was obtained by fraud, or
(bb) the Court by order sets aside the debtor's bankruptcy on the ground that, in the Court's opinion, the debtor should not have been declared bankrupt,
the tax exempted under this subsection is payable from the date of disposal;
(iii) in the context of a Personal Repayment Plan which is in force in accordance with the provisions of the Personal Insolvency (Personal Repayment Plans and Debt Relief Order) Law of 2015:
It is provided that, in the event that any undistributed part of the property is returned to the debtor, then on the date of the return, the tax exempted pursuant to this subsection shall be paid up to the amount or value of the property returned to the debtor:
Provided further that, where a debtor is guilty of a criminal offense under the provisions of the Personal Insolvency (Personal Repayment Plans and Debt Relief Order) Act 2015, the tax exempted under this subsection shall be payable from the date of the disposition;
(iv) in the context of a company liquidation decree or in the context of the appointment of an examiner by the Court pursuant to the provisions of the Companies Law:
Provided that, in the event that any assets are distributed to the shareholders of the company, then on the date of the distribution the tax exempted under this subsection shall be paid up to the amount or value of the property distributed to the shareholders;
(n) pursuant to the provisions of Part VIA of the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law:
Provided that, in the event that any balance from the proceeds of sale is paid to the mortgagor, then the tax exempted under this subsection shall be paid up to the amount of the balance paid to the mortgagor.
For the purposes of this paragraph-
(j) in the event that n residence belongs to a company and this is the main residence of its shareholder and is used by the shareholder exclusively for the purposes of own residence, then the shareholder is considered the owner of the residence;
(ii) The exceptions provided for in this paragraph (a) apply in relation to a disposal that takes place up to and including the 31sth December 2030.
(b) No tax is payable on a gain arising from the disposal of property in the context of any other restructuring:
It is provided that, in the case of disposal or possession for own use of immovable property by the lender which was acquired in the context of restructuring for the purposes of taxation of the lender pursuant to the Capital Gains Taxation Law, the cost of acquisition is considered to be the restructuring price and the disposal proceeds are reduced by any amount returned to the borrower:
Provided further that, in the event that:
(i) upon restructuring or
(ii) upon the disposal or possession for personal use of immovable property by the lender, which was acquired in the context of restructuring,
any part of the disposal proceeds is returned or left over, as the case may be, to the borrower, then tax shall be paid by the borrower pro rata on the amount returned or left over, as the case may be.
For the purposes of this proviso-
(i) the resulting tax shall be borne by the borrower and withheld by the lender from such amount refunded or surplus, as the case may be and paid to the Commissioner.
(ii) the tax arising and payable under this proviso and the corresponding provisions of the Capital Gains Taxation (Amendment) (No. 4) Law of 2015 and the Income Taxation Law, do not in their entirety exceed the amount refunded or left over, as the case may be:
It is further provided that, in the event that the Director, in the context of restructuring, finds that exemption from the obligation to pay tax or imposition of zero tax based on the provisions of the Capital Gains Tax (Amendment) (No. 4) Law of 2015 is wrong due to actions of the person which are proven not to be genuine or are fictitious, he has the authority to, despite the provisions of the above law, proceed to imposition on this person of the correct amount of tax.

## Special provision
5A.(1) Irrespective of the provisions of this Law, disposal of property which consists of immovable property consisting of land, land with buildings or land with buildings, is exempt from the payment of tax, provided that:
(i) Acquired within the period prescribed from the commencement of the Capital Gains Taxation (Amendment) (No. 2) Law of 2015 and 31h December 2016;
(ii) acquired by purchase or purchase agreement, but not by exchange or donation, at its market value from an unrelated person, as this term is interpreted by the provisions of the Income Tax Law in force from time to time:
Provided that, disposal of immovable property, within the meaning of this article, falls under the exemption from the obligation to pay tax based on the provisions of this Law, regardless of whether it was carried out within the above specified period or at any time after its expiry.
(2) No exemption from the obligation to impose and pay tax, based on the provisions of this Law, applies, in relation to the disposal of property within the meaning of this article, which was acquired in the context of a property sale procedure pursuant to the provisions of Part VI and Part VIA of the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law.

## Special provision regarding the Grant Scheme
5B.(a) Irrespective of the provisions of this Law, in case of disposal of immovable property by a Beneficiary to the Republic of Cyprus or to a private developer, which is done in the context of the implementation of the Grant Plan, the Beneficiary is exempt from paying the tax:
It is provided that, in compliance with the provisions of article 6, for the purposes of taxation of the Beneficiary pursuant to this Law, in the event of a subsequent disposal of the relevant immovable property, which is carried out outside the context of the Sponsorship Plan, the cost of acquisition shall be considered in relation to-
(i) immovable property acquired by a Beneficiary through the reconstruction of an apartment building in the context of the implementation of the Grant Plan, the amount of the government grant granted to him under the said plan and the additional amount of contribution he paid to acquire the said immovable property,
(ii) real property of the Beneficiary that was repaired, strengthened or reconstructed in the context of the implementation of the Grant Plan, the amount of government funding granted to him in the context of said plan for the repair, strengthening or reconstruction of the real property, as well as the additional amount of government funding corresponding to the real property of the Beneficiary for the installation of an elevator.
(b) For the purposes of this article, the term "Beneficiary" means a person who participates in the Sponsorship Scheme.

## Determination of profit.
6. In computing the profit shall be deducted—
(a) The value of the property on the 1st day of January, 1980, as determined or to be determined under sections 66, 67, 69, 70, 71 and 72 of the Real Estate (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Act or by any new section or proviso thereto, or by ordinance, regulation or decree in relation to the Real Estate Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Act, as well as the subsequent increase in the value of the property due to inflation:
(i) In the event that the property was not freehold, the value of the property as such on 1.1.1980 shall be calculated as its market value as determined by the Director of the Department of Land and Survey;
(ii) in the event that the value of the property has not been determined in accordance with sections 66, 67, 69 70, 71 and 72 of the Real Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Act or any relevant new article or proviso or by order, regulation or decree in relation to the Real Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Act, value of the property as it was on 1.1.1980, its market value is calculated as determined by the Director of the Department of Cadastre and Land Surveying;
(iii) in the event that in the general assessment of the property, any additions or changes made after January 1, 1980 were taken into account, the value of the property as it was on 1.1.1980, before the additions or changes were made, shall be considered its market value as determined by the Director of the Department of Land and Survey;
(iv) in the event that the property allocated has arisen or will arise after division into plots, the value of the allocated property on 1.1.80 is calculated as the part of the market value of the property as it was before the partition that corresponds to the allocated property, as determined by the Director of the Department of Cadastre and Land Survey;
(v) in the event that part or all of the value is allowed as a deduction, in accordance with the Income Tax Laws in force from time to time, such part of the value shall not be deductible;
(vi) in case the owner elects, the market value of the property on July 14, 1974, instead of the value on January 1, 1980, shall be deducted;.
(vii) in case the disposition concerns the granting of a right to purchase or a right to exchange or a right to lease property no amount shall be deducted with respect to the value of the property;
(viii) where the disposal is for the collection of an amount under a term of agreement in respect of the cancellation of the sale or agreement of sale or exchange or lease, no amount shall be deducted in respect of the value of the property.
(b) Any expenditure incurred wholly and solely for the purpose of making a profit after January 1, 1980 and which is not deductible under the Income Tax Laws for the time being in force, adjusted for inflation:
Provided that, in the event that the disposition concerns the granting of a right to purchase or the right to exchange or the right to lease property or the collection of an amount based on a term of an agreement regarding the cancellation of the sale or the sale agreement or the exchange or the lease, only expenses incurred entirely and exclusively for the realization of the disposition and which do not relate to the value of the property shall be deducted.
(c) For the purposes of this article inflation is calculated based on the retail price index issued from time to time by the Department of Statistics and Research of the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Cyprus.
(d) In case of disposal of company shares, the value of the immovable property on 1h January 1980 or the acquisition value of the immovable property on the date of its acquisition by the company which is its direct owner or the market value of the immovable property on the date of acquisition of the said shares by the person disposing of them, whichever of these dates is later:
It is understood that, in case –
(i) Disposition of immovable property which was directly or indirectly the property of a company or companies during the preceding disposal of shares, or
(ii) disposal of shares of a company that directly owns immovable property or
(iii) disposal of shares of a company that directly or indirectly participates in a company or companies
and in which disposal involves immovable property for which tax was imposed and paid on a previous disposal, the value of the immovable property deducted is equal to the amount of the proceeds of disposal used for purposes of calculating the gain in the immediately preceding disposal in which such immovable property was involved:
It is further provided that, in the event that the shares available were acquired by a related person in a transaction which was not subject to taxation pursuant to the provisions of the Capital Gains Taxation (Amendment) (No. 3) Law of 2015, the date of acquisition of the shares shall be disregarded for the purposes of this subsection.

## I'm damaged
7.― (1) The amount of loss resulting from the disposal of property is calculated in the same way as the profit from the disposal of property is calculated.
(2) When the amount of loss that arises due to the disposal of property is such that it cannot be set off in its entirety after a profit due to the simultaneous disposal of another property, the amount of such loss, to the extent that it is not offset in this way, is transferred and set off after the profit due to a subsequent disposal of property until this loss depreciated.

*(52/1980)*

## Time of acquisition of profit or loss.
8. The profit or loss due to the disposal of property is considered as arising or, as the case may be, as arising at the time of the disposal of such property.

*(52/1980)*

## Property disposal product
9.-(1) The property disposal product is the amount declared by the parties:
It is provided that in case the statement is contested by the Director, the latter is entitled, no later than six months from the date of the statement, to carry out an investigation and if it is demonstrably found that the statement was false he has the right to demand the payment of tax on the difference between the amount of the statement and the amount of the proven disposal of the property and interest as determined by virtue of the Uniform Public Default Interest Law, from the day of payment of the tax based on the declaration of the contracting parties:
It is further provided that, in the event that the disposal concerns the granting of a right to purchase or a right to exchange or a right to lease property or the collection of an amount based on a term of an agreement regarding the cancellation of the sale or the sale agreement or the exchange or lease, the proceeds of the disposal of the property shall be the amount collected:
It is further provided that, in the event that the product of property disposal includes an amount for granting the right to purchase or the right to exchange or the right to lease the property, the collection of which has preceded and which is taxed based on the provisions of this Law, the product of property disposal does not include the amount in question.
(2) In case of disposal of company shares, the product of the disposal of the property is calculated exclusively on the basis of the immovable property of the company and of the companies in which it directly or indirectly participates, and is calculated as an amount equal to the amount that the immovable property, in the opinion of the Director, would yield if it were sold on the open market at the time when the disposal of shares took place:
It is provided that, in the event that company shares are disposed of, the market value of which is represented and essentially derived from the market value of the immovable property, the disposal proceeds of the shares, as declared by the contracting parties, adjusted to the market value of any other assets and liabilities, shall be considered as the disposal proceeds of the property.
(3) If no purchase or sale has taken place, there shall be deemed to have been paid or received, as the case may be, an amount equal to the amount such property, in the opinion of the Director, would have fetched if offered or sold, as the case may be, in the open market at the time the event occurred.
(4) In the event of a sale between related persons, as this term is interpreted by the currently applicable Income Tax Law, where an amount is declared as disposal product which is less than the market value of the property, the amount of the market value of the property on the date of disposal, as calculated by the Director, will be considered as disposal product.

## Disposition of Property.
10. For the purposes of this Law, disposal of property includes sale, sale by the Director of the Department of Cadastre and Land Surveying or by a Provincial Land Surveyor by public auction, agreement of sale, assignment of rights deriving from a contract of sale of property, exchange, lease, or registered in accordance with the provisions of the currently in force on Real Estate (Possession, Registration and Appraisal) of Law, freehold, relinquishment of use or exploitation of any related right, grant of right to purchase or right of exchange or right to lease property, as well as collection of any amount under a term of agreement in respect of cancellation of the sale or agreement to sell or exchange or lease, but does not include―
(a) transfer cause of death;
Provided that in such case the value of the property shall be the value at the time of acquisition or the adjusted acquisition value at the time of acquisition of the property by the deceased or its value on January 1, 1980, whichever of these dates is later:
It is further provided that, in case the property was acquired by the deceased before July 14, 1974, the testator may choose to consider the value of the property as the value it had on July 14, 1974.
(b) A gift made from a parent to a child, whether married or unmarried, between spouses or between persons related up to the third degree, whether these persons are unmarried or married:
It is provided that in this case, the value of the property shall be the original value or the adjusted acquisition value, whichever is lower at the time of acquisition of the property by the donor, or its value on January 1, 1980, whichever of these dates is later. In the case of successive donations, the donor for the purposes of this reservation is the first donor:
It is further provided that in a case where the property was held by the donor before July 14, 1974, the donee may elect that the value of the property be counted as its value on July 14, 1974;
(b1) Donation of immovable property made by a foster parent to a foster child:
It is understood that, for the purposes of this paragraph, "foster parent" and "foster child" have the meaning assigned to these terms by Part III of the Children Law.
(c) Exchange in respect of property vesting in each of the parties effecting the exchange:
(i) In the event that the value at the time of acquisition of the property or the adjusted acquisition value of the property, whichever is less, or its value on January 1, 1980, whichever is later, is equal to or greater than the value of the property for which it is exchanged at the time of the exchange, tax is payable on the entire gain on the disposition.
(ii) In the event that the value at the time of acquisition of the property or the adjusted acquisition value of the property, whichever is the lesser, or its value on January 1, 1980, whichever is later, is less than the value of the property for which it is exchanged at the time of the exchange, tax is payable only on the gain not applied to the acquisition of the other property. In this case the acquisition cost of the new property that comes into the possession of the testator is reduced by the amount of the profit for which no tax was paid.
(iii) Where the property was acquired by the previous owner prior to July 14, 1974, the owner may elect to have the property valued at its value on July 14, 1974;
Provided further that, for the purposes of this paragraph, the term "exchange" also includes consideration, where the term "consideration" means the agreement pursuant to which-
(i) an owner of a parcel or parcels of land disposes of part of them to a land development entrepreneur for the construction of a building, in return for granting the owner, after construction, the right of ownership over certain units or parts of the building being erected and the construction of said units or parts provided for in the agreement is completed within five (5) years from the date of the agreement; or
(ii) an owner of a parcel of land disposes of a part of it to a land developer for his fee for the subdivision of the parcel of land into lots, and the separate title deeds of the parcels resulting from the subdivision are issued within five (5) years from the date of the agreement.
For the purposes of this paragraph, the term "land development entrepreneur" has the meaning assigned to it by subsection (6) of article 9A of the Roads and Buildings Regulation Law:
Provided further that, in the event that the construction of the units or sections referred to in paragraph (i) of the second proviso or the issuance of separate title deeds of the lots referred to in paragraph (ii) of the second proviso is not completed within five (5) years from the date of the agreement, the disposition of a piece of land or part thereof by the owner to the land development entrepreneur shall constitute a disposition of property, the gain of which shall be taxed and paid in accordance with the provisions herein of Law on the date of completion of five (5) years from the date of the agreement.
(d) A donation made to a limited liability company of which the shareholders are members and continue for a period of five years after the donation to be members of the donor's family:
Provided that in such case the value of the property shall be the value at the time of acquisition or the adjusted acquisition value at the time of acquisition of the property by the donor, whichever is the lesser, or its value on January 1, 1980, whichever date is later:
It is further provided that, in case the property was acquired by the donor before July 14, 1974, the donee may choose to consider the value of the property as the value it had on July 14, 1974.
It is also understood that this paragraph also applies in the event that the shares of a member of the testator's family pass, before the expiration of five years after the donation, to a non-member of the testator's family, due to inheritance.
(e) A donation made by a limited liability company, all the shareholders of which are members of the same family, to a shareholder thereof when the object of the donation was acquired by the said company also by donation:
Provided that in such case the value of the property shall be the value at the time of its acquisition or the adjusted acquisition value at the time of acquisition of the property by the donor, whichever is the lesser, or its value on January 1, 1980, whichever of these dates is later:
It is further provided that, in case the property was acquired by the donor before July 14, 1974, the donee may choose to consider the value of the property as the value it had on July 14, 1974:
It is also understood that, in the case of a donation by the company to its shareholder, the exception provided for in article 5 will not be granted, when the property is disposed of by the donee before the lapse of at least three years from its transfer to his name.
(f) Donation to the Republic and donation made for educational, educational or other charitable purposes to a local authority or to any charitable institution in the Republic approved by the Council of Ministers.
(g) exchange or sale pursuant to the Consolidation and Redistribution of Agricultural Estates Laws in force at any time.
(h) transfer of ownership in case of reorganization:
Provided that in such case the value of the property shall be the value at the time of acquisition or the adjusted acquisition value at the time of acquisition of the property by the testator, whichever is the lesser, or its value on January 1, 1980, whichever of these dates is later;
(i) the transfer of shares in the event of a reorganization, representing the capital of the receiving or acquiring company to or from a shareholder of the transferring or acquired company in exchange for shares representing the capital of the latter company:
Provided that in such case the value of the property shall be the value at the time of acquisition or the adjusted acquisition value at the time of acquisition of the property by the testator, whichever is the lesser, or its value on January 1, 1980, whichever of these dates is later.
(j)(i) In the event of a transfer of property between persons who were spouses and whose marriage has been dissolved by the issuance of a divorce decree by a competent court, or in the event of a transfer of property between the same persons for the purpose of regulating their property relations based on the Law on the Regulation of Property Relations of Spouses if it is provided for in a decree or in a decision of a competent court, which resolves or settles the any property disputes between them:
Provided that in this case, the value of the property shall be the original value or the adjusted acquisition value, whichever is lower at the time of acquisition of the property by the ex-spouse or its value on January 1, 1980, whichever of these dates is later. In case the property was acquired by the ex-spouse through donation, the provisions of paragraph (b) of this article shall apply:
Provided further that in case the property was acquired by the ex-spouse prior to July 14, 1974, the owner may elect to have the property valued at its value on July 14, 1974.
(ii) The provisions of sub-paragraph (i) shall apply in respect of any pending proceeding for the regulation of property relations of spouses under the Regulation of Spousal Property Relations Law or in respect of any pending divorce action before a family court or proceedings for dissolution of marriage before an ecclesiastical court, on the date of entry into force of the Capital Gains Tax (Amendment) Law of 2008.
(k) (i) Simple finance lease or reverse finance lease in which there is a right to purchase the lease from the lessee, which has been registered in accordance with the provisions of the Immovable Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law.
(ii) Transfer from the lessee to the lessor of the lease-back lease which provides for a right to purchase the lease from the lessee, which has been registered in accordance with the provisions of the Real Estate (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law:
Provided that, in such a case, the value of the property at the time of acquisition or the adjusted acquisition value at the time of acquisition of the property by the lessee, whichever is the lower, or its value at 1h January 1980, whichever is later, regardless of the amount paid by the lessor to the lessee.
(iii) Transfer from the lessor to the lessee, of the simple finance lease or reverse finance lease in which there is a right to purchase the lease from the lessee, which has been registered in accordance with the provisions of the Immovable Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law:
Provided that, in the case of a simple financial lease, the value of the property shall be the value of its acquisition by the lessor:
It is further provided that, in the case of a reverse financing lease, the value of the property at the time of its initial acquisition or the adjusted acquisition value at the time of the initial acquisition of the property by the lessee, whichever is the lower, or its value at 1h January 1980, whichever is later.
(l) Donation to a political party and acquisition by it of immovable property:
Provided that, the term "political party" has the meaning attributed to this term by the provisions of the Law on Political Parties, which is registered in the Office of the Compliance of the Registry of Political Parties Law according to the provisions of article 3 of the said Law.

## Administrative fines
23A. Regardless of the provisions of articles 20, 21, 22 and 23 of this Law-

(e) a person who fails to pay tax due by the time specified in this Act or by the time specified in a notice to the Director, shall be subject to a fine equal to five percent (5%) of the tax due:
