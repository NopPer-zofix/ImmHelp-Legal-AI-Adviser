## The Law of 2019 on the Regulation of the Establishment and Operation of Hotels and Tourist Accommodations (34(I)/2019)

## Short title
1. This Law shall be referred to as the Regulation of the Establishment and Operation of Hotels and Tourist Accommodations Law of 2019.

*(34(I)/2019)*

## Interpretation
2. In this Law, unless a different meaning arises from the text-
"operating licence" means a hotel operating license issued under section 6 or a tourist accommodation operating license issued under section 13;
"urban hotel" means a hotel which is zoned according to the current development plan
"self-catering accommodation" means either an individual furnished tourist villa or an individual residence or an individual apartment, which is a "unit" under the Real Estate (Occupation, Registration and Valuation) Act, which is not a "hotel" or "tourist accommodation" as defined in Parts II and III, which is let as a villa or residence or unit and not a part thereof and which is registered in the Register of Self-catering Accommodations pursuant to the provisions of Part IIIA;
"Accredited Classification Organization" means the classification organization as defined in Article 7;
"manager" means the person who exercises the management of the hotel and/or tourist accommodation;
"hotel or tourist accommodation or self-catering accommodation operator" means the natural or legal person to whom the entrepreneur and/or owner may entrust the management and administration of the hotel or tourist accommodation or self-catering accommodation:
It is understood that, in this case, the administrator bears the responsibilities of the entrepreneur
"entrepreneur" means the natural or legal person, in whose name the hotel operating license is issued in accordance with the provisions of article 6 or the tourist accommodation operating license in accordance with the provisions of article 13 or the license to register the self-catering accommodation in accordance with the provisions of article 16A and/or has the financial exploitation of the hotel or the tourist accommodation or the self-catering accommodation and/or the responsibility for the management of
"Regulations" means the Regulations issued pursuant to Article 19 ∙
"Regulation (EC) No. 765/2008" means Regulation (EC) no. 765/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of July 9, 2008 to determine the requirements for accreditation and market surveillance regarding the marketing of products and to repeal Regulation (EEC) no. 339/93 of the Council;
"Regulation (EU) 2024/1028" means Regulation (EU) 2024/1028 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 April 2024 on the collection and sharing of data relating to short-term rental services and amending Regulation (EU) 2018/1724:
It is provided that, where in this Law reference is made to an obligation to comply with this Law, it is considered that this obligation also includes the obligation to comply with Regulation (EU) 2024/1028, as well as with all acts of the European Union issued pursuant to said Regulation:
It is further understood that, for the purposes of implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1028, in this Law, the definitions referred to in Article 3 of said regulation shall apply.
"Cyprus Quality Promotion Organization" means the Cyprus Quality Promotion Organization established in 2002 under the Accreditation, Standardization and Technical Information Law;
"Register of Self-Catering Accommodation" means the register maintained by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism in which all self-catering accommodation is registered;
"MTA" means Mixed Tourism Developments, where:
(a) on the same field, but in a separate development from the main hotel and outside the building of the hotel unit, coexist with the hotel unit and privately owned private self-contained residential units, such as villas/residences, bungalows or apartments or other permitted uses, in a development of collective ownership, with common areas and which can be created or operated and/or
(b) within the building of the hotel unit coexist with the rooms, apartments of the hotel unit and residential suites and residential apartments which are self-contained units in a single development, with common areas and centralized management in accordance with the applicable terms and conditions referred to in the relevant policy as amended or replaced from time to time which is announced by the Deputy Minister, or any person, body or authority he designates;
"hotel", "hotel unit" or "hotel shop" means an accommodation in which temporary accommodation with or without food is provided professionally, to those who come to it, and has a valid operating license, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it;
"unstarred hotel", "unstarred hotel unit" or "unstarred hotel outlet" means an accommodation which does not meet the criteria for classification in a class of one (1) or more stars, which professionally provides, to those who come to it, temporary accommodation with or without food, and has a valid operating license in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it:
Provided that, the "unstarred" accommodation is prohibited from using the terms "hotel", "hotel unit" or "hotel accommodation" without completing the term "unstarred";
"rural hotel" means a hotel located within the Community core of mountainous and disadvantaged areas or in other suitable locations within the development boundary of these areas, in accordance with the applicable development plan;
"development plan" means the development plan as defined in the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Act;
"unstarred tourist accommodation", means an accommodation that does not meet the criteria for classification as "Luxury", "A' Class" or "B' Class", in which temporary accommodation with or without food is provided professionally, to those who come to it, and has a valid operating license in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it;
"tourist accommodation" means accommodation or organized space, other or hotel, in which accommodation or facilities for accommodation are professionally provided and has a valid operating license in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it;
"Minister" means the Minister of Finance
"Deputy Ministry of Tourism" has the meaning given to it by virtue of article 3 of the Establishment of a Deputy Ministry of Tourism and the Appointment of a Deputy Minister of Tourism despite the President and Related Matters Law;
"Deputy Minister to the President for Tourism" or "Deputy Minister" means the person appointed by the President of the Republic pursuant to article 6 of the Law on the Establishment of the Deputy Ministry of Tourism and the Appointment of the Deputy Minister of Tourism to the President and Related Matters.

## Hotel distinctions
3.-(1) Hotels or hotel units are distinguished in-
(a) Mainly hotels
(b) small housing estates
(c) traditional buildings and/or
(d) any other hotel accommodations, as well as a combination of the above together, with or without tourist accommodations as may be determined.
(2) A hotel accommodation is defined as a main hotel, the building composition of which consists of an independent main building or of several buildings that make up a single whole within a single space, which was erected or arranged specifically for this purpose and which has a single functional organization, which includes the premises, furniture, equipment and facilities, as defined in the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it.
(3) A hotel accommodation is defined as a complex of small houses that consists in whole or in part of individual small houses built in a sparse arrangement within a single space, which is served by means of one or more central buildings which have the necessary common and auxiliary spaces, and have the characteristics mentioned in subsection (2).
(4) A traditional building is a hotel accommodation housed in an independent building or part of a building in respect of which a Conservation Decree was issued, pursuant to Article 38 of the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Law, or which has been designated as an ancient monument in accordance with the Antiquities Law, is designated as a traditional building by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism upon request of the owner and is properly arranged to provide professional temporary accommodation and possibly food for the customers who come to it.

## Hotel licenses
6.-(1) The hotel operating license is issued by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism within two (2) months from the date of submission of the complete application or from the completion of the application with all the necessary forms provided in accordance with the specified types, conditions and procedure specified in the Regulations issued pursuant to this Law and upon payment of the specified right as determined by a decree issued by the Council of Ministers and published in the Official Gazette Newspaper of the Republic.
(2) For the purposes of securing an operating license from the Deputy Ministry of Tourism, the entrepreneur submits to the Deputy Ministry of Tourism an application in accordance with the form as may be specified in Regulations issued pursuant to this Law as well as certificates and other documents specified in Regulations issued pursuant to this Law.
(3) Subject to subsections (1) and (2), the operating license is valid for a period of three (3) years from the date of its issuance and is automatically renewed for each period of three (3) years, provided that the specified fee is paid to the Deputy Ministry of Tourism as determined by a decree issued by the Council of Ministers and which is published in the Official Gazette of the Republic and provided that the valid certificates provided for in paragraph (3) of Regulation 3 of the Regulation of the Establishment and Operation of Hotels and Tourist Accommodation (General) Regulations.
(4) The hotel operating license may be revoked by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism in case of-
(a) Where the operating license was granted on the basis of false or misleading information submitted by the entrepreneur;
(b) definitive cessation of business operations;
(c) that following an ex officio check by an Officer of the Deputy Ministry of Tourism, it is established that any of the certificates and documents necessary to secure an operating license, as defined in the relevant Regulations, have not been renewed;
(d) that the entrepreneur has been convicted of serious offenses in accordance with the Safety and Health at Work Law and the Regulations issued pursuant thereto, after prior consultation with the Director of the Labor Inspection Department, as well as for offenses relating to violations in health matters, as regulated by the relevant legislation for which the Ministry of Health is responsible for its implementation.
(e) death of the entrepreneur or by the issuance of a bankruptcy decree in the case of a natural person, or by the appointment of a liquidator in the case of a legal person;
(f) issuing a temporary decree or finalizing an issued temporary decree for the cessation of the operations of the hotel;
(g) following violations of the provisions of this Law or the Regulations issued pursuant to it or any other relevant legislation;
(h) that the entrepreneur has been convicted after the issuance of the operating license for any of the following offenses-
(i) premeditated murder, in violation of article 203 of the Criminal Code, or for any offense in violation of articles 144 to 177 of the Criminal Code;
(ii) robbery, in violation of articles 282 to 286 of the Criminal Code;
(iii) burglary, in violation of articles 291 to 296 of the Criminal Code;
(iv) forgery, in violation of article 333 of the Criminal Code
(v) theft, in violation of articles 255 to 272 of the Criminal Code;
(vi) conspiracy to commit murder, in violation of section 302 of the Penal Code;
(vii) circulation of a forged document, in violation of article 339 of the Criminal Code
(viii) securing goods or credits, in violation of articles 298 and 301, respectively, of the Criminal Code;
(ix) fraud, in violation of article 300 of the Criminal Code
(x) any offense in violation of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act;
(xi) any offense in violation of the Domestic Violence (Prevention and Protection of Victims) Act, as well as the Anti-Trafficking and Exploitation of Persons and Victim Protection Act.
(5) From the death of the entrepreneur or, in the case of a legal person, from the date of its dissolution or the appointment of a liquidator, and in the case of a natural person from the appointment of an administrator of the bankrupt's property, the issued operating license ceases to be valid and a new operating license may be issued by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism for the period that has not expired in the name of the executors of the will or administrators of the property of the deceased entrepreneur or liquidator or the administrator of the bankrupt's estate, as the case may be, without the payment of any right:
It is provided that the executors of the will or the administrators of the estate of the deceased businessman or the liquidator or the administrator of the bankrupt's estate, as the case may be, inform the Deputy Ministry of Tourism as soon as possible and no later than ten (10) days after the death of the businessman or, in the case of a legal entity, of the dissolution of the legal entity or of the appointment of a liquidator, and in the case of a natural person for the appointment of an administrator of the bankrupt's property.

## Hotel ranking
7.-(1) The final ranking of the hotels is carried out by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism within two (2) weeks from the issuance of the operating license:
Provided that, the preliminary classification may be given by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism on the basis of the Building Permit regarding the number of rooms and upon presentation of a signed certificate by the entrepreneur regarding the quality characteristics of the hotel, and is valid until the mandatory issuance of the final classification:
It is further provided that, after authorization by the Deputy Minister, the classification of the hotels may be carried out by an Accredited Classification Organization in accordance with the ISO 17065 Standard "Conformity Assessment – Requirements for bodies certifying products, processes and services" and in accordance with the additional requirements set by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism and the accreditation of the Classification Organizations is carried out by:
(a) The Cyprus Quality Promotion Organization, which has been designated as the national accreditation organization pursuant to the Accreditation, Standardization and Technical Information Law and Regulation (EC) no. 765/2008 or
(b) accreditation body of another member state or body that participates in the Multilateral Agreement (Multilateral Agreement-MLA) with the European Co-operation for Accreditation (EA) that meets the provisions of Regulation (EC) no. 765/2008.
(2) The hotel may operate after the issuance of the operating license and before the first classification is carried out, provided that it does not use the term "star" or any other term that gives the meaning of classification to the hotel pursuant to paragraphs (a), (b), (c), (d), (e) and (f) of article 4:
Provided that, in case of non-compliance, the hotel is subject to a fine not exceeding five thousand euros (€5,000).
(3) The Deputy Ministry of Tourism carries out an on-site inspection of the business and carries out an evaluation for classification purposes, based on the mandatory and optional criteria as defined in Regulations issued pursuant to this Law.
(4) The score of the criteria referred to in subsection (3) is determined by a decree issued by the Council of Ministers and published in the Official Gazette of the Republic.
(5)(a) The classification is renewed every three years according to the established procedure, in accordance with subsection (3) of article 6.
(b) The entrepreneur submits an application for renewal of the classification at least six (6) months before the expiry of the existing classification:
It is provided that, in the event that the entrepreneur has submitted the application for renewal of the classification within the deadline specified and the Deputy Ministry of Tourism delays making the relevant classification, the hotel maintains the existing classification until the completion of the renewal of the classification.
(6) [Deleted].
(7) Any subsequent classification may result in the reclassification of the hotel to the appropriate lower or higher class:
Provided that, in case of reclassification of the hotel to a higher class, the entrepreneur may choose to maintain his existing classification:
It is further provided that, in the event of reclassification of the hotel to a higher class, this shall take effect, provided that all mandatory criteria for such class are met in accordance with the criteria determined pursuant to subsection (3).
(8)(a) The Deputy Minister, for the purpose of classifying a hotel in a class, following a reasoned proposal from the manager or the entrepreneur, may approve relaxations of a limited area, as long as the hotels otherwise exceed the specified minimum conditions for classification in a class, as provided for in the provisions of article 4 and determined in Regulations issued pursuant to the provisions of this Law.
(b) The Deputy Minister appoints an Advisory Committee, which is composed of the Director General of the Deputy Ministry of Tourism as Chairman, the Director of Quality Assurance and Licensing of Tourism Businesses of the Deputy Ministry of Tourism and a representative of the Ministry of the Interior.
(c) The meetings of the Advisory Committee are attended by (2) representatives of hotelier associations, appointed by the Deputy Minister with observer status for a specific period of time, which is specified in their appointment document.
(d) The Chairman of the Advisory Committee convenes the meetings of the committee based on the agenda which is communicated to the members and observers.

## Distinguish between boutique hotels and suites
8.-(1) Every hotel may receive from the Deputy Ministry of Tourism the "boutique" distinction provided that-
(a) In the case of an urban hotel the number of rooms does not exceed one hundred (100) and in other hotels the number of rooms does not exceed eighty (80), and
(b) satisfies at least one (1) of the following criteria-
(i) has a particular theme or character of decoration and/or architecture throughout the business;
(ii) has a different themed decoration in each room;
(iii) specifies a specific decorative theme for each common room.
(2) Every hotel can receive from the Deputy Ministry of Tourism the distinction of "suites", provided that 80% of its rooms have an area of at least forty (40) square meters.

## Hotel name
9.-(1) Subject to the provisions of any other relevant legislation, each hotel has the same name as it appears in its operating license:
Provided that, when issuing an operating license by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism, the appropriateness of the name will be examined:
It is further provided that, during the examination of the operating license application, the Deputy Ministry of Tourism may not accept any name, which, in its opinion, is contrary to morals or public order:
Provided further that the use of foreign names is also permitted.
(2) The right of ownership and use of the name in the Republic belongs to the entrepreneur of the hotel business and in the event of its transfer, the right to the name is also transferred and in case of cessation of operation of the hotel business, the owner of the name retains the rights to this for a period of two years within which he may use the name again, or, in case of definitive cessation of its use as a hotel, he may use this name for another hotel of similar qualifications in the city, area or this area, after the approval of the Deputy Ministry of Tourism.
(3) Anyone who uses a hotel name in violation of the provisions of this article is guilty of an offense and in case of conviction, is subject to imprisonment not exceeding six (6) months or to a fine not exceeding five thousand euros (€5,000) and/or to both penalties together and in case of repetition of the violation, is subject to a fine not exceeding five hundred euros (€500.00) for each day the violation continues.

## Customer relations
10.-(1) Subject to the provisions of any other relevant legislation, the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it and which govern the relations between hotel businesses and their customers, prevail, in the event of a conflict, over the provisions of an agreement concluded between the hotel businesses and their customers.
(2) The competent authorities which receive customer complaints regarding hotel businesses, inform the Deputy Ministry of Tourism of any complaint made to them by a customer of any hotel business.
(3) Each hotel employs the required staff of various specialties to provide a satisfactory level of service to its customers and for the general normal operation of the hotel.

## Liability of hotels and their protection
11.-(1)(a) The entrepreneur is responsible for any damage, destruction or loss of property brought to the hotel by any customer staying in it and to whom a room has been allocated:
Provided that, for the purposes of this article "property" means the property of the customer located in the hotel during the period of the customer's stay in it.
(b) Liability is limited to the amount of fifteen thousand euros (€15,000):
It is understood that, in cases of MTA operation, the responsibilities of the hotel manager defined in this article extend to the MTA units, for as long as they are under the management of the hotel.
(2)(a) The entrepreneur's liability is unlimited in the event that he-
(i) Took custody of the property;
(ii) refused to receive property which he is required to receive for safekeeping;
Provided that, the businessman or the manager of the business is obliged to receive securities, money and valuable objects, but may refuse to receive any property that is dangerous or considering the size or level of the hotel is of excessive value.
(b) The entrepreneur or the manager of the business is entitled to demand that the item is in a locked or sealed box.
(3) The entrepreneur is not liable if the damage, destruction or loss is due to:
(a) To the customer himself, or to his visitor, companion or employee;
(b) in force majeure or hostilities;
(c) in the nature of the object.
(4) Regardless of the provisions of paragraph (b) of subsection (1), the entrepreneur's liability is unlimited if the damage, destruction or loss resulted from an intentional act, omission or negligence of himself or any person for whose actions he is responsible.
(5) With the exception of the cases in which the provisions of subsection (4) apply, the customer's claim for compensation is extinguished if, upon becoming aware of the damage, destruction or loss, he unreasonably delays in informing the entrepreneur or manager.
(6) Any unilateral notification that excludes or limits the entrepreneur's liability and which is given or made before the damage, destruction or loss, is void.
(7) The provisions of this article do not apply to vehicles, any property located in them or live animals.
(8) The entrepreneur or the manager of the business has the right to withhold the property brought by the customer to the hotel for the legal claims arising from the customer's stay at the hotel, including the related benefits to the customer resulting from his stay at the hotel, if this is deemed necessary:
Provided that, the entrepreneur or the manager of the business has the right to deliver a relevant notification within twenty-four (24) hours to the competent police authority and in said notification the detained items are briefly described.
(9) Unjustified retention of property brought by the customer to the hotel is the responsibility of the entrepreneur or manager, as the case may be.

## Tourist accommodation
12. Tourist accommodation is-
(a) Tourist camps (camping grounds and car campings), which are characterized as fenced land areas located outside a residential area, have methodically arranged spaces for parking cars, caravans or pitching tents, have a valid operating license in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it and meet the specifications as listed in the First Annex;
(b) organized apartments (hotel apartments), which are characterized as tourist accommodations that have a single building or are part of a single building or buildings that make up a single set within a single space, properly arranged that professionally offer temporary accommodation, and have a valid operating license in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it and meet the criteria as listed in the Fourth Appendix:
Provided that, the organized apartments that are part of a single unit consist of at least five (5) apartments:
It is further provided that, the existing registered tourist apartments which were registered in accordance with the Hotels and Tourist Accommodations (Tourist Apartments) Regulations of 1985 to 2005, with the entry into force of this Law, are integrated as they are, with the existing capacity in the organized apartments category and article 26 of the Law does not apply to them;
(c) tourist villages, which are characterized as facilities consisting of a set of organized apartments and/or tourist villas as defined in paragraph (b) and (e) of this subsection and meet the criteria listed in the Fourth Annex;
(d) the tourist villas, which are characterized as self-contained residences appropriately arranged that provide temporary residence by profession and for a fee, have a valid operating license in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it and meet the specifications as listed in the Second Appendix;
(e) traditional buildings, which are classified as accommodation and for which a Preservation Decree has been issued pursuant to article 38 of the Law on Town Planning and Spatial Planning, or are classified as traditional buildings by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism following the request of the owner, are properly arranged to provide professional temporary accommodation and possibly food to the customers who come to them, have a valid operating license in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it and meet the specifications listed in the Third Annex.

## Classification of tourist accommodation
15.-(1) The final classification of tourist accommodations is carried out by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism within two (2) weeks of the operating license:
Provided that, the preliminary classification may be given by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism on the basis of the Building Permit regarding the number of apartments and upon presentation of a signed certificate by the entrepreneur regarding the quality characteristics of the tourist accommodation, and is valid until the mandatory issuance of the final classification:
It is further provided that, after the authorization of the Deputy Minister, the classification of tourist accommodation can be carried out by an Accredited Classification Organization in accordance with the ISO 17065 Standard "Conformity Assessment – Requirements for bodies certifying products, processes and services" and the accreditation of the Classification Organizations is carried out by:
(a) The Cyprus Quality Promotion Organization, which has been designated as the national accreditation organization pursuant to the Accreditation, Standardization and Technical Information Law and Regulation (EC) no. 765/2008, or
(2) In the event of a delay in issuing the operating license by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism for reasons not related to safety and health issues beyond three (3) months from the date of the relevant application, the tourist accommodation may submit an application for classification.
(3) The Deputy Ministry of Tourism carries out an on-site inspection of the business and carries out an evaluation based on the criteria as defined in the First to Fourth Annexes, as the case may be.
(4) The management or updating or modification of the criteria referred to in subsection (3) is the competence of the Deputy Ministry of Tourism.
(5) The classification is renewed every three years according to the established procedure.
(6) In the case of finding, in the prescribed manner, the absence of the elements taken into account during the classification, a reclassification is made to the appropriate lower class or, in serious cases, it is not granted:
It is understood that, before making any reclassification or decision, the Deputy Ministry of Tourism sets a reasonable deadline to make up the identified deficiencies or violations:
(7) Any subsequent classification may result in the reclassification of the tourist accommodation to the appropriate lower or higher class:
Provided that, in case of reclassification of the tourist accommodation to a higher class, the entrepreneur may choose to maintain his existing classification.
It is further provided that, in the event of a reclassification of the tourist accommodation to a higher class, this shall take effect provided that all the mandatory criteria for that class are met in accordance with the criteria established pursuant to subsection (3).

## Self-catering accommodation
16A.-(1) Regardless of the provisions of this Law or any other law, self-catering accommodation may not be rented and/or leased unless the following conditions are met:
(a) The self-catering accommodation has the characteristics and specifications of a detached house and/or detached holiday furnished villa and/or detached apartment as set out in the Fifth Schedule;
(b) the self-catering accommodation is registered in the Register of Self-catering Accommodations, which is maintained by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism pursuant to the Fifth Schedule,

## Offenses and penalties
22. (1) Any person-
(a) Uses the terms "hotel", "mainly hotel", "cottage complex", "holiday camp", "tourist camp" (camping ground and car camping), "hotel apartments and service flats", "tourist villages", "tourist villa complex", "unstarred hotel", "organized flats without order", "tourist village without order", "self-catering accommodation" or other term that conveys the meaning of a hotel or tourist accommodation to describe a business unit that has not been licensed under sections 6, 13 and 16A; or
(b) maintains or operates a hotel, hotel unit or tourist accommodation without an issued operating license or self-catering accommodation without a registration license, or has its operating license or registration license revoked under sections 6, 13 and 16A,
is guilty of an offense and, on conviction, is liable to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one (1) year or to a fine not exceeding five thousand euros (€5,000) or to both, and if the offense continues after conviction, is guilty of a further offense and liable to a further fine not exceeding two hundred euros (€200) for each day continuation of the infringement.
(2) In addition to any other penalty provided by this article, the Court, upon conviction of any person for an offense under subsection (1) may order-
(a) The interruption of the operation of the hotel, hotel unit or tourist accommodation or self-catering accommodation in relation to which the offense was committed, within the period provided for in the order of the Court, but in no case exceeding two (2) months;
(b) the payment of the costs of the trial by the convicted person:
Provided that, this decree is issued in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law, the Courts Law and the Civil Procedure Procedural Regulations.
(3) In case of failure of any person to comply with a court order, issued in accordance with paragraph (a) of subsection (2), the District Police Director or his representative, executes the order and claims the payment of the costs, which are demonstrably incurred during its execution and these costs are considered a penalty, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law and their payment is imposed in accordance with the provisions of this Law:
Provided that, the execution of the decree consists in the sealing of the hotel, hotel unit or tourist accommodation, on behalf of the relevant police bodies, which is carried out with a procedure and means, determined by a decision of the Council of Ministers:
It is further provided that any violation of the seals on the part of the entrepreneur or the director or any person acting on the instructions of the entrepreneur or the director is punished, according to subsection (5).
(5) Any person who fails to comply with a court order issued pursuant to paragraph (a) of subsection (3) or violates any seals placed pursuant to subsection (4) commits an offense and, on conviction, is liable to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two (2) years or to a fine not exceeding five thousand euros (€5000) or in these two sentences.
(6) In addition to any other penalty provided by the Act and the Regulations made thereunder, the Court shall have power to order any person convicted of an offense to comply with the relevant provisions of the Act or the Regulations in respect of which the offense was committed.
(7)(a) The Court before which a charge brought against a person for an offense committed in violation of subsection (1) is tried, may, upon unilateral application, order the suspension of any work related to the maintenance or operation of a hotel or tourist accommodation until the final adjudication of the case in respect of which the charge was brought:
Provided that, this decree is issued in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law, the Court Laws and the Civil Procedure Procedural Regulations.
(b) If any person, against whom a decree has been issued under the provisions of paragraph (a), refuses or fails to comply with it, within the time specified, the competent authority is authorized to execute the decree and the costs for its execution shall be paid to the competent authority by the person against whom the decree was issued, such costs being counted as a penalty in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law and the payment thereof is imposed, in accordance with the provisions of the said Law:
It is understood that the execution of the decree consists in the sealing of the hotel, hotel unit or tourist accommodation or self-catering accommodation on behalf of the relevant police bodies, which is carried out by a procedure and means, determined by a decision of the Council of Ministers.
(8) When an offense under this Law, which has been committed by a legal person, is shown to have been committed with the consent or tolerance or to be due to the negligence of any managing director, director, secretary or other similar officer of the legal person or any person who appeared to be acting in any such capacity, that person, as well as the legal person, is guilty of the said offense and, in case of conviction, is subject to the penalties provided by this Law for the specific offense.
(9) Anyone who violates or fails to comply with any provision of this Law in which no special provision is made in this regard, is guilty of an offense and upon conviction is subject to a prison sentence not exceeding one (1) year or a fine not exceeding five thousand euros (€5,000) or to both of these penalties.

## Administrative fine
23.-(1) In case of violation of the provisions of this Law or of the Regulations issued pursuant to it by any person, the Deputy Ministry of Tourism may impose on the violator an administrative fine of two thousand euros (€2,000) and, in case of repetition or continuation of the violation, an administrative fine not exceeding four thousand euros (€4,000), without affecting any other provisions of this Law.
(2) Without prejudice to subsection (1), in the event that the Deputy Ministry of Tourism finds a violation of this Law and/or the Regulations issued pursuant to it, it may impose administrative fines on-
(a) Legal entities; and
(b) a director, manager or officer or secretary of the legal entities, in the event that it is established that the violation was due to their own fault, omission or negligence.
(3) The Deputy Ministry of Tourism may publicly announce any measures or sanctions imposed in case of violation of the provisions of this Law and/or the Regulations issued pursuant thereto, unless such announcement may cause disproportionate damage to the parties involved.
(4) The Deputy Ministry of Tourism imposes an administrative fine pursuant to the provisions of subsection (1) with a written and reasoned decision which it forwards to the affected person, and-
(a) Which defines the offense; and
(b) by which it informs the affected person-
(i) on his right to challenge the decision by appealing to the Administrative Court in accordance with the provisions of Article 146 of the Constitution and the Law on the Establishment and Operation of the Administrative Court, and
(ii) on the deadlines within which the aforementioned rights can be exercised, and
(c) which becomes enforceable upon its said transfer.
(5) In case of failure to pay an administrative fine imposed by the Deputy Ministry, judicial measures are taken for collection in accordance with subsection (7).
(6) The administrative fines imposed by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall be counted against the revenues of the Permanent Fund of the Republic.
(7) In case of failure to pay an administrative fine or a monetary payment determined in the context of a compromise, the Deputy Ministry of Tourism may take legal measures to collect it and the amount due is collected as a civil debt in the Republic.
(8) Before issuing its decision on the possible imposition of an administrative fine, the Deputy Ministry must grant the right to be heard to each affected person and point out the rights granted to them pursuant to subsection (9).
(9) A person who is notified based on subsection (8) has the right, within a deadline set by the Deputy Ministry of Tourism and which may be between three (3) and twenty-one (21) days from said notification, to make written representations to the Deputy Ministry of Tourism.
(10) The Deputy Ministry of Tourism must take into account these representations before issuing a decision on the existence or non-existence of a violation, imposing an administrative fine and determining its amount.
(11) The Deputy Ministry of Tourism may invite a person or accept their request for oral presentations, in case they are required, to explain the written presentations that have already been submitted.
(12) The Deputy Ministry of Tourism may grant an extension of the deadline set pursuant to subsection (9) in the event of an obstacle or other reasonable cause.
(13) In case of non-submission of any representations within the set deadline, the Deputy Ministry of Tourism may proceed to take a decision without further notification.

## Hierarchical appeal
24.-(1) Any person who is not satisfied with a decision of the Deputy Ministry of Tourism issued pursuant to the provisions of this Law, may file an appeal before the Minister within twenty (20) calendar days from the notification to him of the relevant decision, in which the reasons in support of it are set forth.
(2) The Minister immediately examines any appeal made to him, decides on each appeal and communicates his decision to the appellant no later than within twenty (20) calendar days of the filing of the appeal:
Provided that, if, in any specific case, the Minister considers this necessary or appropriate, he may, at his discretion, hear or otherwise give the appellant the opportunity to support the reasons on which the appeal is based:
It is further provided that, the Minister may assign an official or a committee of officials of the Ministry of Finance to examine certain issues presented in the appeal and submit to him the conclusion of this examination before the Minister issues his decision on the appeal.
