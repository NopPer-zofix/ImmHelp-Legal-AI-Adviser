## The Roads and Buildings Regulation Law (CHAP. 96)

## Short title
1. This Law will be referred to as the Roads and Buildings Regulation Law.

*(CHAPTER 96)*

## Interpretation
2. In this Law-
“agro-voltaic systems” means the simultaneous use of land areas for both semi-transparent photovoltaic panels and agriculture, including pole solar arrays over crops and/or greenhouse solar arrays;
"licensed credit institution" has the meaning assigned to this term by article 2 of the Credit Institutions Operations Law;
"restructuring" has the meaning assigned to this term by article 2 of the Cadastre and Spatial Department (Fees and Rights) Law;
"competent authority" means the Development Licensing Director of each Provincial Self-Government Organization, as defined in article 2 of the Provincial Self-Government Organizations Law:
Provided that, for the issuance of a permit, which is required in the framework of the licensing process for a strategic development project, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Facilitation of Strategic Development Projects, the competent authority is considered to be the designated and/or those designated as such authorities, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Facilitation of Strategic Development Projects:
It is further provided that, for the issuance of a license, which is required in the context of the licensing process, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Operation and Control of Casinos, the competent authority is considered to be the designated authority, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Operation and Control of Casinos;
"safety in use and accessibility" means the design and construction of construction works, so as to prevent risks of accidents or damage during their use and/or operation and to make their accessibility safe for all persons, including persons with disabilities and persons with reduced mobility;
"disabled persons" means persons who have long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairments which, in their interaction with various barriers, may prevent the full and effective participation of such persons in society on an equal basis with others;
"people with reduced mobility" means people whose mobility is reduced, due to any permanent or temporary physical, mental, motor or sensory disability, as a result of which they face permanent or temporary difficulty in accessing and using a building or road;
"Municipality" has the meaning assigned to the term "municipality" in article 2 of the Provincial Self-Governing Organizations Law;
"approved use" means the use of a building as approved by permit and as shown on the approved plans:
It is understood that the use of a building on the effective date of this Law will be considered as the approved use if it does not conflict with the building permit that was issued and the approved plans.
"virtual net-billing" means the method according to which the cost of electricity produced/exported to the distribution system of a photovoltaic system is offset against the cost of electricity imported from the distribution system of one or more premises located in a different location from the photovoltaic system;
"virtual net-metering" means the method according to which the difference between the electricity imported from the distribution system to cover the needs of a building and the electricity exported to the distribution system from a photovoltaic system installed in a different space from the building is calculated;
"building energy performance" means the amount of energy actually consumed or estimated to satisfy the various needs associated with the normal use of the building, which may include, among others, heating, domestic water heating, cooling, ventilation and lighting and which is expressed by one or more numerical indicators, which have been calculated taking into account the thermal insulation, the technical characteristics and the characteristics of the electromechanical installations, the design and the location of the building in relation to climatic factors, the exposure of the building to the sun and the effect of neighboring structures, the energy production from the building itself as well as other factors that affect energy demand, including the climatic conditions inside the building.
“superintending engineer” means a person who—
(i) is registered in the register of members of the Scientific and Technical Chamber of Cyprus (ETEK) and holds a license to practice a profession, pursuant to the Law on the Scientific and Technical Chamber of Cyprus, who is entitled to undertake work appropriate to an architect or a civil engineer for the specific project and who is the designer of the specific project based on the corresponding definition given in this article
(ii) has been properly appointed and given oversight of the project and
(iii) is registered in the Register of Surveyors and Supervising Engineers which is maintained in accordance with the provisions of the Cyprus Scientific and Technical Chamber Law;
[S.S.: Sub-paragraph (iii) of the term “supervising engineer” comes into force from the date determined by the Minister by notification, which is published in the Official Gazette of the Republic].
"project supervision" means the carrying out of site inspections as deemed necessary by the supervising engineer, in accordance with accepted technical procedures and technical ethics, subject to any duties or requirements imposed on him by or under the provisions of Article 9A
"project" means any work or any other matter involving construction work, for the execution or implementation of which prior authorization is required pursuant to Article 3
"credit acquisition company" has the meaning assigned to this term by article 2 of the Credit Facility Trading and Related Matters Law;
"damage" includes causing bodily harm, illness and/or death;
"study" includes the preparation or drawing up of blueprints, technical specifications and calculations related to the intended execution or implementation of a project, as well as any other work required for the purposes of examining an application for the issuance of a permit pursuant to article 3
“researcher” means a person who—
(i) is registered in the register of members of ETEK and holds a license to practice a profession, pursuant to the Cyprus Scientific and Technical Chamber Law, who is entitled to undertake work appropriate to an architect or a civil engineer for the specific project and to be a researcher of the specific project and who is the supervising engineer of the specific project based on the definition given in this article
(ii) has been duly authorized and undertaken the preparation of a project study suitable for an architect or a civil engineer. and
[S.S.: Sub-paragraph (iii) of the term "researcher" comes into force from the date determined by the Minister by notification, which is published in the Official Gazette of the Republic].
“alteration”, “addition” or “repair”, when used in reference to buildings, means any structural alteration, addition or repair by which any dimension of such building is altered, but does not include:
(a) the replacement of tiles, clay or other material for the purpose of making any roof watertight
(b) the repair of any existing door the leaves of which do not open or project into a street
(c) the repair of any balcony or terrace window which does not open or project onto a street
(d) the whitewash, water-colouring, dusting or painting of any wall, ceiling, wooden or iron structure in any building
(e) the re-laying, re-laying of boards or tiles of any floor or floor contained within the external walls of any building or within any existing porch or verandah attached to the building, but which does not open or project into a street.
"rescue and fire-fighting team" means a public, voluntary or private service which undertakes the rescue of persons and the fighting of fires;
“street” includes any street, animal crossing path, footpath, cul-de-sac, thoroughfare, footpath, pavement or public square;
"building" means any structure, whether of stone, concrete, clay, iron, wood or other material, and includes any pit and any foundation, wall, roof, chimney, porch, porch, crown or projection or part of a building, or any thing attached thereto, or any wall, embankment, fence, enclosure or other structure which encloses or bounds or is intended to enclose or delimit any land or space.
"property" has the meaning assigned to this term by the Criminal Code Law
"person" means any natural or legal person
"fire protection" means the overall measures taken to eliminate or reduce to the minimum degree the risks arising from the occurrence of fire and refers to all building facilities, including construction products, materials, equipment and systems that contribute to the timely and effective response created by the occurrence of fire;
"parapet" means a protective structure that is erected or installed permanently on terraces and balconies, as well as on the roof (roof) of a building, in the event that a staircase leads to it, and includes a wall or any other protective structure.
“completion of the work” means the removal in any way of the machinery and workmen, and the non-performance of any work on the property on which the building is erected or the work is carried out for a continuous period of four months;
"distribution system" has the meaning assigned to this term by the Electricity Market Regulation Law;
"local authority" has the meaning assigned to that term in article 2 of the Provincial Self-Governing Organizations Law;
"Minister" means the Minister for the Interior.

## Marking etc. of roads and construction etc. of buildings is prohibited without permission of the competent authority
3.-(1) No person may-
(a) to open or construct a road
(b) erect or permit or permit to be erected any building or demolish or rebuild or make any alteration, addition or repair to any existing building or permit or permit to be made any such demolition or rebuilding or any such alteration, addition or repair
(c) open up or divide any land (whether or not any other buildings or structures used exclusively for agriculture or forestry are thereon) into divided plots
(d) divide any building (whether or not any such division necessitates any construction) into separate apartments
(e) convert or permit or tolerate the conversion of the approved use of a building.
(f) to commence any of the tasks or matters set out above,
without a permit therefor, which is previously obtained from the competent authority as specified in subsection (2) or, where the permit is issued under the second proviso to subsection (2) of section 14, by the Director of the Department of Town Planning and Housing:
Provided that, a permit pursuant to this article may concern exclusively a specific part of a building or any appendices to it and this permit will not be a permit for other parts of the building for which a permit has not been issued after the examination of a relevant application:
It is further understood that photovoltaic systems installed on the shell of an existing building which belongs to one owner or two owners who are spouses or cohabitants, in the sense given to the term in question by the Law on Civil Cohabitation, are not considered, for the purposes of this Law, annexes to a building and their installation does not require the issuance of a permit by the competent authority pursuant to the provisions of this article, provided that the building in question has not been declared as a preserved or ancient monument and does not fall under a controlled area based on the Antiquities Law or an archaeological site protection zone or a special airport zone:
It is further provided that, in the cases of existing buildings which belong to more than one owner, excluding the case provided for in the immediately above reservation which concerns spouses or cohabitants, for the installation of photovoltaic systems on their shell, the provisions of paragraph (b) of subsection (2) of article 38 of the Promotion and Encouragement of the Use of Renewable Energy Sources Law shall apply:
It is further provided that, in the cases where it is required pursuant to the provisions of this article, the issuance of a permit by the competent authority for the installation of photovoltaic systems for self-consumption purposes, said permit shall be deemed to have been issued, provided that the conditions specified in a decree of the Minister issued pursuant to article 4B are met cumulatively, as well as the following conditions:
(a) Photovoltaic systems, within the same piece of land or adjacent to this or another piece of land connected to the distribution system by any method, have a maximum total power of 100kW, regardless of their coverage area.
(b) the photovoltaic systems have been installed-
(i) on the roof or surface of an agricultural warehouse, tank, greenhouse or other premises, whether agricultural or livestock. and/or
(ii) on land within the same parcel of land where the serviced warehouse, tank, greenhouse or premises is located. and/or
(iii) on the roof of an immovable or on the ground within an adjacent parcel thereof or within a parcel of land other than the one where the serviced warehouse, tank, greenhouse or immovable is located, connected to the distribution system by any method, such as by the method of virtual net-billing or virtual net-metering,
provided that in case the photovoltaic systems are installed on the ground, they may not exceed a height of 150 centimeters from the contiguous ground, with the exception of the installation of agro-voltaic systems which may exceed the mentioned height. and
(c) the owner or occupier of the piece of land on which the agricultural warehouse, tank, greenhouse or building referred to in paragraph (b) is located is a person who is registered in the Register of Farmers and Agricultural Holdings and carries out agricultural activity, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Creation of the Register of Farmers and Agricultural Holdings or an irrigation commission, as defined in the provisions of of Irrigation Departments (Villages) Law or irrigation association, as defined in the provisions of the Irrigation Associations (Private Water) Law or Council, as defined in the provisions of the Communities Law:
It is further understood that the provisions of the above reservation do not apply, in a case where a building has been illegally erected on the plot of land referred to in paragraph (b), which is used exclusively as a residence:
It is further provided that, in cases where it is required, pursuant to the provisions of this article, to issue a license from the competent authority for the installation of photovoltaic systems for self-consumption purposes, said license shall be deemed to have been issued, in a case where-
(a) the said systems are installed on the ground of a piece of land or on the roof of a building.
(b) [Deleted]
(c) the conditions specified in a decree of the Minister issued pursuant to the provisions of article 4B are met, which may not determine the maximum area of the systems in question:
It is further provided that, the permit required to be issued pursuant to the provisions of this article by the competent authority shall be deemed to have been issued-
(a) for the installation and/or operation of an electrical energy storage facility in combination with a power generation station from renewable energy sources for which the licenses required under the relevant legislation have already been secured.
(b) for the installation of a data center, as this term is attributed in article 2 of the Law on Energy Efficiency, at the electricity generating station referred to in paragraph (a). and
(c) for the installation of photovoltaic systems for self-consumption purposes and/or for commercial purposes on the roofs of legal carports of public or private car parks, which are licensed for exclusive use as car parks and/or are licensed to serve legal development;
provided that the conditions specified in a decree of the Minister which is issued pursuant to the provisions of article 4B, which cannot determine the maximum area of the installation in question, are met.
(1A) For a building that consists of more than one unit, which can operate independently, at the same time as the building permit, a permit is issued for the division of the building into the individual units, which, where applicable, includes each common area and each common area with exclusive rights:
It is provided that, in the case of such a building, the application for the issuance of a building and division permit shall specify precisely the individual independent units, the common areas with exclusive rights of use for each unit, the common areas and the remaining, unused development rights:
It is further provided that, for a building erected before the date of entry into force of the Regulation of Roads and Buildings (Amendment) Law of 2011, the competent authority, following an application for a subdivision permit to define the individual self-contained units, which is submitted by the permit holder or by any person with an interest in the building or subdivision or ex officio, after relevant information from the Director of the Department of Land Registry and Spatial Survey, issues a permit to divide the building:
It is further provided that, in the event of a subdivision permit being issued following an application submitted by any person having an interest in the building or the subdivision or ex officio, the competent authority may require the payment of the relevant fees from said person; in the event of refusal to pay the fees, the competent authority shall appropriately inform the Director of the Department of Cadastre and Land Surveying, so that the due fees are paid before the transfer and registration of a unit to the owner or to a person who has an interest in the building.
(1B) Irrespective of the provisions of this Law or the Regulations issued pursuant to it, in the case of co-ownership, an application for the granting of a license for the division of immovable property shall be submitted and signed by any of the co-owners, provided that it is in accordance with a decision of the Director of the Department of Land and Surveying pursuant to the provisions of subsection (9) of article 29 of the Immovable Property (Possession, Registration and Assessment) of Law.
(1C) In the case of projects involving the opening or construction of a road or the opening or division of land, following the issuance of a permit pursuant to the provisions of this article, the competent authority may entrust the monitoring of the implementation of the road network of such projects to the local authority of the area within which the project falls and, in the event that the local authority accepts the assignment, the local authority is considered to be acting on behalf of the competent authority and to have authority, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to this, to exercise general supervision, make suggestions and submit demands to the project owner and/or the supervising engineer, regarding the correction of construction errors or failures and/or the conduct of material quality controls and/or the presentation of documents, attestations and certificates, in order to ensure compliance with the conditions of the license and the technical specifications for the implementation of the road network of the project:
It is understood that the monitoring of the implementation of the project by the competent authority or the local authority does not exempt the supervising engineer from his responsibilities and obligations, as defined in the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant thereto.
(2) The Development Licensing Director of the respective Provincial Self-Government Organization or any other person authorized by him has presumptive authority to issue any permit, notification or other act or document as the competent authority for such authority under the provisions of this Law and the Minister coordinates the competent authorities, for the uniform application of the provisions of this Law:
Provided that, the Director of Development Licensing of each Provincial Self-Government Organization may delegate the powers he has as a competent authority, as defined in this Law, to the Head of the Building Licensing Sector of each Provincial Self-Government Organization, in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Provincial Self-Government Organizations, in the following cases:
(a) For residential development of up to two (2) residential units and/or up to four (4) residential units and/or up to twelve (12) townhouses and/or up to twenty (20) apartments in an apartment building, as well as for additions and/or conversions to said developments;
(b) for commercial and/or office and/or mixed residential with commercial and/or office development, with a total usable area of up to one thousand (1000) square meters.

## Application form and procedure, etc.
3A(1)(a) The Minister may, after consultation with the competent authorities, as, by decree, determine -
(i) the type and manner of submitting an application for the issuance of a permit for any road or building under this Law,
(ii) the procedure for submitting and examining an application for the issuance of a license, including the time frame for completing the examination.
(b) In order to achieve the purposes of this Law, the Minister may, if he deems this to be necessary or desirable, issue instructions to the competent authorities to coordinate the handling of issues regulated under paragraph (a).

## Special provision regarding application for permission in special cases
3B. (1) Regardless of the provisions of this Law or the Regulations issued pursuant thereto, an application for the granting of any license that the competent authority has the authority to issue pursuant to the provisions of this Law, when-
(a) the town planning permit has been issued pursuant to article 90 of the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Law, is signed by the persons who, in accordance with the provisions of the above article, are entitled to sign the application for town planning permit; and
(b) prior issuance of a planning permit pursuant to this Law or the Regulations issued pursuant thereto is not required, signed by the following persons:
(i) In the case of co-ownership, by one or more co-owners, as long as the application concerns a part of real estate that is evident from the object that it corresponds to the share of the applicant by virtue of a planning permit or other permit issued by any competent authority or that this clearly results from a distribution agreement signed by all co-owners;
(ii) in case the application concerns the exercise of a registered development right, by the registered owner or the beneficiary of the unit for the benefit of which the right is registered, without the production of the consent or approval of the co-owners in the jointly owned building; and
(iii) in case the application relates to the development of a property with a share in a jointly owned section, it is signed only by the owner of the property under development.
(2) In case the provisions of paragraph (b) of subsection (1) are applied, the following procedure is followed:
(a) The application is notified to the remaining co-owners and, if for any reason the notification is not possible, a relevant notice is published in at least two (2) daily newspapers according to the format determined from time to time by the Minister;
(b) in the cases of sub-paragraphs (i) and (iii) of paragraph (b) of subsection (1), a co-owner who considers that his amenities or legal ownership interests are affected, as well as the comfortable, legal and reasonable fruition of his property may, within thirty (30) days from the date on which he becomes aware of the content of the application, submit an objection to the competent authority; and
(c) the competent authority may, before granting permission on an application made under paragraph (b) of subsection (1), summon the objecting co-owners to hear them.

## Conditions for issuing a license pursuant to article 3
4.-(1) No permit shall be issued in accordance with article 3, unless the competent authority is satisfied that it concerns work, for which an application is submitted and which is in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant thereto and the content of a planning permit granted on the basis of the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Law of 1972, as amended or replaced from time to time.
It is understood that the control exercised by the competent authority based on this Law, is limited only to matters that fall under the provisions of this Law and does not extend to matters that fall under the provisions of other specialized legislation.
(2) Regardless of the provisions of the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Law of 1972 to 2011 or any General or Special Development Decree issued pursuant to said Laws, the competent authority may issue a permit in cases of applications for residential development that are not in accordance with a granted planning permit or in cases provided for in a General or Special Development Decree issued with based on the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Law, as amended or replaced from time to time, as regards –
(a) the internal organization of each self-contained unit provided for in a license granted;
(b) the modification of the layout of the parking spaces provided for in a planning permit, provided that they operate adequately,
(c) the placement of openings on building faces,
(d) the floor height of the building and the total height of the building, when the number of floors does not exceed the permitted one,
(e) the height of a building wall,
(f) any other matter specified by decree of the Minister, published in the Official Gazette of the Republic, on terms and subject to the conditions specified therein, provided that the amenities of adjacent properties or properties in the area of the building are not materially affected.

## Buildings on parcels of land from division or distribution
4A. In compliance with the provisions of this Law and the Regulations in force issued pursuant thereto, a permit may be granted pursuant to Article 3 of this Law, for the construction of a building on a plot of land resulting from the division or distribution of land made pursuant to the provisions of the Real Estate (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law or any law amending or replacing it, only in the following cases:
(a) For the construction of a building on such a piece of land located within a water supply area, as defined in paragraph (c) of subsection (3) of article 9 of this Law, or within a tourist zone or within a zone for special industries or professions, as this zone wanted to be determined by the Competent Authority with the approval of the Council of Ministers, pursuant to the provisions of article 14 of this Law:
It is understood that, when granting a license pursuant to the provisions of this paragraph, the Competent Authority has the power, in compliance with the Regulations in force at any given time, to impose conditions set forth in the license, both by virtue of paragraph (b) of subsection (1) of article 9 of this Law, and by virtue of paragraph (c) of the same subsection, as if it were a permit for the opening or division of land for building purposes.
(b) For the construction of up to two houses on such a piece of land that is outside the water supply area, as determined above with a permit from the Competent Authority, which is granted if the following conditions are met:
(i) the residence to which the application refers is intended to be used for the permanent or temporary accommodation of the applicant himself or his children. For the purposes of this subsection, the term "applicant" includes any legal entity whose sole shareholders are any of the following, i.e. the husband or wife or their children and the residence to which the application refers is intended for the permanent or temporary housing of any of them0 and
(ii) such piece of land adjoins a public road which is registered in the books of the Provincial Land Registry and which satisfies the Competent Authority or
(iii) such plot of land is accessed by a right of way approximately 300 meters long and not less than 3.65 meters wide, provided that the dwelling to which the application relates shall be located at a distance of at least 7.60 meters from the limits of such passage:
It is understood that the Competent Authority may allow the construction of a second residence on a piece of land that has as access a right of way less than 12 feet wide but not less than 3 meters if on the date of commencement of the Regulation of Streets and Buildings (Amendment) Law of 1987 there was another residence on such piece of land.
(c) For the construction of a building for use for agricultural or livestock purposes.

## Decree exempting from the obligation to secure a permit
4B.-(1) The Minister may, by a reasoned decree, which is published in the Official Gazette of the Republic, exempt any categories of constructions that fall under the term "building" and are defined in the decree, from the obligation to secure any license under this Law.
(2) An order under subsection (1) may be made—
(a) as a “General Exemption Ordinance”, which applies generally to every building, under the conditions that may be specified in the ordinance, or
(b) as a "Special Exemption Ordinance", which applies specifically only to any building or immovable property located in an area specified in the ordinance or in a category of buildings specified in the ordinance.
(3) A building, which has been exempted by virtue of subsections (1) and (2) from the obligation to secure any license under this Law, is considered a licensed building, for the purposes of any law or regulation that requires, as a condition of its application, the existence of a license under this Law.
(4) For the purposes of this article, construction categories are considered to be-
(a) buildings, with an area of less than twenty (20) square meters,
(b) auxiliary buildings, with an area of less than twenty (20) square meters,
(c) buildings, with an area of less than fifty (50) square meters, related to agricultural and livestock operations,
(d) construction and/or reconstruction of retaining walls in a traditional way of building in the natural environment,
(e) attachments to buildings, such as water tanks on the roof of the building, television aerials, air conditioners on the outside of the building, radio communication stations placed on legally erected masts and other attachments to buildings of a similar nature;
(f) additions and alterations, small scale, which do not affect the safety of the buildings, do not increase the permitted buildable area, do not alter the external aesthetics of the buildings and do not materially affect the amenity of the adjacent properties or parts of the property:
Provided that, small-scale additions and conversions are conversions that do not exceed ten percent (10%) of the total existing built area of the building and do not exceed fifty (50) square meters:
It is further understood that conversions concerning energy upgrading of the building are considered as small-scale conversions, as long as they do not exceed the permitted buildable area of the property and do not involve large-scale renovation, as defined in the Roads and Buildings (Energy Performance of Buildings) Regulations of 2006 and 2014.

## Exemption from the obligation to obtain a permit
4C. Irrespective of the provisions of this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant thereto, structures with an area not exceeding ten (10) square meters, which are erected on a privately-owned plot in rural areas, excluding zones and protection areas, are considered as licensed buildings, under the terms and conditions specified in a decree of the Minister, as licensed buildings, for the purposes of security tools or domestic animals and/or poultry.

## License duration and renewal
5.-(1) The permit, issued in accordance with the provisions of this Law, is valid for a period of three years from the date of its issuance:
Provided that, if a planning permit has been granted for the same works upon application, in accordance with the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Law, the competent authority may in appropriate cases, including a change in the town planning status of the application area, define the period of validity of the permit issued on the basis of this Law, so that it is valid only for as long as the corresponding town planning permit is valid.
(2) It is allowed to renew the license at the request of its owner, under the following conditions:
(a) If a planning permit has been issued for the same works upon application, in accordance with the provisions of article 23 of the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Laws, the permit is renewed for as long as the relevant planning permit is renewed and valid, in accordance with the provisions of article 28 of the same law:
(b) in the remaining cases the permit is renewed if the works to which the permit refers have been started but not completed, are substantial and are under active execution at the expiry of the permit.
(3) The renewal of the license is allowed, with the exception of the provisions of paragraph (b) of the previous subsection, for as long as is justified by the circumstances, in the following cases:
(a) In the case of a permit issued pursuant to the provisions of paragraph (a) or (c) of subsection (1) of article 3 of this Law, if the work of opening or constructing a road or dividing the land into plots did not begin during the initial validity of the permit due to the untimely demarcation by the Department of Cadastre and Geometry, as long as the relevant application for demarcation was submitted to the competent department within a period of three months from the date of of issuing the license and has not withdrawn since0 and
(b) for any permit, if the works referred to in the permit did not begin during the initial validity of the permit, he/she acknowledges that proceedings are pending before the Rent Control Court for the recovery of possession of the property to which the permit pertains, according to the provisions of the currently applicable Tenement Law, provided that this proceeding began either before the issuance of the permit or during the initial validity of the permit.
(4) The renewal is valid for a period of two years from the date of expiry of the initial validity of the licence. Further renewals of the validity of the license for a period of one year are also allowed, as long as the conditions for the initial renewal, specified in subsections (2) and (3) of this article, are met.
(5) When renewing the permit, the competent authority has the power to amend the conditions under which the permit was originally issued or to impose new conditions or to reject the application for renewal, provided that this authority derives from a regulatory provision or an administrative notice that is valid at the time of the renewal, and the amended or new conditions or the rejection relate to the unexecuted part of the work to which the permit refers and do not materially affect the already executed.
(6) With regards to a license issued before the entry into force of this Law, the following shall apply:
(a) In the event that three years have not elapsed from the date of issue of the permit, its validity shall be extended, subject to subsection (1) of article 5, for a period of three years from the date of issue
(b) in the event that three years have passed since the date of issuance of the license, it is permitted under the conditions of subsections (2) and (3), the renewal of the license for a period of one year, from the date of renewal, provided that the holder thereof shall submit an application to this effect within a period of three months from the date of entry into force of this Law.
It is allowed to renew the license in accordance with what is defined in subsection (4) of this article
(c) the provisions of subsection (5) apply in all respects in this case as well.

*(CHAPTER 96)*

## Special arrangements in relation to the imposition of an administrative fine
28.-(1) An administrative fine is imposed by reasoned decision of the competent authority, which confirms the violation, after hearing or giving the opportunity to the person concerned or his representative to be heard orally or in writing, within a period of fifteen (15) working days from the date of issuance of the decision to impose the administrative fine.
(2) The competent authority imposes an administrative fine under the provisions of articles 21, 22 and 23 by means of a written and reasoned decision, which is sent to the affected person and which –
(a) Defines the offence;
(b) informs the affected person of his right to challenge the decision with a hierarchical appeal to the Minister, in accordance with the provisions of article 18, and
(c) becomes enforceable upon such transfer thereof.
(3) The competent authority may, if it deems it appropriate, take into account any commitment provided to it by the offender or on behalf of the offender in relation to the offense occurring and the prospect of its removal or restoration and, in any case, the competent authority shall ensure that no administrative fine and penalty are imposed as a result of a conviction for committing a criminal offense at the same time.
(4) The competent authority collects the administrative fine which is imposed by virtue of the provisions of articles 21, 22 and 23 when the deadlines provided for in subsection (1) of article 18 for filing an appeal have expired or, in the case of dissatisfaction with the Minister's decision, in accordance with subsection (2) of article 18, when the seventy-five deadline has expired (75) days to file an appeal before the Supreme Court in accordance with the provisions of subsection (3) of article 18.
(5) In the event of non-payment of an administrative fine imposed by the competent authority pursuant to this Law, the competent authority shall take judicial measures and the amount due shall be collected as a civil debt owed to the Republic.
(6) The Minister determines with his instructions the indicative criteria for calculating the amount of an administrative fine imposed pursuant to articles 21, 22 and 23, without this limiting the discretion of the competent authority to freely decide on its amount based on the facts of each case.

*(19(I)/2016)*

## Civil liability
29.-(1) Violation of an obligation imposed by this Law and the Regulations issued pursuant to it, constitutes a civil offense pursuant to the provisions of the Civil Offenses Law, against any person who may suffer damage from the violation of this obligation.
(2) Violation of any obligation under this Law by any person creates, without prejudice to the legal principles applicable to actions for breach of official duties, a conductive right for the benefit of the injured person.
(3) The liability pursuant to subsection (2) may not be limited or excluded by any contractual term, by any notice or by any other provision.
