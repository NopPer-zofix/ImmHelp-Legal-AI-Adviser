## The Town Planning and Zoning Law of 1972 (90/1972)

## Short title
1. The Town Planning and Zoning Laws of 1972 to (No. 2) of 1999 shall be referred to together as the Town Planning and Zoning Laws of 1972 to (No. 2) of 1999.

*(90/1972)*

## Interpretation
2.-(1) In this Law, unless a different meaning arises from the text-
"application for determination" has the meaning given to this term under subsection (2) of article 44
"real property" has the meaning given to this term under article 2 of the Real Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law
"development" acquires the meaning attributed to this term under article 20, "development" is not interpreted accordingly
"erection", in relation to a building, includes extension, conversion and re-erection
"open space" means any immovable property which has been left as a public garden, or is used for the purposes of public recreation, or land which was a cemetery which has ceased to be used;
"value", in relation to immovable property, means the amount it would be expected to fetch if sold in a public market under a willing seller
"responsibilities" includes powers and duties
"ancient monument" acquires the meaning given to this term under the Antiquities Law
"authority to which competences have been transferred" means any person, body or principle exercising the competence of the Minister transferred to this person or body or this principle by virtue of this Law
"General Development Ordinance" means an Ordinance as referred to in paragraph (a) of subsection (2) of article 22
"agriculture" includes horticulture, fruit growing, seed growing, dairying, animal husbandry (including the rearing of any animal for the production of food, hide, skins or furs or for the purpose of its use in the cultivation of the land), the use of land for grazing, basket making or as pasture, vegetable garden and nursery and the use of land as a forest whenever this use is ancillary to the cultivation of the land for other agricultural purposes, "agricultural" is not interpreted accordingly
"Policy Statement" has the meaning given to this term under Article 18A
"Municipality" acquires the meaning given to this term in article 2 of the Law on Regulation of Roads and Buildings, excluding "rural municipalities" in the sense of subsection (3) of article 3 of the same Law
"dispose" means a disposition by sale, exchange or lease or by the creation of any easement, right or privilege or in any other way than by dedication, gift or mortgage, "dispose" shall not be construed accordingly
"Development Ordinance" shall have the meaning given to this term under Article 22
"Detention Order" has the meaning given to this term under Article 38
"Tree Protection Ordinance" has the meaning given to this term under Article 39
"advertisement" means any word, letter, effigy, sign, mural, panel, notice, emblem or display, whether or not illuminated, which is in the nature of advertising, announcement or guidance and is used in whole or in part for those purposes and (without prejudice to the foregoing provisions of this definition) includes any wooden fence or similar structure used, or made suitable for use, for the display of advertisements, and any reference to the display of advertisements shall be construed accordingly
"Court" means a competent Court
"Special Development Ordinance" means an Ordinance as referred to in paragraph (b) of subsection (2) of article 22
"enforcement notice" has the meaning given to this term under Article 46
"clearing", in relation to immovable property, means the removal of buildings or materials from the immovable property, the leveling of the surface thereof and the performance of such other works in relation thereto as may be specified
"owner", in relation to immovable property, means the person who is registered or who is entitled to be registered in the books of the Provincial Land Office as the owner thereof
"determination" has the meaning given to this term under subsection (2) of article 44
"prescribed" (except in relation to matters specifically required or authorized under this Law as determined in various ways) means prescribed under Regulations or Decree or Order, issued or given pursuant to this Law
"Regulations" means Regulations issued under this Law
"building envelope" means the constructed building envelope, externally, which is formed by its supporting body;
"Joint Council" has the meaning given to this term under article 12
"White Zone" has the meaning given to this term under article 35
"means of access" includes any means of access, private or public, vehicular or pedestrian, and also includes a road
"ore" includes all material having economic value and forming part of the earth's crust or produced naturally from it, including mineral oil, bitumen, asphalt and natural gas, but not dissolved ore or trees, timber and similar types of forest products
"engineering works" includes the marking and construction of roads and the forming or marking of means of access to roads
"legislation" includes any Administrative Act
"street" means any street, public or private, and includes any street, square, public promenade, lane, cul-de-sac, alley, bridge, pedestrian thoroughfare, equestrian thoroughfare, thoroughfare or major public thoroughfare, whether arterial or not and whether existing or proposed in any plan, and includes all pavement, culvert or small bridge, underpass, skyway, tunnel
"buildings or works" includes waste materials, waste and other objects deposited on property, and reference to the erection or construction of buildings or works is interpreted accordingly
"building" includes any building of any material, caravan, tent and any part of a building, but does not include machinery or mechanical installations included within a building
It is understood that for the purposes of article 38 and in general for anything related to the authority to issue a Conservation Decree, the word "building" also means machines or mechanical installations that are included in the building or are firmly connected to the ground
"building work" includes demolition, reconstruction work, building conversion or additions or repairs to buildings and other work normally carried out by a person practicing the work of a builder
"plot" means a piece of land suitable for building purposes, which has been obtained by the implementation of a planning permit pursuant to this Law or a subdivision permit pursuant to the Regulation of Streets and Buildings Law, for the conversion or division of immovable property into a plot of land or into separate plots and for the latter a certificate of approval has been issued by the competent authority;
"appointed day", in relation to this Law or any provision of this Law, means the date appointed in accordance with article 88 for the commencement of the force of this Law or, as the case may be, the relevant provision of this Law
"planning permission" means a permission under Part Five with or without conditions and the term "conditional planning permission" includes a temporary planning permission
"planning decision" means a decision taken to grant or refuse to grant a development permit pursuant to Part Five or to grant a conditional or unconditional development permit, and includes a decision taken to grant a temporary planning permit or to determine or serve a notice pursuant to Article 28
"Urban Planning Authority" has the meaning given to this term under Article 4
"Urban Master Plan" means the detailed plan by which the layout and/or arrangement of infrastructure and uses, including plots, public roads, sidewalks, parking spaces, open public spaces, squares, public green spaces, spaces for social equipment, other common and public spaces, of a specific area, located within a development area and in which an urban redevelopment is prepared and/or implemented pursuant to the provisions of the Urban Development Zones (Urban Redevelopment) and Related Matters Law;
"temporary urban planning permit" acquires the meaning given to this term under subsection (4) of article 25
"development plan" includes an Island Plan, Local Plan, Area Plan, Town Planning Master Plan and Policy Statement, issued in accordance with the provisions of section 18A, and any such plan amended;
"Plan for the Island" has the meaning assigned to this term under Article 7 and includes a reference to the Plan in question as amended in accordance with Article 9
"Area Plan" has the meaning given to this term under article 13
"local authority" has the same meaning as the term "competent authority" in the Roads and Buildings Regulation Law or any law amending or replacing it
"Local Plan" has the meaning given to this term under Article 10 and includes a reference to the Plan in question as amended in accordance with Article 15
"the Minister" has the meaning given to this term under Article 3
"plot under development" means a piece of land suitable for building purposes, which will arise with the implementation of a valid urban planning permit pursuant to this Law and/or a subdivision permit pursuant to the Regulation of Streets and Buildings Law for the conversion or division of immovable property into a plot of land or into separate plots and the subdivision is at a stage where the curbs have been installed and the roads are easily passable by vehicles and has the area measurement of the plots is confirmed by a person competent for this purpose;
"existing development" means any development complying with the provisions of article 21 and any development completed in accordance with a planning permit duly granted and registered and complying with any conditions under which it was granted, and the terms "existing building" and "existing use" are interpreted accordingly
"bearing body" means the structural parts of a building, which statically support it (columns, beams, slabs, cantilevers).
"fence" includes a wooden fence, a picket fence, or otherwise, as well as embankments and walls;
"use", in relation to immovable property, does not include the use of immovable property for the execution of any building or other works on it.
(2) For the purposes of this Law, the development of a property is considered to have begun-
(a) if the development consists of the execution of works, at the time when those works commence
(b) if the development consists of a change of use, at the time when the new use begins
(c) if the development consists of the execution of works and the change of use, during the earlier of the times mentioned in the previous paragraphs.
(3) For the avoidance of doubt, whenever, by virtue of any provision of this Law, it is required that the value of an interest in real property be assessed under the condition that a planning permit would be granted for development of any category, this condition is imposed on the assumption that any such development would comply with the provisions of any legislation, other than this Law, which would be applicable to it.
(4) References in this Law to any piece of legislation are interpreted, unless otherwise apparent from the text, as references to said piece of legislation as it may have been amended under or by virtue of any other piece of legislation, including this Law.

## The Minister
3.-(1) For the purposes of this Law, the Minister is, as far as the Third Party is concerned, the Minister of Finance, but the Minister of the Interior, and in this Law the term "the Minister" is interpreted accordingly and includes any person, body or authority authorized by the Minister as he exercises his competence under this Law.
(2) In the exercise of his powers under this Law, the Minister consults any Committee established by a decision of the Council of Ministers for this purpose, any other Minister in relation to any matter related to the competences of this Minister and any person, body or authority for advice for which there is a special provision in this Law or in Regulations issued pursuant thereto, and any other persons possessing special knowledge on any subject on which the Minister shall consider it appropriate to receive advice may also be consulted.

*(90/1972)*

## Urban Planning Authority
4.-(1) Subject to the provisions of this Law, the Town Planning Authority is, as the case may be, the Minister or any authority to which powers have been delegated.
(2) Irrespective of any other provision of this Law, the Council of Ministers for the better implementation of the purposes of this Law will establish a Council of Regulations. The Regulations will therefore provide, among other things, about the name of the Council, its powers, the number of its members appointed by the Council of Ministers and the qualifications of the persons to be appointed and generally about the structure and operation of the Council. Thus, the Regulations may provide that all or any of the Minister's responsibilities under this Law may be transferred to the Council, or that the Council will be the Urban Planning Authority under this Law, in which case all of the Minister's responsibilities under this Law may be transferred to the Council and exercised under of this.

*(90/1972)*

## Transfer of powers of the Minister
5.-(1) Subject to the provisions of this Law, the Minister may transfer, if required by the Council of Ministers to transfer, to any person, body or authority the power to exercise any of his powers under this Law. Such transfer may be made to various persons, bodies or authorities in relation to various areas or in relation to the exercise of various responsibilities.
(2) Such transfer is carried out by a Decree issued by the Minister and published in the official gazette of the Republic.

*(90/1972)*

## Decrees, etc. under the Minister
6. In the exercise of his powers under this Law, the Minister acquires the power to issue such Decrees and give such Orders as he deems necessary or desirable to achieve the purposes of this Law.

## Local Plans
10.-(1) The Minister may, and must, if so required by the Council of Ministers, conduct a survey of any area and draw up a plan (referred to in this Law as a "Local Plan") for that area in accordance with the provisions of this Part and any Regulations issued pursuant to this Law and in compliance with these provisions.
(2) This area (in this Law referred to as "Local Plan Area") is defined by reference to a map.

*(90/1972)*

## Purposes, type, etc., of the Local Plan
11.-(1) The purposes of the Local Plan are to ensure methodical development in the interest of the health, amenities, services and general well-being of the community, to indicate the general principles on which development in the area will be promoted and controlled, to guide the selection or designation of sites for special purposes, or to carry out development on them or otherwise, the protection of characteristic elements or areas of social, historical or architectural importance, the provision of transportation lines of road arteries, pipelines and other services and the indication of the stages in which the development should be carried out.
(2) In all Local Plans, the aims and objectives of any applicable Plan for the Town are taken into account.
(3) Subject to the provisions of any Regulations issued pursuant to this Law to regulate the type and content of Local Plans, every Local Plan shall include such maps and such descriptive material as may be required to explain the proposals in question to such a degree of detail as may be appropriate for various parts of the Local Plan Area, and shall define the population for which the Plan will make a prediction and provide an indication of the intended general use zones in terms of land and buildings and those required to protect and serve these transportation zones, and such a Plan may specifically determine the areas of the proposed roads, public and other buildings and projects, airports, public gardens, recreational areas, areas of protection of the nature and other open spaces and the accesses to them, or to provide for the distribution of areas for residential use or for agricultural, industrial, commercial, touristic or other purposes of any category defined in the Plan.
(4) Without affecting the generality of subsection (3), a Local Plan may be provided for matters mentioned, among others-
(a)(i) to the distance between buildings, the distance between buildings and boundaries and the distance between buildings and the center of any street
(ii) in respect of plots, to the proportion or percentage of the land which may be covered by any buildings
(iii) to the minimum size of plots
(iv) at the height of buildings
(v) in the floor area of buildings
(vi) in the area of property that will be dedicated and reserved exclusively for the parking of vehicles and for the creation of public parking spaces
(b) in the reservation of spaces for new roads
(c) in the disuse of any existing road
(d) the density of population in any area
(e) in the destination of lands for public open spaces.

## Revision of Local Plan or Area Plan.
14. (4) A development area in relation to which an Urban Development Plan has been drawn up for the purposes of urban redevelopment is excluded from the revision of the Local Plan or Area Plan, until the completion of the process of implementing the urban redevelopment.

## Policy Statement
18A.-(1) The Minister may, and shall, if required by the Council of Ministers, prepare a Policy Statement regarding which applications for planning permission for development will be considered, in any area for which there is no Local Plan or Area Plan in force for the purpose of methodical development in the interest of health, amenities, service and general welfare:
Provided that when a Local Plan or Area Plan comes into force in respect of any such area, the Local Plan or Area Plan shall prevail over the Policy Statement.
(2) The Policy Statement takes into account the aims and objectives of any applicable Plan for the City.
(3) The Policy Statement shall outline the general policy for the promotion and control of development on a regional basis, shall provide for the allocation of areas for residential use or for agricultural, industrial, commercial or other purposes, shall define nature conservation areas, village conservation areas, and areas of natural beauty, shall generally not contain the general First of all, it will govern the regulation and control of development in the countryside and villages.
(4) The Policy Statement is accompanied by such maps and other descriptive material as may be required to explain such Statement.
(5) The Policy Statement is under continuous review by the Minister and is the subject of a report published under him, in such manner as the Council of Ministers may determine, at intervals not exceeding five years. This report includes such proposals for amending the Policy Statement as the Minister considers desirable.
(6) Upon the preparation or amendment of the Policy Statement, the Minister files a copy of it with the Office of the District and the District Office of Town Planning and Housing of the province in which the area covered by the Policy Statement is located and publishes as provided for in subsection (7) a Notice stating that the Policy Statement has been prepared or it was amended and that a copy thereof was filed as aforesaid and will be available for inspection by any interested party at any reasonable time.
(7) The Notification referred to in subsection (6)-
(a) is published in the official gazette of the Republic
(b) is published in at least two newspapers and
(c) is posted on notice boards or other prominent points of the District Office, the District Office of Town Planning and Housing, and the Offices of the Improvement Councils and Rural Authorities of the province in which the area included in the Policy Statement is located.
(8) The Policy Statement which refers to a notice issued in accordance with the provisions of subsection (6), comes into force on the day that this notice is published in the official newspaper of the Republic and the other conditions of subsections (6) and (7) are observed.
(9) Reasoned objections based on specific reasons may be submitted in writing to the Minister within a period of four months from the publication of the notification in the official gazette of the Republic.

## Conflict between development plans
19. If during this time and with reference to this area there are two or more development plans, in the event of a conflict between the apparent intentions which are incorporated in these plans, the provisions of the larger scale plan prevail.

*(90/1972)*

## Interpretation of "development"
20.-(1) In this Law, unless otherwise derived from the text, "development" means, subject to the following provisions of this article, the execution of building, mechanical, mining or other works within, on, above or below real property or the execution of any substantial change in the use of any building or other real property.
(2) For the avoidance of doubt, for the purposes of this article, development includes-
(a) the use as two or more dwellings of any building previously a single dwelling, regardless of whether any such change of use requires any construction or not
(b) the depositing of refuse or useless objects on real property, despite the fact that the real property is included in a space already used for this purpose, if either the surface area of the deposit is so extended or the height of the deposit is so extended as to exceed the level of the property adjacent to that space
(c) without prejudice to any provisions of any law for the time being in force relating to the control of advertisements or any Regulations issued pursuant to this Law and relating to the control of advertisements, the use for the display of advertisements of any external part of a building which is not normally used for that purpose
(d) the conversion or division of any immovable property into a plot and/or into separate plots;
(e) the demolition of any building
(f) the opening of any road.

## Penalties for non-compliance with an enforcement notice

(4) If, after the conviction of a person by virtue of the previous provisions of this article, that person does not do as soon as practicable everything that depends on him to ensure compliance with the enforcement notice, he is guilty of a further offense and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding two hundred pounds for each day following the first conviction thereof, any of the requirements of the enforcement notice (other than the termination of the use of the property) remain unfulfilled.

## Offenses
87.-(1) Wherever-
(a) willfully obstructs any person in the exercise of any of his powers or in the performance of any of his duties under this Law or willfully interferes with such exercise or performance
(b) knowingly makes any false or inaccurate statement in any document made or provided pursuant to this Law or for the purposes thereof or for the purposes of any development plan pursuant thereto
(c) omits or refuses to provide or present any information, statement or other document required under this Law or for the purposes thereof,
is guilty of an offense and is liable upon conviction to imprisonment not exceeding four months or to a fine not exceeding ,450 or to both of these penalties.
(2) Wherever-
(a) initiates any development or performs any act or acts or permits the performance of any development or act in violation of this Law or any Regulation, Decree, notice or plan issued or made pursuant to this Law
(b) fails to comply with any obligation or condition imposed on him under any Regulation, Decree, planning permit, plan, notice or Order issued, granted, made or given under this Law, or to perform any act or work while he is required to do so under this Law or under any Regulation, Decree, planning permit, plan, notice or Order,
is guilty of an offense and is liable upon conviction to imprisonment not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding five hundred pounds or to both of these penalties.
(3) For the avoidance of any doubt, the provisions of subsection (2) shall apply to every owner of immovable property, to every person who has under his control any immovable property and to every architect, engineer, building contractor, or any other person acting in any development of any immovable property on behalf of the owner thereof.
(4) The court before which a charge is brought against any person for an offense committed in contravention of subsection (2) may, on application without notice (ex parte), order the suspension of all further work in respect of any development or part thereof, until the final adjudication of the case:
It is understood that the issuance of such a decree is subject to the provisions of the Civil Procedure Law, the Courts Law and the Civil Procedure Procedural Regulations.
(5) In addition to any other penalty prescribed by this article, the court before which a person has been convicted of any criminal offense under subsection (2) may order:
(a) such building or any part thereof as the case may be, in relation to which the criminal offense was committed is demolished and/or moved within such time as may be specified in such order, unless in the meantime permission has been obtained in respect thereof from the Planning Authority;
(b) in the case of a building for which the specific use of the building is not in accordance with the use approved, based on the relevant permit, the termination of the use of this building within the period specified in the decree of the court, but not exceeding two months unless in the meantime the relevant permit for the specific use is secured by the Town Planning Authority;
(c) the person convicted shall pay the costs of the proceedings and any fees relating to the charge which such person should have paid and which he has omitted or refused or neglected to pay.
(6) If any person against whom a decree has been made under subsections (4) and (5), fails or neglects to comply with such decree within the time specified therein, it shall be lawful for the Planning Authority to execute such decree and the costs incurred in carrying it out shall be paid to the Planning Authority by the person against whom the decree is made and such costs shall be deemed to be a penalty under meaning of the Civil Procedure Law and the payment is imposed pursuant to them.
(7) The Town Planning Authority when executing an ordinance as referred to in subsection (6) may request the assistance of the Police, which must provide it, as and when it wanted to be requested by the Town Planning Authority.

(e) concern the non-application of the terms of the issued urban planning permit related to the non-construction or improper construction of infrastructure projects provided for in the permit. In this case, the town planning permit by way of derogation may be issued as long as the independent operation of the building, the self-sufficiency and comfortable fruiting are not affected and with the condition of the construction of the infrastructure works within a regular deadline as determined by the Town Planning Authority, depending on the required time, in accordance with the conditions of the permit and a relevant report is filed by a registered member of ETEK, an architect or a civil engineer, with an effective annual professional license:
Provided that in the event that the applicant is a buyer and incurs the cost of constructions that do not directly concern his unit but affect its independent operation and comfortable fruition, he may recover with a lawsuit from the owner or another buyer to the extent that each is responsible, any amount of money spent by him for work done in order to comply with a notification of a competent administrative body. In the event of the applicant's refusal to comply with the constructions that do not concern his unit and if these relate to the implementation of conditions for the construction of infrastructure projects provided for in the original urban planning permit, the Commission may recommend to the Urban Planning Authority to proceed with legal procedures, to complete the infrastructure projects, as provided for in the conditions of the permit, and to demand, pursuant to the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Law, the payment of the cost by the owner or buyer to the extent that each is responsible and in the event of non-payment, the payment of their expenses may be requested through a court.
2. Irregularities concerning existing buildings on plots under construction, whose planning permits were issued based on the provisions of the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Laws of 1972 to 2002, and which have expired, without the construction works required by the permit being completed on site if:
(a) the plots on which the existing building is located are part of a subdivision that is being promoted in phases and are included in the phase where the required works have been completed or at least are at such a stage of construction that there is independent operation and only small-scale constructions remain: Provided that in this case the Certificate of Completion of Works is granted for the part of the separation where the required works have been completed as a reservation of article 10 of the Regulation of Roads and Buildings Law.
(b) not all required work has been completed due to serious deficiencies where the project is not progressing in phases. In such a case the owner of the immovable property is required to complete within a regular deadline the remaining works as the Town Planning Authority wanted to determine and in accordance with the terms of the town planning permit. In case the owner is unable to respond for objective reasons:
(i) since the constructions concern an existing road, the Town Planning Authority is invited to investigate the possibility of undertaking the completion of the works at its own expense, in accordance with the provisions of article 49 of the Town Planning and Spatial Planning Laws 1972 to 2002.
(ii) subsequently, in the event that the Town Planning Authority is unable to take the initiative to remove the defects and since the constructions do not concern the completion of an existing road, the buyer may submit an application for a town planning permit in accordance with article 12 of this Law to complete those works, which are required as a minimum, in addition to those corresponding to him, so as to achieve a comfortable and independent fruition of his unit:
Provided that in the event that the buyer, in order to implement this permit, also incurs the expense of constructions that do not directly concern him, but affect the independent operation and comfortable fruiting of his unit, he may demand from the owner of the property or also the buyer to the extent that each is responsible, any amount of money spent by him for work done in order to comply with a notification of a competent administrative body;
(c) not all work has been completed because a border dispute or other ownership problem is pending. In such a case, the size of the border difference is identified and if a small section of land is affected which leaves the larger part of the separation unaffected, the Commission may recommend to the Town Planning Authority to request, as the case may be, from the specific owner or the buyer the completion of the work within a specified period in the corresponding part that belongs to him in the unaffected part of the separation which does not affect other property and assuming that in both cases there will be the possibility of independent and comfortable fruiting, in a time determined by it, the Town Planning Authority grants the permit.
The validity of this Law [S.S.: i.e. L. 150(I)/2012] is considered to have started from 31h October 2012.
