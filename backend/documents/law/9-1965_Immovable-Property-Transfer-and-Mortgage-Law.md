## The Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law of 1965 (9/1965)

## Short title
1. The Transfer and Mortgage of Immovables Acts of 1965 to 1981 shall be referred to collectively as the Transfer and Mortgage of Immovables Acts of 1965 to 1981.

*(9/1965)*

## Interpretation
2.-(1) In this Law, unless otherwise derived from the text-
"real estate" has the meaning assigned to this term in article 2 of the Real Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law
"value" has the meaning assigned to this term in article 2 of the Real Estate (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law
"prohibitions" has the meaning assigned to this term in article 12
"acceptance", after the grammatical variations of this term as well as related expressions, as long as it is used in reference to any declaration of transfer, means the acceptance of such declaration by the competent official of the Provincial Land Registry Office or annex pursuant to article 8 or 9
"competent Provincial Land Office" for matters concerning immovable property, means the Provincial Land Office of the Province in which the immovable property is located or any other office or place that the Director, by notice in the Official Gazette of the Republic, wished to specify for all or some of the provisions of this Law and includes any branch of the Provincial Land Office operating in that province, and the term "competent Provincial Lands Officer” will be interpreted accordingly:
Provided that only such operations are carried out in an annex as authorized by the Director;
"competent officer" means an officer of the Provincial Land Office or branch who has been authorized by the Director to accept declarations of transfer or mortgage
"administrator" means an administrator appointed under the provisions of the Condemned Property Act, or the provisions of the Criminal Damages Act
“covered securities business manager” has the meaning assigned to that term by the Covered Securities Act 2010;
"Director" means the Director of the Cadastre and Spatial Department of the Ministry of the Interior, and includes the District Cadastre Officer and any other officer appointed by the Director for all or any of the purposes of this Law, either generally or for special purposes
"beneficiary" means the person to whom immovable property is voluntarily transferred or, as the case may be, mortgage
"grantor" means the owner voluntarily transferring immovable property or, as the case may be, the mortgagor voluntarily transferring a mortgage
"registered", with its grammatical variations and related expressions, means registered in the land register, whether it is handwritten or computerized, and the date of registration is considered the date on which the entry precedes in time in any of the two registers;
"assessed value" acquires, regardless of the meaning attributed to the definition of the word "value" in this paragraph, the meaning given to the word "value" in the Annex to the Land Surveying and Spatial Department (Fees and Rights) Law
"de facto weight" has the meaning assigned to this term in article 12
"mortgagor" means the person in whose favor a mortgage is made
"mortgagor" means a property owner securing a mortgage over such property
"province" means a cadastral province, as defined in article 39 of the Real Estate (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law
"District Court" for matters relating to any immovable property means the District Court of the province in which such immovable property is located
"Provincial Cadastre Office" means the provincial office of the Cadastre and Spatial Department
"District Cadastre Officer" means the head of the District Cadastre Office
"trustee", as far as this appears from the text, means a commissioner pursuant to the provisions of the law in force and relating to trusts, and includes a bankruptcy trustee pursuant to the provisions of the Bankruptcy Law
"another Provincial Land Office" for matters relating to any property means the Provincial Land Office of another province or the province in which the property is located
"commissioner" means a commissar of enemy property appointed under the provisions of the Trading with the Enemy Act
"Sales Regulations" means the Sales Regulations made pursuant to the provisions of article 101 of the Civil Procedure Law, or any other Regulations amending or replacing them
"guardianship" means a guardian pursuant to the provisions of the Guardianship of Minors and Prodigals Law
"land registry" means the land registry maintained pursuant to the provisions of the Real Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law
"owner" means the registered owner of the property
"transfer", after the grammatical terms of this term, variations and related expressions, means-
(a) in respect of immovable property, the transfer of title thereto from one person to another
(b) as regards mortgaged property, the assignment thereof by the mortgagee to another person,
by voluntary action of the above persons
"annex" means an annex of the Provincial Land Registry Office operating for the processing of public affairs on the days determined by the Director
"personal representative" has the meaning given to this term in article 2 of the Law on the Administration of Inheritance, and includes the executor or administrator as defined in article 2 of the Law on Wills and Inheritance, as well as a consular official managing an inheritance pursuant to the provisions of article 2 of the Law on Consular Contracts
"mortgage", after the grammatical variations of the term and related expressions, means an encumbrance created on a property at the will of the owner to secure a definitive, future or to discharge a monetary debt or obligation and includes, without limitation, the post-severance mortgages, as this term is referred to in article 32A.
(2) For the purposes of Articles 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35, 36, 44, 49 and Part VI, the terms "mortgagor" and "mortgagor" in subsection (1) of this section include, unless otherwise provided from the text, the heirs, personal representatives, administrators, guardian and trustees of these persons, as well as the trustees of the property of such persons and-
(a) on a mortgagee, the term includes any person who, by virtue of one or more transfers of the mortgage as provided for in article 32, is entitled to receive the amount secured by the mortgage
(b) on a mortgagee, the term includes any person over-
(i) by virtue of one or more transfers of the mortgaged property, as provided for in article 31 or
(ii) after the purchase by auction of such property subject to the mortgage, as provided for in subsection (6) of article 41,
is the respective owner of such property.
(3) For the purposes of articles 29, 31, 37, 40, 41 and 42, the term "existing mortgage" or "predecessor mortgage" includes any encumbrance from under numbers 5, 6, 8 and 10 of Part I of the First Schedule, and the term "mortgagor" of an existing or predecessor mortgage refers to, in the case of such an encumbrance, to the person, authority or organization for whose benefit such an encumbrance acts.

(b) the Director may authorize the use of other types of a similar nature, as well as the modification necessary under the circumstances to those set forth in the Second Annex (any deviation of such or modified type from the corresponding types set forth in the Second Annex, as long as it is not a substantial difference affects the valid or normal of such type
(2) Whenever it is stipulated in this Law that the Director takes a certain action, before or after taking such action, he may demand that the fees or rights collected for such action be paid first in accordance with the provisions of the Land and Land Survey Department (Fees and Rights) Law, or any of another law amending or replacing this Law.

## The registered property owner may transfer or mortgage this
4.-(1) Subject to the provisions of this and any other Law in force at any given time, the owner of the property may transfer or mortgage it or any undivided ideal portion thereof to another person or persons.
(2) The provisions of subsection (1) are applicable to two or more principals of two or more properties, and to two or more contiguous parts of any property:
It is understood that on a mortgage the aforementioned owners or co-owners of a property are both jointly and severally liable for the payment of the mortgage debt, in the manner specified in the mortgage contract or provided for in the provisions of this Law.
(3) Irrespective of the provisions of subsection (1), whenever a person's immovable property belongs to another natural or legal person or authority by virtue of the provisions of any law in force at any given time, the first mentioned above person is no longer considered the owner of such property, despite the fact that it is registered in his name in the books of the Provincial Land Registry Office.

*(9/1965)*

## Valid transfer and mortgage of property
5.-(1) No transfer or mortgage of real estate is valid unless made in accordance with the provisions of this Law.
(2) An attempt to transfer or mortgage a property made otherwise or in accordance with the provisions of this Law in no way induces the establishment, change, transfer, depreciation of any right or interest in a property, it does not affect this in any way.
(3) None of the provisions contained in this article shall apply to the acquisition of any right, privilege, liberty, servitude, or any advantage pursuant to the provisions of Article 11 of the Immovable Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law, or to the transfer thereof pursuant to the provisions of subsection (1) of Article 12 of aforementioned law.

*(9/1965)*

## Transfers and mortgages between companies or cooperatives and their members
6.-(1) The provisions of this Law apply to any transfer or mortgage of real estate made between any company and any of its directors or shareholders, or between any cooperative and any of the partners (transfer or mortgage of real property, made in exchange for the grant of company shares or as a participation in the capital of the cooperative or otherwise, is not valid unless declared and registered pursuant to the provisions of this Law.
(2) In this article "company" and "cooperative" mean a company or, as the case may be, another legal entity or cooperative, incorporated under the provisions of the law in force at any given time or, if not so incorporated, having the power to acquire real estate under the provisions of the Legal Entities (Registration of Real Estate) Law or any of the other party in force from time to time.

*(9/1965)*

## The licensor or mortgagor must be registered
7. It is prohibited to transfer or mortgage real estate to any person other than the owner:
It is understood that, subject to the provisions of this Law, the person to whom the immovable property has been registered in the name of another person pursuant to the provisions of any other law, shall be considered the owner of such immovable property for the purposes of this article.

*(9/1965)*

## Transfer or mortgage declaration procedure
8.-(1) Anyone who wishes to transfer or mortgage or accept a transfer or mortgage of real estate must, like the person with whom he agreed the intended transfer or mortgage, present himself to the competent Provincial Land Office during the working hours specified under article 46, and declare such transfer or mortgage to the competent officer of the Office as appointed by
(a) the submission and validation of a written statement as provided for in article 18 or, as the case may be, in article 21
(b) the bringing of other documents that are not provided for under this Law or that were required by the Director at the end of the application of the provisions of this Law or any Law in force at any given time
(c) the verification of his signatures on the declaration and on any slip, attachment or other document bearing his signature, by his declaration that he recognizes such signatures as his own
in compliance with the provisions of this Law, the competent official accepts the declaration of transfer or mortgage made and keeps the declaration and the aforementioned documents and notes as a signature on the document of the declaration the fact that the declaration was made before him and was accepted by him, as well as the date and time of the declaration and such acceptance.
(2) Irrespective of the provisions of subsection (1), in the event of a transfer of immovable property belonging to the Republic or which has come to the Republic pursuant to the provisions of any law, it shall be carried out either pursuant to the provisions of this Law, or by filing with the competent Provincial Land Office the decision of the Council of Ministers on the transfer, together with a relevant statement of acceptance by the person in whose name the transfer will be made. The declaration of acceptance shall state both the name and address of the person accepting the transfer and the details of the immovable property being transferred:
Provided that, in the event that the transfer of immovable property is made to a beneficiary, in accordance with the Immovable Property (Grant of Property Titles to Displaced Persons and Other Persons) (Special) Regulations 2006 or any Regulations amending or replacing them, and the said beneficiary died on or after April 7, 2006, then the beneficiary is deemed to be in life on the date of transfer or registration of the immovable property in his name and the transfer is made without the presentation, by any, of the relevant declaration of acceptance, in accordance with this subsection.

## Declarations in another province or in annexes
9. Regardless of the provisions of article 8, declarations of transfer or mortgage may be made-
(a) before the competent branch office of the competent Provincial Land Registry Office or
(b) before the competent officer of any other Provincial Land Registry Office or annex, the bringing of an additional copy of such declaration:
Provided that such declaration is made at the own risk of the franchisee or, as the case may be, the mortgagor-
(a) in respect of the fact that the transferred or mortgaged property is not registered in the name of the grantor or, as the case may be, of the mortgagee, on the day and time such declaration is made, or in respect of the fact that such property is then subject to any encumbrance, or that the licensor or mortgagor is prohibited from managing his property
(b) as regards the Director's refusal to register such a transfer or, as the case may be, a mortgage, in the exercise of the discretionary power not acquired by virtue of the provisions of article 14 when such declaration is forwarded to the competent Provincial Land Registry Office.

## Declarations by representative
10.-(1) Whenever it is intended to make a declaration of transfer or mortgage of real estate or to accept such a transfer or mortgage through an agent, unless the agent acquires authority by virtue of the provisions of any other law to make such a declaration on behalf of the represented person, the latter must prove to the Director that the person on account to whom the declaration is made, provide him with a written authorization, duly endorsed by the authority of an authority and notarized in accordance with the provisions of the law in force at any given time, to carry out such declaration of transfer, or, depending on the case, mortgage:
Provided that the Director may-
(a) before or after accepting the statement made, to demand the production of evidence that the authorization provided has not been revoked, in any case as it deems appropriate either because of the long time that has passed since the signing of the relevant document or for any other reason
(b) not to allow the declaration to be made by virtue of the authorization deemed to have been provided by the aforesaid document, if in his opinion there is a defect or ambiguity about the authorization provided or doubt about the identity of the person who signed the document, or about the competence of the authority sanctioning the document in the country where the document was signed, until until such defect is rectified or, as the case may be, such ambiguity or doubt is removed to the satisfaction of the Director.
(2) If the Director accepts the declaration made, the document referred to in subsection (1) is filed and kept with the competent Provincial Land Registry Offices:
Of course-
(a) if so requested, the aforesaid document may be returned and replaced by a photocopy thereof, certified as true by the Director
(b) if the document was already deposited in another Provincial Land Registry Office, a copy certified by the Director as a true copy is accepted instead of the original.
(3) For the purposes of this article "a document duly notarized by the competent authority" means a document bearing
(a) if it was signed in Cyprus, the seal and signature of a certifying officer appointed and acting in accordance with the provisions of the Certifying Officers Law, or the seal and signature of the Mukhtar and the signature of an Aza of any city, village or parish, acting in accordance with the provisions of article 82 of the Law on Real Estate Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law
(b) if signed in any other country-
(i) the seal and signature of a consular official of the Republic, or a person having authority as usual for such duties on behalf of the Republic, and either such seal and signature were placed thereon to certify the signature of the person who signed the document, or to certify the signature of any person having the authority from time to time by law as a signature certificate in such country, or issue documents proving the signature of documents of the nature provided for in this article
(ii) if in such country there is no consular officer of the Republic or another person having authority as in the exercise of such duties on behalf of the Republic, the seal and signature of any person whom the Director is satisfied that he has from time to time the authority to certify signatures in such country or issue documents evidencing the signature of documents of the nature provided for in this article

## Declarations on behalf of disabled persons
11. Any person who is incapacitated or otherwise prohibited from managing his affairs and property by virtue of the provisions of any law in force from time to time (hereinafter in this article the person referred to as an "incapacitated person"), any declaration of transfer or mortgage of property in which this person participates as to the contracting parties, it is carried out on his account by the guardian, administrator, commissioner or, as the case may be, the tax collector of his property, upon the presentation of documents satisfactory to the Director and issued by a court or other authority having authority to issue such documents by virtue of the provisions of any law in force at any given time-
(a) appointment of guardian, administrator, commissioner or, as the case may be, tax collector:
Provided that it is not required to bring such a document by a person acting as guardian pursuant to the provisions of paragraph (a) or (b) of article 6 of the Law on Guardianship of Minors and Vagrants and
(b) authorization of the executed declaration of transfer or mortgage:
Provided that it is not required to bring such a document-
(i) whenever, by virtue of the provisions of any law for the time being in force, the guardian, administrator, commissioner or registrar acquires authority to make such declaration on behalf of the incapacitated person without the aforesaid authorization or
(ii) when such declaration concerns the transfer or mortgage of real estate to an incompetent person, made either without any consideration or against consideration, or as stated in writing by the guardian, administrator, commissioner or tax collector, it was not paid out of money or, as the case may be, other property of the disabled person.

*(9/1965)*

## Transfers and mortgages affected by an encumbrance or prohibition
12.-(1) Except as provided for in article 29 or 31, whenever immovable property is subject to any encumbrance or its owner is under prohibition-
(a) no declaration of transfer or mortgage of such property shall be accepted except by the competent Provincial Land Registry Offices
(b) no declaration of transfer or mortgage of such property shall be accepted by any other Provincial Land Registry Office or annex, when the existence of such encumbrance or prohibition is known to the competent officer of the other such Land Registry Office or, as the case may be, appendix
(c) no transfer or mortgage of such property declared at any other Provincial Land Registry Office or attachment without the knowledge of the competent official regarding the existence of such encumbrance or prohibition shall be registered without the competent Provincial Land Registry Office.
(2) When two or more properties are included in this declaration of transfer or mortgage and any one of them is subject to an encumbrance, the provisions of paragraph (c) of subsection (1) will be applied to the said declaration in its entirety, and if any other of the properties included in the declaration is not subject to this or any other encumbrance:
It is understood that with regard to such other free real estate, the transfer or mortgage may, with the consent of both parties to the declaration, be subject to registration following such changes regarding the consideration or other possible conditions as the parties may agree on this.
(3) Whenever the simultaneous execution of two transfer declarations is requested for the execution of an agreement regarding the exchange of real estate between two persons, and the immovable properties included in one of the declarations constitute in whole or in part the consideration for the transfer of those included in the other declarations, and any of such properties in either of the aforementioned declarations is subject to a real encumbrance, the provisions of paragraph (c) of subsection (1) will be applied to both declarations and if any other real estate included in this or the other declaration is free of all real encumbrances.
(4) When the simultaneous execution of two transfer declarations is requested in order to execute an agreement concerning the exchange of real estate between these persons and the immovable properties are included in one of the declarations, the consideration for the transfer of those included in the other declarations is, in whole or in part, and any of such persons is subject to prohibition, the provisions of paragraph (c) of subsection (1) will be applied to both statements and if the other of the persons is not subject to such prohibition.
(5) In this article-
(a) "encumbrance" means a direct charge on real property, a claim, lien or obligation, existing by virtue of the provisions of any law in force from time to time
(b) "prohibition" means the prohibition imposed by him and as a result of which he is deprived of the right to transfer or mortgage all or part of his property by virtue of the provisions of any law in force at any time
(6) Without affecting the generality of subsection (5) and subject to the provisions of subsection (7), it is hereby declared that the encumbrances and the prohibitions due to them are determined respectively in Parts I and II of the First Annex constitute encumbrances and prohibitions within the meaning of this article:
It is understood that the Council of Ministers may, by decree published in the official gazette of the Republic, make such amendments, deletions or additions to the First Annex as may be deemed necessary to implement the provisions of the law in force at any given time.
(7) None of the present articles
(a) constitutes an encumbrance or prohibition, unless this has been notified, or any document relating thereto has been declared, registered, brought or, as the case may be, deposited with the relevant District Land Office, all the formalities required by any law or regulations have been complied with and all payables for their acceptance or registration, fees and royalties must be paid
(b) affects the powers of any person authorized by virtue of the provisions of any applicable law from time to time such that the transfer or mortgage of immovable property belonging to a person is prohibited.

## Transfer of immovable subject to an encumbrance, etc. in some cases
12A.-(1) Regardless of the provisions of this Law, the owner of a property subject to an encumbrance or affected by a temporary Court order prohibiting him from expropriating such property may transfer it by declaring the sale and depositing the entire sale price to the competent Provincial Land Registry Office, which accepts such declaration as long as the sale price stated in it is not lower than the amount determined by the competent Court as representing the market value of the property and as long as the competent Provincial Land Office has not received a written notification of the registration of an appeal against the determination of the market value of the property by the decision of the competent Court.
(2) The market value of the property is determined by the competent Court following the submission by the owner of the property of an originating summons, served on those for whose benefit the encumbrance or temporary decree referred to in subsection (1) exists.
(3) Any interested person may, no earlier than four months from the date on which the decision of the competent Court was issued, apply to the competent Court for a review of the market value of the property determined by the decision.
(4) The applicant shall give a written notice to the competent Provincial Land Office of the registration of such application, and the competent Provincial Land Office shall not accept any declaration of sale in relation to the property referred to in the application until the decision on the application is issued.
(5) The sale price deposited with the competent Provincial Land Registry Offices pursuant to subsection (1) shall be allocated in order of priority to those for whose benefit there is an encumbrance on the property.
(6) In this article-
"competent Court" means the District Court in the District in which the property is located.

## Questions and further evidence about statements
13.-(1) Whenever it is intended to make a declaration of transfer or mortgage before the competent or any other Provincial Land Office or annex, or whenever such a declaration is accepted by any other Provincial Land Office or annex, the Director may, before exercising the discretionary power is prevented by virtue of article 14, to request that the persons making such a statement or any of them to answer orally or in writing to any question that he reasonably deems necessary to submit, including questions concerning
(a) the identity and nationality of the contracting parties
(b) the contractual capacity of the parties by virtue of the provisions of any law in force at any given time and the capacity of persons to appear before the Provincial Land Office or annex to make the declaration
(c) the continuation or non-continuance of the authority of one person such as a declaration of transfer or mortgage for the account of another person
(d) the capacity of any of the parties to the declaration as he perceives the nature of the intended tort;
(e) the existence, identity, nature, condition, area or quantity of the property intended to be transferred or mortgaged
(f) the ownership or possession of the property intended to be transferred or mortgaged
(g) any interest related to the property intended to be transferred or mortgaged and owned by either of the parties to the intended declaration or to any other person
(h) the existence of any term, covenant or agreement not disclosed in the declaration or otherwise recorded
(i) the amount of any consideration and, in the case of a transfer, the date on which it was agreed and
(j) in the case of a transfer, the existence of any agreement to re-transfer the property to the transferor either upon the payment of any amount or upon the occurrence of any event.
(2) The Director may, instead of submitting to the parties of the intended declaration any of the questions mentioned in subsection (1) above, or in addition to them, demand the production of a sworn statement or confirmation or other evidence necessary in his judgment concerning any of the above questions.
(3) Whenever the Director has any doubt in relation to any of the issues covered in paragraphs (e), (f) and (g) of subsection (1), or in relation to any information or evidence provided to him under the parties to a statement intended on the above matters, before he accepts the statement made, he may order the carrying out of an autopsy by an official of the Cadastre and Spatial Department at the owner's expense.
(4) The Director may allow the declaration of the transfer or mortgage of any property before the autopsy is carried out as provided for in subsection (3), provided that the fees and rights collectable under the provisions of the Law in force at any given time in relation to the performance of such autopsy will be paid at the same time after the fees and rights collectable under this law on the registration of the title on the transferred property or, as the case may be, registration of the mortgage.
(5) Regardless of anything contained in this Law, at any time after the acceptance of the declaration of transfer or mortgage and before its registration, the Director may order the performance of an autopsy by an official of the Land and Spatial Department on the property in respect of the declaration of transfer or mortgage (if the Director so decrees, the fees and royalties, pursuant to the provisions of the law in force at any given time, collected for the performance of such autopsy shall be considered for the purposes of such Law as the remainder of the fees or royalties collected for the performance of any service at the request of the licensee or, as the case may be, the mortgage debtor.

## Director's discretion
14. The Director may refuse to allow the competent official of the Provincial Land Office or annex to accept any declaration of transfer or mortgage or, when such declaration was made in a Provincial Land Office of another province or of the province where the property is intended to be transferred, or mortgage or attachment thereof, the Director may refuse to register such transfer or mortgage in any of the following circumstances, namely-
(a) whenever from any provided information, adduced evidence or certification made at the request of the Director pursuant to article 13 or from the findings of an autopsy conducted by order of the Director pursuant to subsection (3) of the aforementioned article-
(i) the Director has any doubt in relation to the issue referred to in paragraphs (a), (b), (c) and (d) of subsection (1) of the aforementioned article
(ii) this judges that in terms of an essential element of this the immovable to which the transfer or mortgage is intended and the ownership thereof do not coincide according to the description entered in the land register
(iii) in the case of an intended transfer of property, the Director is convinced that there is an agreement to re-transfer it to the licensor upon the payment of any amount or the occurrence of any event, or that there is another agreement, condition or agreement, subject to or inconsistent with the nature of the transfer
(b) whenever any of the provisions of this Law is not observed or
(c) whenever the intended transfer or mortgaging would contradict or conflict with the provisions of any other party currently in force.

*(9/1965)*

## Payment of fees
15.-(1) If the declaration of transfer or mortgage is generally accepted, all the fees and rights collected under the provisions of the applicable law for the registration of such transfer or mortgage shall be paid to the Provincial Land Office or annex as soon as such declaration is accepted, on the day the acceptance is entered (the declaration is considered invalid and devoid of any legal force as long as the aforementioned fees and rights were not paid on the day specified above:
It is understood that the provisions of this article do not apply to fees and rights or part of them that were determined as receivable after the day on which the relevant declaration was accepted.
(2) If the payment of the fees and rights referred to in subsection (1) is made by check issued on a Bank operating in Cyprus, but it is not paid if it is presented for payment, such fees and rights are considered unpaid on the day on which the declaration was accepted.

*(9/1965)*

## Registration of transfer or mortgage
16.-(1) In compliance with the provisions of articles 24 and 25 of the Immovable Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law and articles 14 and 15, the Director registers any transfer or mortgage of real estate declared pursuant to the provisions of this Law and accepted by the relevant official.
(2) The transfer is registered by registering the name of the licensee as the owner of the transferred property.
(3) The registration of a mortgage is done by the registration of such a mortgage against the registration of the mortgaged property and a note on the certificate of registration of the said property, relating to the mortgage.
(4) If the transfer or mortgage registration is carried out, the Director issues, depending on the case-
(a) to the licensee, a certificate of registration of the transferred property in his name
(b) to the mortgagor, a certificate of registration of the mortgage in his name, noted on the contract and declaration of the mortgage, and accompanied by a certificate of registration of the mortgaged property in the name of the mortgagor
(c) to the mortgagee, a copy of the mortgage registration certificate noted on the mortgage agreement and declaration
(5) In the event that an autopsy of a property is conducted after the acceptance of the declaration of transfer or mortgage, as provided for in subsections (4) and (5) of article 13 respectively, the provisions of this article are applicable and if the findings from the autopsy are not available:
Provided that after carrying out the aforementioned autopsy, the Director may proceed with a new edition of the registration of such property and amend any registration made or certificate issued pursuant to the provisions of this article:
It is understood that no such new edition or amendment shall be carried out unless thirty days' notice is given by the Director to any person whose interests may be prejudiced as a result of such action. Any such person may, within the aforementioned period of thirty days from the date of delivery of such notification, register an objection with the Director, who investigates the whole issue and communicates the decision on the objection submitted to the person submitting the objection.

*(9/1965)*

## Simultaneous declarations of transfer and mortgage
17. Whenever a declaration of transfer of property and declaration of a mortgage on it is requested by the licensee in favor of the licensor or any other person in order to secure all or part of the consideration or for any other reason, the Director may allow the simultaneous execution of such declarations despite the competent Provincial Cadastre Offices in order to achieve the desired result through the enforcement of the condition upon which the transfer and mortgage were agreed (assuming that the provisions of article 15 will be observed, the two statements are considered to have been made and have legal effect from the time the transfer statement was made.

*(9/1965)*

## Real estate exchange
17A. When transfers of real estate are carried out to execute an agreement regarding the exchange of real estate between two persons and the real estate transferred under one person constitutes in whole or in part the consideration for the real estate transferred under the other person, such transfers will not be considered as occurring by exchange unless the two declarations of transfer are made simultaneously.

## Licensee and licensor declarations and attached documents
18.-(1) The written declarations to be submitted to the Provincial Land Office under the licensor and licensee of any property shall contain the following information:
(a) a description of the property intended to be transferred, with reference to its location, registration number and date, appraised value and portion or interest of the intended transferee

44D.-(1) In compliance with the provisions of subsection (3) of article 44C, two (2) appraisers are appointed, one on behalf of the mortgagee and one on behalf of the mortgagee to carry out, at the same time, independent appraisals of the mortgaged property for the purposes of calculating its market value, to whom they are assisted by the mortgagee and by the mortgagee lender and/or received from competent departments or agencies of the Republic, the same data and information regarding the mortgaged property:
It is understood that, instead of appointing an appraiser in accordance with the above, the mortgage debtor may request that the general appraisal value concerning a mortgaged property, as defined in the Real Estate (Possession, Registration and Appraisal) Law, is the estimated market value of the property in question:
It is further provided that the mortgagor and/or any interested person, as well as the mortgagor must cooperate and facilitate the appraiser in the performance of his work:
It is further provided that the two appraisers, when carrying out their independent assessments, also take into account the respective assessment of the competent Provincial Land Office.
(2) Subject to the provisions of subsection (1), the mortgagor serves a notice to the mortgagee, according to Form "IB" of the Second Schedule, which may be served either before, after or simultaneously with the notice according to Form "IA", stating in it that within ten (10) days from the service of the notice, he will proceed with the appointment of an appraiser:
It is provided that, the mortgagor informs the mortgagor of the identity of the appraiser he appointed before the end of the ten (10) day period, so that the mortgagor can appoint his appraiser:
It is further provided that, in the event that, for any reason, the mortgagee has not appointed an appraiser within the specified period, the mortgagee shall proceed with the appointment of two (2) appraisers to determine the market value of the mortgaged property:
Provided further that the parties shall pay the cost of services of the appraiser whom each appoints.
(3) Appraisers, subject to the provisions of subsection (1), draw up the valuation reports simultaneously, independently and without consulting each other and without communicating their valuation to each other or to any third party until they are formally delivered; the reports are delivered to the mortgagor and the mortgagee at the place and time specified in the notice referred to in subsection (2), but in no case after from the expiry of the period of thirty (30) days from the appointment of the appraiser on behalf of the mortgagee:
Provided that, in the event that any of the two (2) appraisers fails to deliver within the thirty (30) day period his appraisal report, then the mortgagee within ten (10) days from the date of the omission requests from the Cyprus Scientific and Technical Chamber the appointment of an independent appraiser, who proceeds to prepare an independent appraisal within thirty (30) days of his appointment, subject to the provisions of subsections (4), (5) and (6).
(4) In the event that the highest estimate based on the appraisal reports referred to in subsection (3), is less than the lowest estimate plus twenty-five percent (25%) thereof, then the average of the two (2) estimates is considered the market value and this market value is final.
(5) In the event that the highest estimate based on the appraisal reports referred to in subsection (3) is equal to or greater than the lowest estimate plus twenty-five percent (25%) thereof, the mortgage lender must, within five (5) days from the day of receipt of the appraisal reports, request from the Cyprus Scientific and Technical Chamber, the appointment of a third independent appraiser within ten (10) days, who, within a deadline thirty (30) days after its appointment prepares an independent appraisal of the mortgaged property and simultaneously delivers a true copy of its appraisal report to the mortgagee, the mortgagee and any other interested person.
(6) In the event of the appointment of a third independent appraiser, pursuant to the provisions of subsection (5), the market value of the mortgaged property shall be considered, taking into account the assessments made pursuant to the provisions of subsection (3), the average of the two closest assessments:
Provided that, in the event that the three (3) estimates differ equally, then the average of the three estimates is considered the market value.

## Preferential mortgage debtor status
44IAA.-(1) Regardless of the provisions of this Law and of any other Law in force or of any Regulations issued under the authority of the Law, in the event of the application of a procedure for the sale of a mortgaged property or mortgaged properties sold either separately or as a single set by a mortgage lender by auction, in accordance with the provisions of article 44G of this Part, which constitutes a principal residence and regardless if the intended sale is part of the first auction or the auction that may follow the first auction, in accordance with the provisions of subsection (1) of section 44H and provided that the mortgage debtor does not receive a government grant as a contribution to partially cover his debts, the mortgage debtor has preferential status over the last bidder who submitted the highest offer.
(2) Subject to the provisions of subsection (1), the auctioneer, after receiving the last oral offer from a bidder regarding the mortgaged property for sale and before declaring him as the last bidder, addresses first the mortgagee or his legal representative, who for the purposes of exercising the rights granted to them by the provisions of the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate (Amendment) (No. 7) Law of 2015 must be present at the auction sale proceeding and asks whether the mortgagor or his legal representative is willing and able to pay the price of the last submitted oral offer in a lump sum and/or in cash so that the mortgagor can replace the last bidder.
(3) In the event that the mortgage debtor or his legal representative declares to the auctioneer that he is able to pay the price, in accordance with the above, the auction is concluded in favor of the mortgage debtor and the last bidder is released:
