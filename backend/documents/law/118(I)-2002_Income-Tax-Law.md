## The Income Tax Law of 2002 (118(I)/2002)
For purposes of harmonization with the acts of the European Community entitled-
(a) "Council Directive 90/434/EEC of 23 July 1990 on the tax regime for mergers, divisions, contributions of assets and exchanges of shares concerning companies of Member States", (EE L 225 of 20/8/1990, p. 1-5),
(b) "Council Directive 90/435/EEC of 23 July 1990 on the common tax system for parent and subsidiary companies of different Member States", (OJ L 225 of 20/8/1990, p.6-9), and
(c) "90/436/EEC: Convention for the elimination of double taxation in case of correction of the profits of associated enterprises - Final act - Joint declarations - Unilateral declarations", (EEL 225 of 20/8/1990, p. 10-24),
The House of Representatives votes as follows:

## Short title
1. This Act shall be cited as the Income Tax Act of 2002.

*(118(I)/2002)*

## Interpretation
2. In this Law, unless the text states otherwise -
"market hybrid transfer" means a hybrid transfer entered into by a broker-dealer in the ordinary course of business and not as part of a structured arrangement;
"question in dispute" means a matter giving rise to disputes between Member States arising from the interpretation and application of agreements and conventions providing for the elimination of double taxation of income and, where applicable, of capital;
"restructuring" means the direct or indirect disposition and transfer of immovable property, as well as the transfer of rights pursuant to a deed of sale filed with the Department of Cadastre and Land Surveying, between one or more borrowers and/or debtors and/or guarantors regarding the same credit facility or grant or debt and one or more lenders, which is carried out within eleven (11) years from the date of entry into force of the Income Tax Act (Amendment) (No. 3) Law of 2015 and aims to reduce or repay credit facilities or grants or debts given to borrowers by one or more lenders:
Provided that the disposal and transfer of immovable property, as well as the transfer of rights pursuant to a deed of sale filed with the Department of Cadastre and Land Surveying, may be carried out to a lender and/or to a non-"connected person", as this term is interpreted in accordance with the provisions of article 33:
It is further provided that the disposal and transfer of immovable property, as well as the transfer of rights pursuant to a deed of sale filed with the Department of Cadastre and Land Surveying to a non-"connected person", excluding the lender, falls within this definition only in relation to the reduction or repayment of those credit facilities or loans or debts, arising in the context of a written agreement with the lender:
It is further provided that, the above provisions apply in relation to non-performing loans (NPLs) on or before December 31, 2015, as this term is interpreted by the European Banking Authority:
It is further provided that the disposal and transfer of immovable property to the Cyprus Asset Management Company Ltd with registration number HE 387704 or to its subsidiary company, which is carried out in the context of the "RENT AGAINST INSTALLMENT" plan, is also considered a restructuring and this provision applies in relation to non-performing credit facilities as of 31h December 2021 and on 31h December 2022, as defined in the “RENT AGAINST INSTALLMENT” plan;
"result of discrepancy" means the double deduction or the deduction without inclusion;
"discrepancy in the treatment of hybrid instruments" means the situation involving a company resident in the Republic or, in relation to the provisions of subsection (3) of section 11A, an entity, when-
(a) a payment under a financial instrument results in a discount without inclusion and-
(i) the payment is not includible in income within a reasonable period. and
(ii) the mismatch results from differences in the characterization of the instrument or the payment made thereunder;.
For the purposes of sub-clause (i), a payment under a financial instrument shall be deemed to be included in income within a reasonable period when—
(i) the payment is included in the payee's jurisdiction during the tax period beginning within twelve (12) months of the end of the payer's tax period. or
(ii) it is reasonable to expect that the payment will be included in the recipient's jurisdiction in a future tax period and the terms of the payment are those that would be expected to be agreed between arm's length businesses;
(b) a payment to a hybrid entity results in a non-excludable deduction and that mismatch results from differences in the allocation of payments to the hybrid entity under the laws of the jurisdiction where the hybrid entity is established or registered and the jurisdiction of any person with an interest in that hybrid entity;
(c) a payment to an entity with one or more permanent establishments gives rise to a non-excludable deduction and the resulting discrepancy is the result of differences in the allocation of payments between the head office and the permanent establishment or between two or more permanent establishments of the same entity under the law of the jurisdiction in which the entity operates;
(d) a payment gives rise to a non-excludable deduction as a result of a payment to a permanent establishment which is not taken into account;
(e) a payment from a hybrid entity gives rise to a non-excludable deduction and the mismatch results from the fact that the payment is not taken into account under the law of the recipient's jurisdiction;
(f) a deemed payment between the head office and the permanent establishment or between two or more permanent establishments gives rise to a deduction without inclusion and the inconsistency results from the fact that the payment is not taken into account under the law of the recipient's jurisdiction; or
(g) a double discount results.
For the purposes of this definition-
(i) a payment representing the underlying return on a transferred financial instrument does not create an inconsistency in the treatment of hybrid instruments under the provisions of paragraph (a) when the payment is made by a broker-dealer based on a market hybrid transfer, so long as the jurisdiction to which the payer is subject requires the broker to include as income all amounts received in respect of the transferee financial instrument;
(ii) a discrepancy in the treatment of hybrid instruments under the provisions of paragraphs (e), (f) or (g) arises only to the extent that the jurisdiction of the payer allows the deduction to be set off against an amount that is not double inclusion income;
(iii) the result of a mismatch is not considered a mismatch in the treatment of hybrid instruments, unless it arises between associated enterprises, between a company resident in the Republic and an associated enterprise, between the head office and the permanent establishment, between two or more permanent establishments of the same entity or through a structured arrangement.
"borrower" means a person who entered into an agreement with the lender "and includes a person whose credit facility was acquired in accordance with the provisions of the Purchase and Sale of Credit Facilities and Related Matters Law and was either taken over by or transferred to a public body or company that is licensed and who maintains or to whom or to which loans from a credit institution that are in arrears have been transferred and/or have become overdue under the technical standards of the European Banking Authority, which define the criteria for classifying a grant as non-performing and/or regulated as established by the European Commission through Executive Regulation 2015/227:
Provided that any related person of the first borrower is also considered a borrower, as the term "related person" is interpreted in accordance with the provisions of article 33 of the Income Tax Law, provided that the disposal and transfer of immovable property, as well as the transfer of rights pursuant to a sale deed filed with the Department of Land and Surveying, is carried out for the benefit of the lender;
"lender" means a licensed credit institution, as defined in article 2 of the Law on Operations of Credit Institutions, and includes the credit and financial institutions operating in the Republic pursuant to article 10A thereof, as well as their subsidiaries or a credit acquisition company, as defined, in accordance with the provisions of article 2 of the Law on Buying and Selling Credit Facilities and Related Matters, as well as its subsidiaries or a public body or company that is licensed and which holds or to which or to which have been transferred from a credit institution loans that are in arrears and/or have become overdue by virtue of the technical standards of the European Banking Authority, which determine the criteria for classifying a loan as non-performing and/or regulated as established by the European Commission through the Executive of Regulation 2015/227∙
"Republic" "includes the national territory, territory, territorial sea, as well as any other area outside the territorial sea, including the contiguous zone, exclusive economic zone and continental shelf, as defined in the Exclusive Economic Zone and Continental Shelf Law and the Territorial Sea Law, as well as any installation, structure and artificial island located in these zones in the exercise of the sovereign rights and jurisdiction of the Republic;
"Director" means the Director of the Internal Revenue Service
"low tax jurisdiction" means a third country jurisdiction in which the corporate tax rate is lower than fifty percent (50%) of the corporate rate as defined in Article 25;
"non-low tax jurisdiction" means a jurisdiction which is not a "low tax jurisdiction";
"double deduction" means the deduction of the same payment, expenses or damages in the jurisdiction from which the payment originates or the expenses are incurred or the damages arise (jurisdiction of the payer) and in another jurisdiction (jurisdiction of the investor), and in the case of payment by a hybrid entity or permanent establishment, the jurisdiction of the payer is the one in which the hybrid entity or permanent establishment is incorporated or established.
"double taxation" means the imposition of taxes by two (2) or more member states, which are covered by an agreement or convention providing for the elimination of double taxation of income and, where applicable, of capital, on the same taxable income or capital, when this entails-
(i) additional tax burden;
(ii) an increase in tax liabilities; or
(iii) cancellation or reduction of losses, which could be used to offset taxable profits;
"structured arrangement" means the arrangement relating to a mismatch in the treatment of hybrid instruments, where the mismatch effect is valued in the terms of the arrangement or the arrangement designed to produce a mismatch effect in the treatment of hybrid instruments, unless the resident company or an associated enterprise could not reasonably be expected to have been aware of the mismatch in the treatment of hybrid instruments and had no interest in the value the tax benefit of the mismatch in the treatment of hybrid instruments;
"investment income" means any income which is not earned or derived from any business, from salaried services, pensions or other annuities paid on account of or in respect of salaried services rendered;
"dual inclusion income" means any item of income, which is included under the laws of both jurisdictions where the inconsistency result has arisen;
"deduction" means the amount allowed as a deduction from taxable income under the laws of the jurisdiction of the payer or investor, and the term "allowed as a deduction" shall be construed accordingly;
"discount without inclusion" means the deduction of a payment or deemed payment between the head office and the permanent establishment or between two or more permanent establishments in any jurisdiction in which the payment or deemed payment is deemed to have been made (jurisdiction of the payer) without a corresponding inclusion for tax purposes of that payment or deemed payment in the jurisdiction of the recipient, the jurisdiction of the recipient being any jurisdiction in which the payment or deemed payment is collected or treated as collected under the laws of any other jurisdiction;
"controlled transactions" means transactions between related persons as defined in subsection (3) of section 33;
"consolidated group for accounting purposes" means a group of companies, which consists of all entities that are fully integrated in its consolidated financial statements, prepared on the basis of International Financial Reporting Standards or the national financial reporting system of a member state of the European Union;
"business" means a commercial or industrial undertaking, trade or craft, liberal or other profession and includes any other business in the nature of trading;
"company" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Companies Act and includes any body with or without legal personality, or body under public law, as well as any company, fraternity or other association of persons, acquired legal personality or not, including any comparatively similar company incorporated or registered outside the Republic and any company referred to in the First Schedule but not including a partnership;
"corporate tax" means the tax levied on the income of every company in accordance with the provisions of this Law;
"Commissioner" means the Commissioner of Income Tax who is charged, in accordance with the provisions of subsection (1) of section 3, with the implementation of this Law and includes any officer of the Internal Revenue Department duly authorized by the Commissioner;
"affected person" means any person, including a natural person, who is resident in a Member State for tax purposes and whose taxation is directly affected by a disputed matter;
"private motor vehicle" means a vehicle falling within sub-clause (vi) of paragraph (7) of Regulation 18 of the Motor Vehicles and Traffic Regulations 1984 to 2001;
"innovative enterprise" Deleted;
"Regulation (EU) No 651/2014" means the act of the European Union entitled "Commission Regulation (EU) No 651/2014 of 17 June 2014 declaring certain categories of aid compatible with the internal market pursuant to Articles 107 and 108 of the Treaty", as amended or replaced from time to time;
"Regulation (EU) 2020/852" means the act of the European Union entitled "Regulation (EU) 2020/852 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2020 establishing a framework for the facilitation of sustainable investment and amending Regulation (EU) 2019/2088", as amended or replaced from time to time;
"Regulation (EU) 2023/1114" means the act of the European Union entitled "Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 31 May 2023 on crypto markets and amending Regulations (EU) No. 1093/2010 and (EU) no. 1095/2010 and directives 2013/36/EU and (EU) 2019/1937", as it is amended or replaced from time to time;
"resident of the Republic"-
(a) in the case of a person means—
(i) a person who remains in the Republic for one or more periods exceeding in the aggregate one hundred and eighty-three (183) days in the tax year; or
(ii) a person who does not remain in another state for one or more periods that in the aggregate exceed one hundred and eighty-three (183) days within the same tax year, if he cumulatively meets the following:
(aa) remains in the Republic for at least sixty (60) days in the tax year; (bb) carries on any business in the Republic and/or is employed in the Republic and/or holds an office as a tax resident of the Republic at any time during the tax year: It is understood that, a person is not considered to meet the provisions of the subparagraph
(bb), if during that year the carrying on of any business in the Republic and/or the employment of the person in the Republic and/or the holding of an office as a person tax resident of the Republic is terminated;
(cc) maintains a permanent residence in the Republic which is owned or rented by the person.
For the purposes of calculating the days of residence in the Republic-
(a) the day of departure from the Republic is counted as a day outside the Republic;
(b) the day of arrival in the Republic counts as a day in the Republic;
(c) arrival in the Republic and departure from the Republic on the same day counts as one day in the Republic; and
(d) departure from the Republic and return to the Republic on the same day is counted as one day outside the Republic.
(b) in the case of a company means—
(i) a company whose control and management is exercised in the Republic; or
(ii) a company incorporated in the Republic pursuant to the Companies Law, unless an agreement to avoid double taxation stipulates otherwise:
Provided that, a company that has transferred its registered office or headquarters to the Republic is considered to have been established in the Republic.
The terms "non-resident" and "resident outside the Republic" shall be interpreted accordingly;
"guardian" when used in relation to a minor includes the minor's parent;
"borrowing costs" means costs of interest on any form of debt, other costs economically equivalent to interest and costs arising from raising finance, including payments under profit-sharing loans, imputed interest on products such as convertible bonds and zero-coupon bonds, loans under alternative financing arrangements such as Islamic finance, finance costs in financial leases, the capitalized interest recorded in the asset's balance sheet value or the depreciation of the capitalized interest, the cost calculated on the basis of the financial performance determined on the basis of the principle of commercial transactions, the notional interest on derivatives or hedging agreements in relation to the lending of the person, foreign exchange gains and losses on loans and products connected with the raising of finance, guarantee fees for financing transactions, settlement fees and related costs related to the borrowing of funds;
"Member State" means a Member State of the European Union;
"Base Dominion Area" means the British Dominion Area of the Akrotiri Base and the British Dominion Area of the Dekelia Base, as defined in Article 1 of the Treaty Establishing the Republic, signed at Nicosia on August 16, 1960;
"non-cooperative jurisdiction" means a jurisdiction included in the latest updated version of the list of third country jurisdictions assessed by the Member States, collectively, as non-cooperative for tax purposes as published in Annex I of the Official Journal of the European Union during the previous calendar year and also included in the last valid version of that list;
"Permanent establishment" means-
(a) fixed base of business through which the business of the business is wholly or partly carried on.
(b) The term 'permanent establishment' includes in particular:
(i) seat of administration;
(ii) branch office;
(iii) office;
(iv) factory;
(n) laboratory;
(vi) a mine, oil or gas well, quarry or any other place of extraction of natural gas resources;
(vii) offshore activities regarding the mining, exploration or exploitation of the continental shelf, subsoil or natural resources, as well as the installation and exploitation of pipelines and other facilities on the seabed.
(c) site or construction or installation work or supervisory activities in relation thereto constitute a permanent establishment only if they last more than three months.
(d) Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraphs (a), (b) and (c) hereof, the term 'permanent establishment' shall be deemed not to include:
(i) the use of facilities only for the purpose of storing, displaying or delivering goods or merchandise belonging to the business;
(ii) keeping stock of goods or merchandise belonging to the business solely for the purpose of storage, display or delivery;
(iii) keeping stock of goods or merchandise belonging to the enterprise only for the purpose of processing them by another enterprise;
(iv) maintaining a fixed base of business solely for the purpose of purchasing goods or merchandise or gathering information about the business;
(n) maintaining a fixed base of business solely for the purpose of carrying out, for the business, any other activity of a preparatory or ancillary nature;
(vi) the maintenance of a fixed base of the enterprise only for the purposes of any combination of the activities referred to in subparagraphs (i) to (n), provided that the total activity of the fixed base of the enterprise resulting from such combination is of a preparatory or auxiliary nature;
(e) Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraphs (a) and (b), where a person - other than an independent agent to whom paragraph (f) applies - acts on behalf of an enterprise and has, and usually exercises in the Republic, authority to enter into contracts in the name of the enterprise, that enterprise shall be deemed to have a permanent establishment in the Republic in relation to any activities which such person undertakes for the enterprise, unless the activities of that person are limited to those referred to in paragraph (d) which, if carried out through a fixed base of the enterprise, would not make it a permanent establishment under the provisions of this paragraph.
(f) An enterprise shall not be deemed to have a permanent establishment in the Republic merely because it carries on business in the Republic through a broker, general contractor with commission or any other independent agent, provided that such persons are acting in the ordinary course of their work.
(g) The fact that a company which is a resident of the Republic controls or is controlled by a company which is not a resident of the Republic, or conducts operations outside the Republic either through a permanent establishment or otherwise, cannot in itself make one of these companies a permanent establishment of the other;
(h) Regardless of the provisions of paragraphs (a) to (g), the investment of a person who is not a resident of the Republic in a mutual fund or cooperative operating pursuant to the provisions of the Open-Type Collective Investment Organizations Law or the Alternative Investment Organizations Law, is not considered to create a permanent establishment of the person in the Republic with respect to his specific investment:
It is understood that the management of an Organization for Collective Investment in Transferable Securities (hereinafter "UCITS") and/or an Alternative Investment Organization (hereinafter "UIE") established outside the Republic, by a person who is a resident of the Republic, is not deemed to create for said UCI and/or UIE the right of permanent establishment in the Republic.
"disregarded permanent establishment" means any arrangement which is treated as creating a permanent establishment under the law of the seat and is not treated as creating a permanent establishment under the law of the other jurisdiction;
"Directive 2017/1852" means the act of the European Union entitled "Directive (EU) 2017/1852 of the Council of 10her October 2017 on tax dispute resolution mechanisms in the European Union;
"Directive (EU) 2022/2523" means the act of the European Union entitled "Council Directive (EU) 2022/2523 of 14 December 2022 on ensuring a global minimum level of taxation of multinational enterprise groups and large domestic groups in the Union;
"organization under public law" means any legal person under public law or any other organization under public law established or established by law in the public interest regardless of whether such organization is considered an agent, employee of the state or holds the position of an employee of the state (in consimili casu to servant of the state);
"affordable rent" means the rent of a residential unit that is thirty percent (30%) below the average market rent, as calculated by the Department of Lands and Surveys and defined in the "Renovate - Rent" Plan of the Ministry of the Interior;
"person" includes a person and a company;
It is understood that in the case of an Alternative Investment Organization operating pursuant to the provisions of the Law on Alternative Investment Organizations or a Collective Investment Organization in Transferable Securities operating pursuant to the provisions of the Open Type Collective Investment Organizations Law, with more than one investment section, each investment section shall be considered as a separate person for the purposes of this Law.
"OECD model rules" means the rules on tax challenges arising from the digitalization of the economy - Global Model Rules against Tax Base Erosion (Second Pillar), as adopted on 14 December 2021 by the inclusive OECD / G20 BEPS framework;
"inclusion" means the amount taken into account in taxable income under the law of the recipient's jurisdiction, and a payment under a financial instrument is not treated as included, to the extent that the payment qualifies for any tax relief due solely to the way that payment is characterized by the law of the recipient's jurisdiction, and the term "included" shall be construed accordingly;
"Constitution" means the Constitution of the Republic of Cyprus;
"Plan" means the Plan for the Promotion of the Audiovisual Industry in the Republic of Cyprus approved by the Council of Ministers on 27h September 2017 with its decision number 1433/2017;
"restructuring price" means the price at which immovable property is transferred in the context of restructuring, and which is determined in a written agreement entered into in this context;
"securities" means shares, debentures, bonds, founders and other securities of companies or other legal entities, legally incorporated in the Republic or abroad and rights thereon;
"third country" means a jurisdiction which is not a member state of the European Union;
"hybrid transfer" means a financial instrument transfer arrangement where the underlying performance of the instrument is treated for tax purposes as arising simultaneously from more than one party to the arrangement;
"hybrid entity" means any entity or arrangement which is treated as a taxable entity under the law of one jurisdiction and whose income or expenditure is treated as income or expenditure of one or more other persons under the law of another jurisdiction;
"excess borrowing cost" means the amount by which the deductible borrowing cost of a company exceeds the tax revenue from interest and other economically equivalent revenue that is taxable based on the provisions of this Law, as calculated before the application of the provisions of subsection (16) of article 11;
"taxable income" means the balance of the sum of the incomes of any person from the sources specifically specified in section 5, which remains after deducting any sums permitted by this Act;
"tax relief" means the tax exemption, reduction in the rate of tax or any deduction or refund of tax, excluding tax credit for taxes withheld at source;
"tax year" means the period of twelve months beginning on the first day of January in each year;
"tax" means the tax levied under this Act;
"broker" means a person or entity who professionally buys and sells financial instruments on a regular basis, on his own account, for profit;
"financial institution" means -
(a) a credit institution, as defined in point 27) of paragraph 1 of Article 4 of Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 on markets in financial instruments and the amendment of Directive 2002/92/EC and Directive 2011/61/EU or an investment firm, as defined in point 1) of paragraph 1 of Article 4 of Directive 2014/65/EU or an alternative investment fund manager (AIFM), as defined in point b) of paragraph 1 of Article 4 of Directive 2011/61/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council or a collective investment undertaking management company (UCITS), as defined in point b) of paragraph 1 of Article 2 of the Directive 2009/65/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council,
(b) insurance company, as defined in paragraph 1) of Article 13 of Directive 2009/138/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council,
(c) reinsurance business, as defined in paragraph 4) of Article 13 of Directive 2009/138/EC,
(d) occupational pension benefits organization, which falls within the scope of Official implementation of Directive 2003/41/EC of the European Parliament and the Council's Journal or a representative of an occupational pension benefits organization, as referred to in paragraph 1 of Article 19 of the said Directive,
(e) pension institutions that manage pension schemes, which are considered social security systems and fall under Regulation (EC) Gazette no. 883/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council and Regulation (EC) no. 987/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council, as well as any legal entity established for the purpose of investing in such programs,
(f) alternative investment organization (AIF) managed by an AIFM, as defined in point b) of paragraph 1 of Article 4 of Directive 2011/61/EU or an AIF established and operating on the basis of the provisions of the Alternative Investment Organizations Law,
(g) UCITS, within the meaning of paragraph 2 of Article 1 of Directive 2009/65/EC,
(h) central counterparty, as defined in paragraph 1) of Article 2 of Regulation (EU) no. 648/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council,
(i) central securities depository, as defined in item 1) of paragraph 1 of Article 2 of Regulation (EU) no. 909/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council.
"financial instrument" means any instrument to the extent that it creates a return on financing or a return on equity that is taxable in accordance with the rules applicable to the taxation of debt, capital or derivatives under the law of the jurisdiction of either the payer or the payer and includes a hybrid transfer.

## Commissioner of Income Tax
3. (1) For the proper and faithful implementation of this Law, the Director will be the Commissioner of Income Tax, who must take any action he deems necessary and appropriate for the implementation of the provisions of this Law.
(2) The exercise of powers and responsibilities referred to in the application of this Law may be delegated by the Commissioner to another or other Officers of the Internal Revenue Department.

*(118(I)/2002)*

## Official confidentiality
4. (1) Every person who exercises duties or is in the service of the implementation of this Law is obliged to consider and keep as secret and confidential every element that comes to his knowledge, every tax return as well as every document or tax list that refers to the income of any person, and shall sign the declaration determined thereon before a District Judge.
(2) A person who becomes aware of an element or has under his control or who may be informed of the content of a document, tax return or tax list that refers to the income of any other person, is obliged to refrain at all times from any transmission or communication or attempt to transmit or communicate their content except in cases where the transmission or communication is made for the purposes of this Law:
It is understood that the Minister of Finance may, in the public interest, authorize the transmission or communication of said information, or the content of such documents, tax returns and lists to a person or persons that he specifically wanted to determine.
(2A) Regardless of the provisions of subsection (2), the Tax Council established pursuant to article 4A of the Tax Certification and Collection Law, may publish the decisions it issues pursuant to the provisions of subsection (5) of article 20A of said law.

## Object of the tax
5. (1) Subject to the provisions of this Law, in the case of a person who is a resident of the Republic, for each tax year a tax is imposed at a rate or rates, as specifically determined in this Law on the income acquired or arising from sources both inside and outside the Republic, with respect to:-
(a) any profits or other benefits from any business for any period of time such business whether carried on;
(b) any income, profits or other benefits, pecuniary or otherwise, provided or granted in respect of any office or salaried services to the person who holds the office or provides the salaried services or to any member of his family, including-
(i) the estimated annual value of accommodation, shelter, house or maintenance;
(ii) any allowance;
(iii) a benefit provided as an inducement to accept employment or appointment to office, including such benefit provided prior to commencement of employment or appointment to office;
(iv) gratuity given gratuitously on retirement or early retirement or termination or early termination of a contract of employment or appointment to office;
(v) a benefit granted under an Employee Early Retirement Plan;
(vi) compensation for termination of employment or appointment to an office, the payment of which is not provided for in a contract of employment or appointment to an office or in a collective agreement or regulations or in any other terms governing the employment or appointment to an office;
(vii) any other benefit granted under a contract of employment or appointment to an office or under a collective agreement or regulations or any other terms governing the employment or appointment to an office;
Provided that amounts receivable, including compensation, which are paid in accordance with a court decision and which relate to any of the sub-paragraphs (i) to (vii) are subject to the tax within the relevant sub-paragraph and are taxed accordingly.
(c) dividends, interest or discounts;
(d) any pension, sums of income payable pursuant to a court order or pursuant to a condition entered into in a will or contract, and annual lifetime or fixed term annuities;
(e) any rents, copyright or patent rights, fees or other profits arising from property, including the value of the benefit which the owner of land acquires by reason of the construction thereon, at the tenant's expense, of any building, or of any addition or alteration, or both, made, at the tenant's expense, to any building, building, or works, when such construction or addition or alteration shall fall under ownership to the owner at the termination of the rental, of the value of this benefit determined by the Commissioner in the year of construction, addition or modification, granted, taking into account the proportions, the deductions provided for under article 10 and taking into account all the circumstances of the case:
Provided that the value of this benefit-
(i) shall not be deemed to exceed the cost of the erection, addition or alteration;
(ii) shall be apportioned in a uniform manner by the Commissioner during the period of the tenancy; but in the event that before the expiry of that period the owner wishes to alienate the land or the building or the building or the works, the untaxed balance of such benefit on the date of the alienation of the land or the building or the building or the works shall be deemed to be the income of the year in which the land or the building or the works building or the building or works were disposed of by the landlord, or that year and the preceding five years, or the actual years of the tenancy, whichever is the lesser number of years;
(f) any amount or consideration for goodwill less any amount expended to acquire such goodwill;
(g) whenever a company grants a loan or any other financial facility including cash withdrawals, excluding balances derived from commercial transactions, to directors or direct or indirect shareholders of individuals or their spouses or to their relatives up to the second degree of consanguinity, then it is considered that such person has a monthly benefit equal to nine percent (9%) per annum on the balance of loan or any other monthly financial facility at the end of each month (including cash withdrawal during the month):
Provided that the calculated tax on the monthly benefit is ascertained and paid in accordance with the Withholding Tax from Salary Regulations:

## Deductible costs for investment in an innovative small and medium-sized enterprise (SME)

Of course that
(i) The amount deducted from the taxable income may not exceed 50% of the taxable income of that person in the tax year in which the venture capital investment is made as determined in accordance with the provisions of this Act before deducting from it the deduction specified in this section and after deducting from it the deduction under section 14 of this Act;
(ii) in the case of a legal person investor, who is an independent investor, thirty percent (30%) of the expenses are deducted either directly, or through an investment fund, or through an alternative trading platform and only for equity investments;
(iii) the deduction, to the extent not allowed by reason of the limitation in sub-paragraph (i) may be carried forward and allowed during the next five years, subject to the above percentage limitation;
(iv) the amount deducted cannot exceed in total one hundred and fifty thousand euros in the tax year;
(v) in the case of follow-on investments, the conditions of paragraph 4 of article 21 of Regulation (EU) no. 651/2014;
(vi) The Commissioner may disallow the granting of any discount under the provisions of this subsection, if a person who incurs expenses for a venture capital investment under this subsection, does not retain possession of said investment in the innovative small and medium-sized enterprise (SME) for a minimum period of three years.

(viii) financial intermediaries and their administrators may benefit from the tax deduction only if they act as co-investors or joint lenders, provided that no tax deduction is granted in respect of the services provided by the financial intermediary or its administrators for the implementation of the measure, as specified in paragraph 90 of the Guidelines for Enterprise Risk Financiers (ERF);
(ix) the discount may not be used to support acquisitions of an eligible business, nor to support export activities to third countries or other member states nor may it be used by an eligible business to import goods, as specified in paragraphs 27 to 29 of the CGIEC;
(x) in a case where an independent investor provides direct business risk financing to an innovative small and medium-sized enterprise (SME), only the acquisition of newly issued ordinary shares (ordinary shares) is considered an eligible investment and these shares should be kept in the possession of the investor for at least three (3) years, while in the case of replacement capital, the conditions of paragraph 7 of Article 21 of Regulation (EU) no. 651/2014;
(xi) regardless of the other provisions of this Law, in the event that an independent investor, who is a natural person, provides business risk financing directly to an innovative small and medium-sized enterprise (SME), sufficient participation of the independent investor is ensured, in accordance with paragraph 5 of Article 21a of Regulation (EU) no. 651/2014:
Provided that, the total maximum tax exemption does not exceed the following upper limits:
(a) fifty percent (50%) of the eligible investment made by an independent investor, who is a natural person, to the innovative small and medium-sized enterprise (SME), as provided for in subparagraph (i) of paragraph (b) of the definition of the term “innovative small and medium-sized enterprise (SME)”;

(iv) the tax credit can be used against the total tax arising for the tax year 2020ˑ
(v) any refundable tax resulting from such credit may not exceed the amount of tax paid.

## Concept of the term reorganization
30. For the purposes of this Part, "reorganization" means a merger, partial demerger, transfer of assets, exchange of shares and transfer of registered office, between companies domiciled in the Republic and or companies not domiciled in the Republic and -
(a) 'merger', means an act by which—
(i) one or more companies, upon their dissolution without liquidation, transfer all assets and liabilities to another pre-existing company in return for the grant to their shareholders of shares in the capital of the other company and, possibly, in return for the payment of an amount in cash which does not exceed ten percent (10%) of the nominal value of the shares, or, in the absence of a nominal value, of the accounting value of these shares,
(ii) two or more companies transfer, upon their dissolution without liquidation, all of their assets and liabilities to a new company that they establish, against granting to their shareholders, shares in the capital of the new company and, possibly, against the payment of an amount in cash which does not exceed ten percent (10%) of the nominal value of the shares, or, in the absence of a nominal value, of the accounting value of these shares,
(iii) a company, on its dissolution without liquidation, transfers all its assets and liabilities to the company which holds all the shares in its capital;
(b) 'demerger' means an act by which a company, on its dissolution without liquidation, transfers the whole of its assets and liabilities, to two or more pre-existing or new companies, in return for a proportionate grant of shares to its shareholders, shares in the capital of the liquidating companies, and, where applicable, for the payment of a sum in cash not exceeding ten percent (10%) of the nominal value of the shares, or, failing that nominal value, the book value of these shares;
(b1) 'partial demerger': means an act by which a company, without being dissolved, transfers one or more lines of business to one or more pre-existing or new companies, leaving at least one line of business to the transferor company, in return for a proportionate concession to its shareholders of debentures of the share capital of the companies receiving the assets and liabilities and, where applicable, in return for a cash payment not exceeding ten per cent. (10%) of the nominal value of such shares or, in the absence of a nominal value, of the book value of such shares;
(c) 'transfer of assets' means an act by which a company, without its dissolution without liquidation, transfers the whole or one or more branches of its business to another company in return for an exchange of shares in the capital of the transferee company;

It is further provided that the distribution of the income of the controlled foreign companies is calculated in accordance with the principle of commercial transactions as defined in this Law and may not exceed the amount of the undistributed income of the controlled foreign company as defined in subsection (3), which has arisen through assets and risks associated with the duties of the important executives performed by the controlling company resident in the Republic.
(6) The undistributed income or loss, as the case may be, is included in the tax year of the resident company of the Republic, during which the tax year of the controlled foreign company in which the income or loss arose ends.
(7) In the event that a controlled foreign company distributes profits to the company resident in the Republic, which are included in the taxable income of the company resident in the Republic and are not exempt from tax pursuant to article 8, the amounts of income that were included in taxable income in a previous tax year, in accordance with subsections (2) to (4), regardless of whether in the said tax year they were exempt from tax pursuant to article 8, are deducted from taxable income when calculating the tax due on distributed profits in the current year, in order to ensure that no double taxation arises:
It is provided that, in case of collection of profits derived from amounts of the income of a controlled foreign company that were included in the taxable income of the resident company of the Republic in a previous tax year, in accordance with subsections (2) to (4), regardless of whether in the year in question they were exempted from tax pursuant to article 8, said profits are not considered a dividend for the purposes of the Extraordinary Contribution for the Defense of the Republic Law.
(8) In the case of a disposal by a company resident in the Republic of its participation in a controlled foreign company or of the business it carries on through a permanent establishment it maintains outside the Republic, any part of the proceeds of disposal which was included in the taxable income in a previous tax year, in accordance with subsections (2) to (4), regardless of whether in that year it was exempted from tax pursuant to article 8, is deducted from the taxable income in the calculation of of tax due on the disposal product in the current year in order to ensure that no double taxation arises.
(9) The foreign tax paid on the income of a controlled foreign company, which is added to the taxable income of a company resident in the Republic pursuant to the provisions of this article, is granted as a deduction from the tax payable pursuant to the provisions of this Law, in accordance with the provisions of article 35 and subsections (1) and (2) of article 36.

## Appeal submission and review process
36C.-(1) The objection, provided for by the provisions of article 36B, shall be submitted within three (3) years from the date of receipt of the first notification of the measure which raises or will raise a questionable issue, regardless of whether the affected person has made use of the means of treatment provided for by the national law of any of the member states concerned.
(2) The affected person submits the objection, with the same information at the same time, to all the competent authorities of the Member States concerned and designates therein the other Member States concerned by the case.
(3) The Tax Commissioner accepts objections in Greek or English.
(4) The Tax Commissioner certifies the affected person and the competent authorities of the other interested member states of receipt of the objection within two (2) months from the date of its receipt and informs the competent authorities of the language or languages he intends to use for communication purposes during the procedure.
(5)(a) The Tax Commissioner decides on the acceptance or rejection of an objection within a period of six (6) months from the date of receipt or within a period of six (6) months from the date of receipt of any specific additional information he requests, which is considered necessary for the substantive examination of the specific case, whichever is later, and promptly informs the affected persons and the competent authorities of the other member states regarding with his decision.
(b) The Tax Commissioner may decide within a period of six (6) months from the date of receipt of the objection or within a period of six (6) months from the date of receipt of any specific additional information requested, which is considered necessary for the substantive examination of the specific case, whichever is later, to resolve the contested issue unilaterally, without the participation of the other competent authorities of the Member States concerned and informs the affected person and the competent authorities of the other Member States concerned regarding its decision.
(6) In the event that the Tax Commissioner does not rule on the objection within the period provided for in subsection (5), the objection is deemed to have been accepted by the Tax Commissioner.
(7) The Tax Commissioner, with a notice published in the Official Gazette of the Republic, determines the form for submitting an objection provided for by the provisions of this Part.
(8) Regulations issued by the Council of Ministers may provide for the better implementation of the provisions of this article and in particular for the following:
(a) The determination of the procedure for submitting and examining an objection and making a decision thereon;
(b) the amicable settlement process;
(c) the resolution of disputes by an advisory committee;
(d) the appointment of an advisory committee and its independent personalities, as these conditions are provided for in Directive 2017/1852, as well as its operating rules;
(e) the process of drawing up a list of independent personalities;
(f) the determination of an alternative dispute resolution committee, as this term is provided for in Directive 2017/1852, as well as its operation;
(g) the allocation of costs incurred in the proceedings;
(h) the procedure for providing information and evidence by an affected person and the hearing procedure;
(i) the procedure for providing an opinion by an advisory committee or an alternative dispute resolution committee;
(j) the imposition of sanctions in the event of a breach of confidentiality;
(k) the establishment of procedures for the removal from the list of independent personalities of any appointed person, in the event that the person ceases to be independent.
(9) The Tax Commissioner may determine, by means of a notification published in the Official Gazette of the Republic, any issue related to the procedures for the implementation of the Regulations provided for in subsection (8).
