## The Real Estate Agents Law of 2010 (71(I)/2010)
The House of Representatives votes as follows:

## Short title
1. This Law shall be referred to as the Real Estate Agents Law of 2010.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Interpretation
2. In this Law, unless the text states otherwise-
"licensed real estate agent" means a registered real estate agent, who at the material time holds a valid license issued pursuant to article 17 and includes a person who legally practices the profession of real estate agent in another member state and provides services to the Republic in accordance with the provisions of this Law;
"property" has the meaning assigned to the term "immovable property" by section 2 of the Immovable Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Act;
"competent Provincial Land Office" in relation to matters relating to immovable property, means the Provincial Land Office of the Province in which the particular property is situated;
"practicing estate agent" [Deleted];
"assistant real estate agent" means a natural person who is employed as an employee of a licensed real estate agent, is registered in the Register of Assistant Real Estate Agents pursuant to the provisions of this Law and carries out real estate work under the instructions of a licensed real estate agent;
"Republic" means the Republic of Cyprus;
"Court" means a competent District Court;
"registered estate agent" means an estate agent, who at the material time is registered in the Register of Estate Agents pursuant to the provisions of this Law;
"member state" means a member state of the European Union and includes the states which are parties to the Agreement on the European Economic Area and Switzerland;
"real estate transaction" means any transaction for the conclusion of an agreement for the sale, purchase, exchange or lease of immovable property for more than one month, including the granting of immovable property for consideration and includes any transaction for the conclusion of an agreement for the sale, purchase, exchange of immovable property through the transfer of corporate shares;
"real estate agent" means the natural or legal person whose employment is the mediation of a real estate transaction for a fee;
"Register of Real Estate Agents" means the Register of Real Estate Agents maintained pursuant to this Law;
"Register of Assistant Estate Agents" means the Register of Assistant Estate Agents maintained pursuant to this Law;
"Chairman" means the Chairman of the Council;
"Board" means the Board of Registration of Estate Agents established pursuant to Article 3;
"Agreement on the European Economic Area" means the Agreement on the European Economic Area signed on 2 May 1992 at Oporto, as adapted by the Protocol signed at Brussels on 17 May 1993 and as such Agreement is further amended from time to time;
"Minister" means the Minister for the Interior.

## Establishment and formation of Council
3.-(1) A Council is established with the name "Council of Registration of Real Estate Agents" and headquartered in Nicosia, which will have competence and authority-
(a) to carry out the registration of real estate agents and assistant real estate agents and to issue the relevant certificates or licenses in accordance with the provisions of this Law;
(b) to maintain the Register of Real Estate Agents and to remove registered real estate agents from it, in compliance with the provisions of this Law;
(c) maintain a Register of Assistant Estate Agents;
(d) exercise disciplinary control over registered real estate agents and assistant real estate agents and impose the disciplinary sanctions specified by this Law;
(e) respecting the competences and powers of the Attorney General of the Republic and the Police provided by the Constitution and the laws of the Republic, to register indictments in the Court regarding the criminal offenses provided for in this Law;
(f) to exercise any other authority or authority which is instrumental or inherent in the fulfillment of the duties assigned to it.
(2) The Council is a legal entity under public law with perpetual succession and has all the legal properties that characterize legal entities, has its own name and seal and has the ability, for the purposes of fulfilling its powers, to sue and be sued, acquire ownership, lease, sell or otherwise dispose of or encumber any movable or immovable property.
(3) The Council is constituted by the President and six other members, as follows:
(a) The President shall be elected by licensed real estate agents by election procedures conducted simultaneously in all provinces in which provincial commissions of real estate agents operate and which shall be convened for this purpose by the Council at least forty-five (45) days before the expiration of the term of office of the outgoing elected members.
(b) Members of the Council are -
(i) the Director General of the Home Office or his representative;
(ii) the Director of the Department of Town Planning and Housing or his representative;
(iii) the Director of the Department of Cadastre and Land Surveying or his representative; and
(iv) three licensed real estate agents elected by licensed real estate agents by election procedures held simultaneously in all provinces in which provincial commissions of real estate agents operate and which are convened for that purpose by the Council at least forty-five (45) days before the expiration of the term of office of the outgoing elected members.
(4) The term of office of the Council is three years:
It is provided that the President of the Council, who will take office after the effective date of the Real Estate Agents (Amendment) Law of 2017 and the end of the term of office of the person who is President on the effective date of said Law, will be elected at a meeting of all licensed real estate agents, which will be convened for this purpose by the Council at least forty-five (45) days before the end of the term term of office of the outgoing President, and will be elected for a term of less than three (3) years, so that it expires on the same date as the term of office of the elected members of the existing Council, on the date of entry into force of said Law, expires:
It is further provided that the President or any of the elected members of the Council may, at any time before the end of his term, submit his resignation in writing to the Minister, in the case of the President, or to the President, in the case of any elected member.
(4A) No person may serve as President of the Council, as long as he holds a position in any organization or association or association dealing with issues related to the exercise of the profession of real estate agent:
It is understood that the provisions of this subsection do not apply in relation to the person who is the Chairman of the Council and was appointed before the effective date of the Real Estate Agents (Amendment) Law of 2017.
(5) No liability shall be attributed to any member or official or employee of the Council for any act or decision of said member, official or employee made or taken in good faith in the exercise by him of the powers, responsibilities or duties provided for by this Law.

## Vacancy in the Council
4.-(1) In the event that for any reason the position of the President is vacated, the vacated position shall be filled as soon as possible, in compliance with the provisions of paragraph (a) of subsection (3) of article 3, for the remaining period of the incumbent's term.
(2) In the event that for any reason the position of any of the elected members of the Council is vacated, the vacated position shall be filled for the remaining term of office by the first runner-up during the last election pursuant to subparagraph (iv) of paragraph (b) of subsection (3) of article 3 of a convened meeting.
(3) The Council meets lawfully despite the existence of any vacancy, except that of the President, as long as the number of remaining members is not less than the required quorum for its meetings.

## Council Meetings
5.-(1) The meetings of the Council are convened by the President at a time and place that he wanted to choose in consultation with the other members:
Provided that the President calls a meeting of the Council whenever at least two members request it in writing.
(2) Meetings of the Council shall be presided over by the President, or in his absence or incapacity, by another member elected by the members present to preside over the meeting.
(3) Minutes of the proceedings are kept during the meetings. The minutes are approved by the Council at its next meeting, signed by the President, or as the case may be, the presiding officer and as soon as they are signed they are admissible in any judicial or other proceedings as evidence without any further proof.
(4) As for the rest, the Council regulates with internal regulations its internal procedure, including the convening and holding of meetings and the observance of minutes.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Quorum and decision-making
6.-(1) At least four present members of the Council constitute a quorum at any meeting.
(2) A duly convened meeting of the Council at which a quorum is present is competent to transact any business of the Council and has and may exercise any power, authority or duty or function conferred on the Council by this Act.
(3) The decisions of the Council are taken by a majority, as expressed by a show of hands of those present at the members' session and, in case of a tie, the President or, as the case may be, the presiding member has a second or winning vote. Abstention from voting is considered a negative vote.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Compensation of members
7. The President and the members of the Council receive for the performance of their duties the compensation and/or allowance approved by the Council of Ministers.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Establishment of committees or subcommittees
8.-(1) The Council has the power to set up from its members or from other persons such committees or sub-committees, either of a general or special nature, as it may deem necessary for the study and submission to it of proposals or reports on matters it wishes to refer to them.
(2) The Council shall determine the composition and procedure of such committees or sub-committees, as well as the terms of reference and the amount of possible compensation of their members, but in no case may this determination be inconsistent or contrary to the purposes of this Law.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Council offices and employees
9.-(1) The Council has an office and service, which are necessary for the fulfillment of the powers and duties assigned to it.
(2) Subject to the provisions of any regulations issued pursuant to this Law, the Council has the power to appoint the necessary staff for the performance of its duties and exercises administrative and disciplinary control over them.
(3) The Council, with the approval of the Minister, determines the structure of its services and the number of positions, draws up service plans for the positions and determines the salary and salary scale of each position.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Maintaining the Register of Real Estate Agents and the Register of Assistant Real Estate Agents
Register of Real Estate Agents and Register of Practicing Real Estate Agents.
10.-(1) The Council shall keep a register, called the "Register of Real Estate Agents", in which, upon submission of a relevant application, the name, address and such other information, as the Council may from time to time deem necessary, shall be entered in relation to each person who is entitled to be registered as a real estate agent pursuant to this Law.
(2) The Council maintains a register, called the "Register of Assistant Real Estate Agents", in which, upon submission of a relevant application, the name, address and such other information, as the Council may from time to time deem necessary, is entered in relation to every person employed by a licensed real estate agent as an assistant real estate agent.
(3) The entries made in the above Registers can be proven with certified copies, in the manner the Council wanted to decide, and these copies will constitute acceptable testimony in any judicial or other procedure.
(4) The Council annually issues and distributes, for a fee it wishes to determine, certified copies of the Registers mentioned above, in which the names of the registered persons are written in alphabetical order as well as such other information as the Council may deem necessary.

## Qualifications for registration in the Register of Estate Agents
11.-(1) Subject to the provisions of article 15, it may be registered in the Register of Real Estate Agents -
(a) a natural person, who-
(i) is a citizen of the Republic or any other Member State;
(ii) is not bankrupt or under any other legal incapacity by virtue of law or court order;
(iii) has not been convicted of an offense involving dishonesty or moral turpitude or, if convicted, has been rehabilitated under the provisions of the Rehabilitation of Convicts Act;
(iv) in compliance with the provisions of the Law on the Recognition of Professional Qualifications, has obtained a recognized diploma attesting to the successful completion of post-secondary education of at least three years or an equivalent duration under a partial attendance regime, at a university or institution of higher education or another institution of the same educational level in matters related to the exercise of the profession of real estate agent and has completed in the Republic a professional practice of real estate agent of at least 12 months, being registered in the Register of Assistant Realtors:
It is provided that the holder of a master's degree or title obtained after studies lasting at least one academic year in the subjects referred to in this sub-paragraph is considered to possess the academic qualifications required by this sub-paragraph;
(v) in compliance with the provisions of the Law on the Recognition of Professional Qualifications, possesses sufficient knowledge regarding real estate and urban planning legislation of the Republic necessary for the correct and responsible exercise of the profession of real estate agent:
Provided that, for the purposes of establishing the qualification of this subsection, the Council must submit the persons applying for registration to written examinations.
(b) a legal entity, i.e. a limited liability company, general or limited partnership, if -
(i) is established under the laws of the Republic or any other member state;
(ii) has a registered office or place of business in Cyprus;
(iii) is not in liquidation or dissolution.
(2) The registration of a legal person in the Register of Real Estate Agents pursuant to subsection (1) shall be a separate and independent registration and shall be carried out in parallel with the registration of any natural person related to a legal person.
(3) Regardless of the provisions of subsection (1), a person who is not a citizen of the Republic or of any other member state, who is the spouse or child of a citizen of the Republic or of another member state and has his or her habitual residence in the Republic, may be registered in the Register, provided that he/she meets all the other conditions and requirements of this Law.

## Qualifications for registration on the Register of Assistant Estate Agents
12.-(1) A natural person may be registered in the Register of Assistant Realtors, who-
(iv) Holds a high school diploma or its equivalent, obtained in the Republic or in another member state or in a third country:
Provided that, in the event that the high school diploma or its equivalent was obtained in a third country, for the purposes of registration in the Register of Assistant Realtors, the interested person is required to present a relevant certificate issued by the Ministry of Education and Culture of the Republic or the competent authority of the third country, which certifies that the relevant high school of the third country is recognized in that country.
(v) is employed in the office of a licensed real estate agent.
(2) An application for the registration of an assistant real estate agent should be submitted no later than 30 days from the date of commencement of employment, otherwise the training period shall be deemed to have begun thirty days prior to the submission of an application for registration pursuant to this article, and this date shall be entered in the relevant certificate as the date of commencement of training.

## Submission and review of applications
13.-(1) (a) Any interested party who wishes to be registered in the Registry as a real estate agent or assistant real estate agent shall submit to the Council a written application in the form determined by the Council, accompanied by the necessary evidence of possession of the qualifications required for registration:
Provided that, notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, possession of the qualification referred to in sub-clause (iii) of paragraph (a) of sub-section (1) of section 11 shall be proved only by original documents.
(b) in the event that the applicant does not submit sufficient evidence of the possession of all the qualifications necessary for the registration of the qualification, the Council may, by notice in writing to the applicant, require him to provide in writing to the Council, within such reasonable time as may be specified in the notice, the necessary evidence and information to enable the Council to consider the application.
(2) By submitting the application, the applicant pays the specified fee for the examination of his application, which regardless of the fate of the application, is non-refundable.
(3) The Council examines and decides on the application for registration and responds accordingly to the applicant as soon as possible, providing him at the same time, in case of rejection, the specific reasons for the rejection.
(4) In the event of the applicant's failure to provide the Council, within the deadline set, with any further data or information, the Council may reject the application for this reason as well.
(5) Upon registration in the relevant Register, the Council issues to the applicant a certificate of registration, which constitutes prima facie proof that the person named therein has been registered in the relevant Register in accordance with the provisions of this Law.
(6) In the event of any deletion from the Register or revocation of the registration pursuant to article 14 or 16, the holder of the certificate has the obligation to return the relevant registration certificate to the Council within a period of 15 days from the date of notification of the relevant decision.
(7) A holder of a registration certificate who fails, without any reasonable cause, to comply within the time limit with his obligation under subsection (6), is guilty of an offense and, in the event of conviction, is liable to a fine not exceeding one hundred euros for each day or part of a day during which the failure to comply continued before the conviction or continues after the conviction.

## Delete, revoke or correct a registration or re-register
14.-(1) Subject to the provisions of this Law, the Council has the power to decide, as the case may be, the deletion from the relevant Register, the revocation or correction of the registration of any person who-
(a) requests in writing the deletion of his name from the relevant Register, or
(b) has been entered in the relevant Register incorrectly or as a result of fraudulent action or false or inaccurate declarations or representations either by the same or a third person, or
(c) lost any capacity, qualification or condition, the possession of which is necessary for his registration or remaining in the relevant Register, or
(d) failed within a period of six months from the date of his registration in the relevant Register to apply to the Council for the issuance of an annual license pursuant to article 16 or failed to renew it within three months from the date of its expiry, unless he notifies the Council in writing, within the above period, that he intends not to renew his annual license for the current year.
(2) Before deciding to delete, revoke or correct any entry in the relevant Register for any of the reasons referred to in paragraphs (b), (c) or (d) of subsection (1), the Council must give written notice of its intention to do so to the affected person and give him the opportunity to submit in writing within a reasonable time all the relevant objections or representations that he wishes to submit to the Council or in person or through a lawyer.
(3) Irrespective of the provisions of subsections (1) and (2), the name of any person is deemed to be automatically deleted from the relevant Register at the same time as-
(a) the issuance by a Court of a decree of permanent hospitalization pursuant to the provisions of the Law on Psychiatric Hospitalization,
(b) his death, or
(c) failing to renew his annual license for a period of three consecutive years, whether or not notice has been given under paragraph (d) of subsection (1), or
(d) his conviction for a disciplinary offense pursuant to this Law and the imposition of the disciplinary penalty of removal from the relevant Register.
(4) When, pursuant to the provisions of this article, the name of any person is duly deleted from the relevant Register, the Council may decide that the person in question is not entitled to request his re-registration under this Law for such a period as the Council, on the occasion of the deletion, wanted to determine.
(5) Irrespective of the provisions of this article, the Council may at any time upon application of the person concerned, decide to re-register the name of that person in the relevant Register, free of charge or upon payment of the fee determined by the Council, provided that it establishes again that the applicant meets the qualifications for registration pursuant to this Law:
Provided that in the case of an application for re-registration in the Register of Real Estate Agents submitted within five years from the date of deletion, the Council may not require the re-registration applicant to pass written exams.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Temporary and occasional provision of services in the Republic by persons established in another member state
15.-(1) The provisions of this article apply only in the event that a natural or legal person moves to the territory of the Republic on a temporary and occasional basis in order to practice the profession of real estate agent:
Provided that the Council may assess the temporary and occasional nature of the services provided on a case-by-case basis and in relation to the duration, frequency, periodicity and continuous nature of the specific provision of services.
(2) A natural or legal person who moves to the territory of the Republic on a temporary and occasional basis in order to practice the profession of real estate agent may provide real estate agent services in the Republic without requiring registration in the Register, provided that-
(a) is a citizen of a Member State or, in the case of a legal person, is incorporated under the legislation of a Member State;
(b) is legally established in a member state other than the Republic and, in the case of a legal entity, does not have a place of work within the Republic;
(c) satisfy the Council that he is legally practicing the activities of real estate agent in the Member State of his establishment or, in case the profession of real estate agent is not legislated in the Member State of his establishment, satisfy the Council that he has practiced the profession of real estate agent in the Member State of establishment full-time for at least two years in the period of the previous ten years.
(3) Upon the first movement of the person referred to in the previous paragraphs of this article from another member state to the Republic in order to provide services, the person in question must inform the Council with a prior written declaration submitted by any means, regarding any insurance guarantees or similar means of personal or collective protection regarding his professional liability in the Republic. This declaration is renewed annually if the provider intends to temporarily or occasionally provide services in the Republic during the year in question.
(4) During the first provision of services or in the event of a substantial change in status, certified by the documents referred to in subsection (3) from another member state in the Republic to provide services, the Council requires the declaration to be accompanied by the following supporting documents-
(a) proof of nationality of the provider;
(b) a certificate that the provider is legally established in a Member State for the exercise of the activities of a real estate agent and that he has not been prohibited, even temporarily, from the exercise of these activities at the time of the granting of the certificate;
(c) evidence of his professional qualifications;
(d) in the event that the profession of real estate agent is not legislated in the Member State of establishment, evidence that he has practiced the profession of real estate agent in the Member State of establishment full-time for at least two years in the previous ten years; and
(e) all the information included in article 13 of the Law on the Recognition of Professional Qualifications.
(5) The provision of real estate agent services in the Republic is carried out on the basis of the professional title of the Member State of establishment, which is written in the official language of the Member State of establishment.
(6) A natural or legal person who provides real estate agent services in the Republic pursuant to this article must have the insurance coverage provided for in subsection (1) of article 19, unless they have equivalent insurance coverage for professional liability, provided by an insurance company that operates legally within the European Union and covers for the Republic the risks referred to in subsection (1) of article 19, and is subject to the provisions of Parts V, VI, VII and VIII hereof Law.

## Public order and public safety
16.-(1) Regardless of the provisions of articles 13, 14 and 15, the Council, with a reasoned decision, which is taken with the consent of the Minister, may, for specific reasons of public order or public security concerning the person affected by the decision -
(a) reject the application for registration of a person in the Register of Real Estate Agents, or
(b) delete a person from the Register of Estate Agents, or
(c) prohibit a natural person who is a citizen of a member state or a legal person established under the legislation of a member state from providing services as a real estate agent in the Republic.
(2) Before taking the decision provided for in subsection (1), the Council must notify the affected person in writing of its intention and give him the opportunity to submit in writing within a reasonable time all the relevant objections or representations that he wishes to submit to the Council either in person or through a lawyer.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Issuance of annual license.
17.-(1) Upon application by any person registered in the relevant Register and upon payment of the specified fee, the Council will issue an annual license to practice the profession of real estate agent or assistant real estate agent, as the case may be, in the form determined by the Council.
(2) Every annual license issued pursuant to this article shall normally expire, unless it is previously canceled or suspended pursuant to the provisions of article 18, on December 31 of each year for which it has been issued and may be renewed under the same terms and conditions as its issuance.

## Cancellation or suspension of annual leave
18.-(1) When at any time after the issuance or renewal of the annual license to practice a profession, the holder thereof wishes for any reason to be deleted from the relevant Register pursuant to the provisions of article 14, the annual license of the deleted holder thereof shall automatically be cancelled.
(2) The Council may, in exercising its disciplinary jurisdiction pursuant to Part V, cancel or suspend the annual license to practice a profession as a result of imposing a disciplinary penalty.
(3) In case of cancellation or suspension of the validity of any annual license pursuant to subsections (1) or (2), the holder thereof is obliged to return it to the Council within 15 days from the date of notification of the relevant decision.
(4) Failure to comply without any reasonable cause with the obligation referred to in subsection (3) constitutes a criminal offense punishable by the penalties provided for in subsection (7) of article 13.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Insurance coverage
19.-(1) Subject to the other requirements and conditions specified in this Law or the Regulations issued pursuant to it, no person shall act as a real estate agent unless an insurance contract has been concluded between him and any licensed insurer, for the insurance coverage of the real estate agent for an amount of 200,000 euros in the event of his liability for paying compensation to a buyer, due to a breach of his legal obligation real estate agent for full information of the buyer, in accordance with the provisions of article 21 and a copy of this submitted to the Council by the real estate agent concerned. The original or true copy of the insurance contract is posted in a prominent place in the real estate agent's office.
(2) For the purposes of this article, "licensed insurer" means the insurer who, by virtue of the Law on Insurance Companies, holds a license to carry out insurance operations in the insurance sector related to the insurance coverage obligation imposed by virtue of subsection (1).

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Real estate agent in real estate transactions
20.-(1) The mediation of a real estate agent is not necessary to achieve a real estate transaction.
(2) When in any real estate transaction the person mediating is a licensed real estate agent, he is entitled to claim and collect the agreed fee:
It is understood that unless otherwise agreed, the agreed fee will amount to 3% of the value of the real estate transaction and will be due and payable upon the completion of the real estate transaction, i.e. upon the conclusion of the sale or rental agreement.
(3) In case of failure to achieve a real estate transaction for which the real estate agent does not bear any responsibility, he is entitled to a reasonable fee, taking into account the time spent and the expense thereof.
(4) A licensed real estate agent is not entitled to mediate a real estate transaction when he has an interest in the property that is the subject of the transaction unless he informs the buyer of this interest in writing:
It is provided that, in the case of a licensed real estate agent who is a natural person, an interest is considered to exist when the real estate agent or a person related to him, by blood or bloodline, up to the third degree of consanguinity, has an ownership or other interest in the relevant property:
It is further provided that, in the case of a licensed real estate agent who is a legal entity, an interest is deemed to exist when the said legal entity, or a consultant, director, secretary, shareholder, partner or other officer of the said legal entity or a person related to them, by blood or bloodline, up to the third degree of consanguinity, has an ownership or other interest in the relevant property.
(5) When a real estate transaction reached through the mediation of a third party is followed by a transfer of any property, during the transfer of the property the interested parties are obliged to indicate in the relevant transfer declaration the name and address of the person who mediated, as well as the amount of the fee paid.
(6) A person who has collected any fee referred to in the previous paragraph, is required to deposit an amount equivalent to 2/10000 of the amount of the transfer declaration to the Council's fund within 30 days from the date of collection:
It is understood that in case of omission, the Council will be able to recover the amount in question plus interest at 8% from the date the payment became due as a civil debt.
(7) A person who omits or refuses to comply with the obligations pursuant to subsections (5) and (6), or in compliance with them, knowingly provides any false or inaccurate information, commits an offense and, in case of conviction, is subject to imprisonment for up to three months or a fine of up to one thousand euros or both of these penalties.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Obligation to inform about physical and legal characteristics of a real estate transaction
21. A real estate agent, regardless of whether he is acting at the behest of the seller or the buyer, must inform the intended buyer of a property about all the data related to the physical condition of the property, the real rights that are burdened by it, as well as about any other restrictions that have been imposed on the property by virtue of a legislative, judicial, administrative or other governmental act or decision:
Provided that a real estate agent will not be considered to be in breach of his above obligation, if-
(a) the failure to provide information on any information is due to reasons beyond his control or the control he is required to carry out in the normal course of his profession, and
(b) the real estate agent informs the interested parties of his inability to do so in a timely manner and without fail before the completion of the real estate transaction.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Right of a licensed real estate agent to express an opinion on the value of a real estate transaction
22. Regardless of the provisions of any other law, a real estate agent may, in the context of mediation for a real estate transaction, express his opinion as to the value of the said real estate transaction, if this is requested by either the seller or the buyer.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Estate agent details in documents and advertisements
23. Every licensed real estate agent must in every document or advertisement used in the exercise of his profession write or mention the phrase "licensed real estate agent", followed by the registration number and the number of the license to practice the profession.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Office maintenance by real estate agent
24. Every licensed real estate agent must maintain an office with suitable furniture and equipment and have his registration certificate and license posted in a prominent place and clearly display on the front of the office the name and the words "licensed real estate agent", the registration number in the Register of Real Estate Agents and, in case a trade name has been declared to the Council, the trade name in question.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Responsibility of a licensed real estate agent for the operation of each office
25.-(1) No office may operate in the Republic and provide real estate agent services unless a licensed real estate agent has the obligation to ensure that the said office complies with the provisions of this Law.
(2) The office owner who operates in violation of the provisions of subsection (1) is guilty of an offense and is liable, in the case of a first conviction, to a fine not exceeding 3,500 euros, and in the case of a second conviction, to a prison sentence not exceeding twelve months or to a fine not exceeding 7,000 euros, or to both of these penalties.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## False or inaccurate statements or representations
26.-(1) An estate agent who, in any notice, advertisement or document or on any sign posted on the facade or inside his office, describes himself falsely or inaccurately or uses a name or trade name different from that under which he is registered in the Register of Estate Agents or falsely represents that he holds any diploma or degree or any specialty or special qualification relating to real estate or real estate transactions, is guilty of a criminal offense and, on conviction, is liable to the penalties provided for in subsection (3) of section 33.
(2) A person who, through a document, advertisement or any other act or omission, misleads as to the identity of the licensed real estate agent mediating for a specific real estate transaction is guilty of an offense and, in case of conviction, is subject to the penalties provided for in subsection (4) of article 32.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Invalidity of contracts
27. Any agreement, written or oral, which refers to the assignment of real estate brokerage to an unregistered real estate agent or a registered but not holder of a valid annual license or a service provider who does not act under the provisions of this Law, regardless of the case, is void.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Disciplinary offenses
28.-(1) A registered Real Estate Agent commits a disciplinary offense and is subject to disciplinary proceedings before the Council if-
(a) commits any criminal offense involving dishonesty or moral turpitude, or
(b) has obtained registration on the Register of Estate Agents or obtained an annual license by using false or fraudulent representations or statements, or
(c) has acted or omitted to do anything in a manner that amounts to a breach of any of the duties or obligations of an estate agent, or
(d) in the exercise of his profession exhibits dishonest or dishonorable conduct or any other conduct or behavior inappropriate or incompatible with his profession or degrades the prestige and reputation of the profession of real estate agent, or
(e) aid or abet virtual real estate work.
(2) For the purposes of this article, the term "duties and obligations of a real estate agent" includes any duty or obligation imposed on a real estate agent pursuant to the provisions of this Law or the regulations issued pursuant thereto or any other legislative provision in force.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Disciplinary sanctions
29.-(1) The following disciplinary sanctions may be imposed by the Council pursuant to the provisions of this Law-
(a) reprimand;
(b) a monetary penalty not exceeding two thousand euros;
(c) suspension of annual leave not exceeding five years;
(d) deletion of the estate agent's name from the Register of Estate Agents.
(2) More than one disciplinary sanction shall not be imposed for the same disciplinary offence:
Provided that the sanction of reprimand may be imposed together with any other sanction provided for in subsection (1):
It is further provided that the disciplinary sanctions pursuant to subsection (1) may be imposed in conjunction with the imposition of an obligation to pay the costs of the proceedings before the Council.
(3) In the event that the real estate agent's name is deleted from the Register of Real Estate Agents as a result of a disciplinary sanction imposed pursuant to this Law, the deleted real estate agent is deprived of the right to re-register for such a period of time as the Council wanted to determine with the decision on deletion.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Investigation of disciplinary offences
30.-(1) When it is reported to the Council or in any other way comes to its attention that a registered real estate agent may have committed any disciplinary offense referred to in subsection (1) of article 28, then the Council immediately ensures that an investigation is conducted in accordance with a procedure determined by regulations issued pursuant to this Law.
(2) When the investigation carried out pursuant to subsection (1) prima facie proves the commission of a disciplinary offense, the affected registered real estate agent is informed in writing of the case against him and he is given an opportunity to be heard either in person or through a lawyer.
(3) The hearing of the case shall be conducted in accordance with a procedure determined by regulations issued pursuant to this Law.
(4) The Board may by its decision either find the registered estate agent concerned guilty of all or any of the disciplinary offenses with which he is charged and impose on him any of the disciplinary sanctions which the circumstances of the case would warrant or discharge him from the charge. These decisions of the Council are announced to the press.
(5) Every person, other than the accused, including any registered real estate agent, who is duly summoned to appear before the Board and fails without cause to appear at the time and place stated in the summons or during the hearing of the case or who refuses to answer any question lawfully put, shall be guilty of an offense and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding five hundred euro.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Relationship between criminal and disciplinary prosecution
31.-(1) When criminal proceedings are instituted against a registered real estate agent, no disciplinary proceedings may be instituted or continued against him for reasons related to the criminal proceedings until such time as it is finally concluded.
(2) A registered estate agent who is prosecuted for a criminal offense and who is ultimately acquitted may not be prosecuted for the same charge, but may be prosecuted for a disciplinary offense arising out of his conduct which is related to the criminal prosecution but does not raise the same issue as that of the charge in the criminal prosecution.
(3) Where a registered real estate agent is convicted of a criminal offense which, in the judgment of the Board, involves dishonesty or moral turpitude, whether such conviction is upheld on appeal or not appealed, the Board shall receive the earliest copy of the record of the proceedings of the trial court or, as the case may be, the appeal court and without further investigation of the case proceed to impose the disciplinary sanction of deleting his name from the Register.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Disciplinary provisions for service providers
32.-(1) A person who, on a temporary and occasional basis, provides services to the Republic, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, is subject to the provisions of this Part by analogy.
(2) For the persons referred to in subsection (1), instead of the disciplinary sanction provided for in paragraph (c) of subsection 1 of article 29, the Council may impose a ban on providing services to the Republic for a period not exceeding five years.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Offenses
33.-(1) No person is entitled-
(a) practice the profession of real estate agent or in any way act as a real estate agent.
(b) hold himself out or advertise as a real estate agent or trade under any name, trade name or title that contains the words “real estate agent”, “real estate consultants”, “real estate business” or “real estate agency” or other identical or similar phrases or words in any language or in any other way implying that he is engaged in real estate brokerage.
(c) to conduct guided tours of any person who wishes to purchase immovable property in relation to immovable property, which during the relevant time is not registered in his name or in the name of his relative by blood or bloodline up to the second degree or in the name of a person for the management of whose property he holds a general power of attorney or in the name of a company of which he is a director, executive advisor or employee.
(d) to advertise or promote or propose any real estate purchase or sale or other real estate transaction regarding immovable property, which at the material time is not registered in his name or in the name of a relative by blood or blood relation up to the second degree or in the name of a person for the management of whose property he holds a general power of attorney or in the name of a company of which he is a director, executive advisor or employee.
(e) knowingly publishes or arranges for the publication of advertisements that do not clearly and completely state the name and contact information of its owner at the material time.
(f) to claim or collect or acquire rights by virtue of a contract in respect of any remuneration in respect of services provided or to be provided which are related, directly or indirectly, to the work of the real estate agent.
unless that person is a registered real estate agent and holds a valid annual license to practice the profession issued under section 17 or falls within the provisions of section 15 and has not been banned under section 16.
(2) Irrespective of the provisions of subsection (1), it does not constitute exercising the profession of real estate agent or otherwise carrying out real estate brokerage:
(a) The advertisement, promotion or proposal by a land and building development entrepreneur of any real estate purchase or sale or other real estate transaction with respect to immovable property that is a product of his business activity arising from a consideration agreement with the registered owner of the property;
(b) any action taken pursuant to an order of a court or by an administrator/receiver, provisional administrator or liquidator of a company or by an administrator or receiver of a person in bankruptcy;
(c) any action taken by an assistant real estate agent or an employee of a licensed real estate agent on the instructions of the licensed real estate agent to whom he is employed.
(3) A person who acts in violation of the provisions of subsection (1) is guilty of an offense and, in case of conviction, is liable to imprisonment not exceeding twelve months or to a fine not exceeding three thousand five hundred euros or to both of these penalties.
(4) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (3), a person who violates or fails to comply with any prohibition, duty or obligation imposed by or under any of the provisions of this Law or the Regulations issued pursuant thereto commits an offense and is liable, unless another penalty is specifically provided, to a fine not exceeding two thousand euros and, in the case of a continuing offence, to an additional fine which does not exceed thirty-five euros for each day or part of a day during which the violation or omission continues after the commission of the offense.
(5) When an offense is committed by virtue of this Law or any of the ordinances issued pursuant to it, each of the following persons shall be deemed to have participated in the commission of the offense and to be the perpetrator thereof and may be prosecuted, tried and punished unreasonably-
(a) any person who actually commits the act or omission which constitutes the criminal offence.
(b) any person who does or omits to do something with a view to enabling the commission of the criminal offense by another or to assist in the commission of the offense by another.
(c) any person who promotes, assists or incites another to commit the offence.
(d) any person who solicits or induces or attempts to induce another person to commit the criminal offence.
(e) any person who carries out any act preparatory to the commission of the criminal offence.
(6) When a legal person commits an offense under this Law, any director, secretary or other officer of that legal person or any person who appears to be acting in any such capacity, who authorizes or incites or permits the commission of the act or omission which constitutes the offence, that person, as well as the legal person is of this offense and in case of conviction he is subject to the penalties provided by this Law for the specific offense.

## Unlawful Activity Suspension Order
34.-(1) Following a unilateral (ex parte) application submitted by the Council at any stage after the registration of an indictment in a Court pursuant to the provisions of this Law, with the exception of Article 38, the Court shall issue an order prohibiting any defendant from carrying out real estate brokerage, as well as an order suspending the operation of any office or building connected to the offense under consideration, after being satisfied that-
(a) the indictment contains reference to offenses under this Law. and
(b) there is prima facie evidence linking a specific office or premises to the offense being prosecuted:
Provided that notwithstanding the provisions of any law or regulation or other practice, the issuance of the said ordinances shall not require the existence of any other condition or the existence of exigent circumstances:
It is further understood that with regard to the form of the application, the provisions of the Institutions of Civil Procedure concerning intermediate unilateral (ex parte) applications are followed.
(2) In addition to the imposition of the penalty and after taking into account whether there is a reasonable risk of committing a new offense in the future, the Court before which a person is convicted for the offense provided for in this Law may order the convicted person-
(a) cease or suspend any acts or practices relating to the criminal offense of which he has been convicted.
(b) close and keep closed any office or premises in relation to which the criminal offense of which he has been convicted has been committed;
immediately or within such a period and under such conditions as the court deems appropriate or necessary to determine in the decree, with the aim of more effectively implementing the provisions of this Law and the regulations issued pursuant thereto.
(3) A person against whom a decree has been issued pursuant to this article and who fails or neglects to comply with such decree within the time period specified therein, is guilty of a criminal offense and, on conviction, is liable to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years.

## Fines fall to the Council
35. All fines imposed in respect of any criminal offenses under this Act shall, upon their imposition by the Court, vest in the Council and be paid into its treasury as soon as they are collected.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Appointment of Inspectors, their duties and responsibilities
36.-(1) The Council may assign to a committee, member, officer or officers thereof (hereinafter referred to as "Inspectors") the task of checking compliance or implementation of the provisions of this Law or the regulations issued pursuant to it.
(2) The duties and responsibilities of the Inspector include, among others, the following:
(a) investigating, taking depositions and securing any other testimony necessary for establishing criminal or disciplinary violations of this Law and the regulations issued pursuant to it;
(b) the obligation to appear as a witness before the courts or the Board in the trial of criminal or, as the case may be, disciplinary offenses under this Act or the regulations made thereunder;
(c) the performance of any other preparatory act deemed necessary to promote or achieve the taking of legal measures against any person who violates or fails to comply with the provisions of this Law or the regulations issued pursuant to it.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Power of entry for audit purposes
37.-(1) An inspector, in the performance of his duties and responsibilities under section 36, has the power, at any reasonable time and by showing his document of authorisation, to enter any property other than that used as a residence, which he reasonably believes is being used for the purposes of practicing the profession of real estate agent, and to demand from the owner or the person who appears to have the supervision or control of it real estate the presentation of any documents, as well as the provision of any information deemed necessary for the control and verification of compliance with the provisions of this Law or the Regulations issued pursuant to it.
(2) When, from the inspection under subsection (1), the Inspector forms a reasonable belief that any offense under this Act has been committed, then he may seize any documents or other evidence or take copies thereof if he believes that they may be needed for evidentiary purposes in future criminal proceedings.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Judicial search warrant
38.-(1) Subject to the rest of the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law relevant to the issuance and execution of search warrants, a Court based on a sworn complaint-
(a) after being satisfied that there are reasonable grounds to believe—
(i) that any articles, books or documents, which an Inspector has power under section 36 to inspect, are on any premises and that the inspection thereof is likely to reveal evidence of the commission of an offense under this Act; or
(ii) that an offense under this Act has been or is being committed or is about to be committed on any premises; and
(b) having also been satisfied that entry to the premises has been obstructed or is likely to be obstructed,
may issue a court warrant allowing the Inspector to enter the premises, using force if necessary.
(2) An inspector who enters premises pursuant to this article may have with him or be accompanied by such other persons, including police bodies, or such equipment as he may deem necessary for the particular case.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Obstruction of authorized Inspector
39.-(1) A person who -
(a) willfully obstructs an Inspector from acting under this Act, or
(b) willfully fails to comply with any demand lawfully made by the Inspector under section 37, or
(c) without reasonable cause fails to provide the above Inspector with any other assistance or information which he reasonably wished to request in the exercise of his powers under article 37,
commits an offense and, on conviction, is liable to imprisonment not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding two thousand euros or to both.
(2) A person who, by providing any information requested of him in accordance with article 37, knowingly makes an inaccurate statement, commits an offense and, in case of conviction, is liable to imprisonment not exceeding twelve months or to a fine not exceeding four thousand euros, or to both of these penalties.
(3) The Court before which an offense under subsection (1) or (2) of this section is tried may, in addition to the penalty imposed, order the convicted person to pay to the Council such other sum by way of compensation for any damages caused as a result of the commission of such offenses as the Court thinks just.
(4) Nothing in this article or in article 37 may be interpreted as imposing on a person the obligation to answer any question or give any information by which he could incriminate himself.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Financial self-sufficiency and the Council's treasury
40.-(1) The Council maintains a fund in which all its revenues and other resources are deposited, from which all its operating expenses will be paid.
(2) The Council ensures and manages the fund in such a way as to achieve the greatest possible financial self-sufficiency and self-financing.
(3) The Council appoints an approved auditor or auditors to audit the accounts of the fund, and the audited accounts are submitted annually to the Minister for his observations.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Fund resources
41. The income and other resources of the fund can come from-
(a) the fees imposed and collected pursuant to the provisions of this Law and the regulations issued pursuant thereto.
(b) pecuniary penalties or sanctions imposed following a criminal or disciplinary conviction.
(c) any donations, contributions or other donations made voluntarily for the benefit of the fund. and
(d) sales of various publications issued by the Council from time to time.

*(71(I)/2010)*

## Issuance of regulations
42.-(1) The Council may, with the approval of the Council of Ministers, issue regulations for the determination of any matter that needs or is susceptible to determination, as well as for the better application of the provisions of this Law.
(2) In particular and without affecting the generality of subsection (1), the regulations issued under this section may provide for all or any of the following matters -
(a) the determination of the fees for any application, examination, registration in the Register of Real Estate Agents and in the Register of Assistant Real Estate Agents or the issuance or renewal of a license prescribed under this Law;
(b) the determination of the maximum or minimum rate of commission which, in the absence of a written agreement between the parties, they are entitled to collect, as well as the person from whom said commission must be paid;
(c) the manner and time of conducting the examinations required or permitted by virtue of this Law, as well as the determination of the subject to be examined in them;
(d) the more specific rules of conduct to be observed by registered real estate agents in the exercise of the profession;
(e) the process of investigating and adjudicating disciplinary offences;
(f) the conditions of employment and service of employees of the Council, including service plans, pension plans and medical care plans;
(g) the determination of matters related to the exercise of the profession of real estate agent.
(3) Regulations issued pursuant to subsection (1) shall be submitted to the House of Representatives for approval.
(4) No provision of this Law may be interpreted as limiting the powers or responsibilities of the Council due to non-issuance of relevant regulations pursuant to this article.

## Cancellations and reservations
43.-(1) The Real Estate Agents Laws of 2004 and 2007 are repealed.
(2) Notwithstanding the repeal of the Real Estate Agents Laws of 2004 and 2007 (hereinafter referred to as the repealed laws)-
(a) any regulations, ordinances or other regulatory acts, made or issued under the repealed Acts and in force immediately before the commencement of this Act, shall be deemed to have been made or issued under this Act and shall, except to the extent inconsistent with this Act, continue in force until they are amended or replaced;
(b) any real estate agent's registration, certificate of registration or annual license made or issued under the repealed Acts and which was valid and effective immediately before the commencement of this Act shall be deemed to have been made or issued under the corresponding provisions of this Act and shall continue to be valid and effective unless and until revoked, suspended or terminated under the provisions of this Act;
(c) by virtue of the repealed laws existing immediately before the coming into force of this Law the Board of Registration of Real Estate Agents, shall continue to function and exercise the powers and duties of the Board established by virtue of article 3 of this Law, and its existing members shall continue to hold their position as if they had been appointed or elected by or under this Law until the expiration of their term for which they had appointed or elected.

## Transitional provisions
44.-(1) Upon the entry into force of this Law, the Council established pursuant to Article 3 automatically becomes the legal owner, beneficiary or debtor, as the case may be, of every asset, right, claim, debt or obligation acquired or created by the Council during the validity of the repealed laws.
(2) The Register of Real Estate Agents, which was maintained pursuant to the repealed laws, shall be deemed to be part of the Register to be maintained pursuant to this Law.
(3) Every document, which refers to any provision of the repealed laws, shall be interpreted as referring to the corresponding provision, if any, of this Law.
(4) All appointments of officers or authorized inspectors under the repealed Acts, which were made and were in force immediately before the coming into force of this Act, shall be deemed to have been made under this Act and shall continue to have corresponding effect.
(5) All registrations of persons under the repealed laws in the Registry of Real Estate Agents maintained pursuant to the repealed laws, which were made and were valid immediately before the entry into force of this Law, will be considered to have been made pursuant to this Law and will continue to be valid, and the persons who were thus registered cannot be deleted from the Register for the sole reason that they do not hold the qualification provided for in subparagraph (v) of paragraph (a) of subsection (1) of article 11 of this Law.
(6) Applications to the Council, the examination of which is pending at the time this Law enters into force, will be handled and decided in accordance with the provisions of this Law:
Provided that, persons who are able to prove that, until the date of entry into force of this Law, they had graduated from a secondary school recognized in the Republic or in any other member state and had, on the said date of entry into force, or for any period lasting at least three months within the five years preceding the said date, as their main occupation in the Republic or in any other member state real estate work, may be registered in the Register Real estate agents if they prove that they have acquired in the Republic or in any other member state five years of experience in relation to real estate work.
(7) Where any time limit provided for in the repealed laws had been activated and continues to run at the commencement of this Law and there is a corresponding provision in this Law, this Law shall apply as if the corresponding provision had come into force at the time when such period or time limit began to run.

## Special provision in relation to registration in the Register of Real Estate Agents
45. Regardless of the provisions of subparagraph (iv) of paragraph (a) of subsection (1) of article 11 and the provisions of subsection (6) of article 44 of this Law, a person who submits an application for registration in the Register of Real Estate Agents until 31h December 2017 may be registered in it, provided that he is able to prove that-
(a) Holds a secondary school leaving certificate or its equivalent, which was obtained, before the date of commencement of the Estate Agents (Amendment) Law 2010, in the Republic or in another Member State or in a third country;
Provided that, in the event that the high school diploma or its equivalent was obtained in a third country, for the purposes of registration in the Register of Real Estate Agents, pursuant to the provisions of this article, it is required that the interested person present a relevant certificate issued by the Ministry of Education and Culture of the Republic or the competent authority of the third country, which certifies that the relevant high school of the third country is recognized in that country. and
(b) by the date of submission of his application for registration in the Register of Real Estate Agents, pursuant to the provisions of this article, he has acquired in the Republic or in any other member state a total of five years of experience in relation to real estate work:
Provided that, the possession of five years of experience is certified by the presentation by the person who submits an application for registration in the Registry of Real Estate Agents of a certificate for the payment of social security contributions in relation to the employment in question for at least five (5) years.
