## The Sale of Real Estate (Special Execution) Law of 2011 (81(I)/2011)
The House of Representatives votes as follows:

## Short title
1. This Act shall be cited as the Sale of Real Estate (Special Execution) Act 2011.

*(81(I)/2011)*

## Interpretation
2. (1) In this Law, unless the text states otherwise -
"purchaser" means the person in whose name, in accordance with the contract, immovable property is to be transferred, for consideration, and includes an assignee in a contract of assignment;
"immovable property" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Immovable Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Act and includes a right in rem acquired by the registration of a lease, which is registered in accordance with the provisions of the same Act;
"prohibition" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Transfer and Mortgage of Immovables Act;
"competent Provincial Land Office" has the meaning assigned to this term by the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law;
"competent Provincial Land Registry Officer" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Transfer and Mortgage of Immovables Law;
"Director" means the Director of the Department of Lands and Surveys of the Ministry of the Interior;
"Court" means the circuit court of the province in which the immovable property is situated or the High Court in the exercise of its appellate jurisdiction;
"registered" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Real Estate Transfer and Mortgage Law;
"registration of immovable property" means the registration of immovable property in the Land Registry of the competent District Land Office in the name of the person referred to in this decree, following a Court order for the specific performance of a contract;
"special performance of a contract" means the execution of obligations arising from a contract by order of a Court, including the registration of immovable property which is the subject of a contract, in relation to which the conditions of this Law have been met;
"collateral assignee" means the assignee in a collateral assignment agreement filed under section 11;
"collateral assignor" means the assignor of a collateral assignment agreement filed under section 11;
"encumbrance" has the meaning assigned to this term by the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law;
"filing of the contract" means the filing of a true copy of the contract with the provincial land office of the province in which the real property is located to create an encumbrance on the real property covered by the contract;
"Land Registry" has the meaning assigned to this term by the Real Estate Transfer and Mortgage Law;
"limited joint property" has the meaning assigned to that term by the Real Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Act;
"seller" means the person or persons referred to in a contract as the seller and who are either registered owners of the whole of the immovable property or registered owners of undivided ideal shares and includes the registered owners of immovable property in respect of which they have entered into a distribution agreement, so that each owner is entitled to the free possession, use and disposal of the portion apportioned to him, and the grantor of the immovable property in a contract consideration;
"contract" means a contract of sale or exchange or assignment or consideration or an agreement to distribute immovable property;
"concession agreement" means a contract between the grantor, who is the registered owner of immovable property, and the grantor, who undertakes its development, in return for the transfer to him -
(a) part or undivided ideal portion of the immovable property, or
(b) whole or a part or an undivided ideal share of the real estate, with a simultaneous undertaking to transfer part of it, after the development, from the grantor to the provider of the real estate.
"assignment agreement" means the written agreement between the buyer in a contract (assignor) and a third party (assignee), by which rights or similar obligations, if any, of the buyer are assigned, by the assignor to the assignee, whether gratuitously or otherwise;
"collateral assignment agreement" means the written agreement between the purchaser of a valid contract (assignor) and a third party (assignee), by which rights of the assignor under the contract are assigned by the assignor to the assignee, for the purpose of securing a pecuniary obligation;
"portion of immovable property" means any portion of immovable property, other than an undivided ideal portion, which is specified on a plan or plan or otherwise and includes a plot, floor or part of a floor, room, office, apartment, residence, shop or any other part of land or building, which, subject to the provisions of the Real Property (Possession, Registration and Valuation) Law, may be registered separately;
"Minister" means the Minister for the Interior.

## Formulations necessary for the submission of the contract
3. (1) The deposit of the contract by any of the contracting parties is accepted, only if -
(a) there is an entry in the Land Registry of the competent Provincial Land Office, in the name of at least one of the sellers in relation to the immovable property-
(i) which is the subject of the contract, or
(ii) which includes the portion of immovable property which is the subject of the contract:
It is provided that, if the object of the contract is a part of co-owned immovable property, for which there is no separate entry in the Land Registry of the competent Provincial Land Registry and the sellers are not all the registered owners, it is accompanied by a distribution agreement, signed by all the registered owners of the immovable property, whose signatures are duly certified, and the part which is the subject of the contract corresponds to a at least by the sellers in the contract, who has the right to free possession, use and disposal thereof.
(b) the contract is in writing, contains sufficient particulars of the identity of the parties to the contract, adequately describes the real property that is the subject of the contract, states the consideration and is signed by all the parties to the contract; and
(c) the contract is filed within six (6) months from the date of its signature, at the provincial land office of the Department of Land and Surveying of the province where the immovable property is located:
Provided that -
(i) in the case of a contract of consideration, the period provided for above for the provider begins with the transfer of the immovable property to the counterparty;
(ii) in the event that, during the said period, there is no registration in the Land Registry of the competent Provincial Land Registry in the name of at least one of the sellers in relation to the immovable property, which is the subject of the contract, or which includes the part of the immovable property which is the subject of the contract, the contract may be filed within six (6) months from the date of said registration;
(iii) if the buyer is a transferee in a contract of assignment, which is entered into before the filing of the contract to which it relates and within the period of six (6) months from the date of signing the contract, the filing of the contract is accompanied by a copy of the contract of assignment and a certificate of settlement of the tax imposed by the Capital Gains Taxation Law;
(iv) if a contract of assignment is entered into which concerns a contract that is deposited in the competent Provincial Land Office, a copy of the contract of assignment is presented to the competent Provincial Land Office within six (6) months from the signing of the contract of assignment and is attached to the contract together with a certificate of settlement of the tax imposed by the Capital Gains Law.
(2) Irrespective of the provisions of the Contracts Law or any other law, it is prohibited to include in a contract or collateral assignment agreement, which is entered into after the effective date of this Law, a clause prohibiting the filing of the contract or the collateral assignment agreement and any such clause is considered void ab initio.

## Seller's Obligations
4. (1) The seller, before mortgaging immovable property which is the subject of a contract which has not been deposited by the buyer, has the obligation to deposit the contract in question in accordance with the provisions of article 3, provided that by the date of deposit of the contract the buyer has fulfilled his contractual obligations until then.
(1A) Regardless of the provisions of the Contracts Law, the seller includes as an integral part of the contract, which is concluded after the date of entry into force of the Sale of Real Estate (Special Execution) (Amendment) Law of 2023, a survey certificate of the immovable property which is the subject of the contract, which bears a date that does not differ from the date of signing the contract beyond of five (5) working days.
(1B) Regardless of whether there is a case of criminal liability under the provisions of this Law, in the event that a seller fails to comply with the obligation provided for in subsection (1A), the Director may impose on him an administrative fine which does not exceed ten thousand euros (€10,000), after having previously notified the seller by registered letter, informing him of the reasons for the whom he intends to act in this way and giving him the right to submit representations within a period of five (5) working days from the date of receipt of said notice.
(1C)(a) A seller who has been imposed an administrative fine pursuant to the provisions of subsection (1B) may, within a period of thirty (30) days from the date of notification to him of the decision on the imposition of the administrative fine, appeal to the Minister, requesting the review of said decision and in such a case, the Minister examines the appeal and, after hearing the appellant or giving him the opportunity to support in writing the reasons on which the appeal is supported, decides on the appeal within thirty (30) days from the date of the appeal and transmits its decision to the appellant.
(b) The Minister may by his decision to-
(i) confirm the contested decision.
(ii) set aside the contested decision.
(iii) amend the contested decision. or
(iv) issue a new decision to replace the challenged one.
(2) A seller who fails to act in accordance with subsections (1) and (1A) is guilty of an offense and, on conviction, is liable to imprisonment for up to two (2) years or to a fine not exceeding five thousand euros (€5 000) or to both.

## Consequences of filing a contract
5. (1) Subject to the other conditions of this Law, the filing of a contract constitutes an encumbrance on the immovable property that is the subject of the contract and the encumbrance created by the filing of the contract follows the order of priority it receives upon its filing:
Provided that, when the immovable property which is the subject of the contract is a portion of immovable property registered in the name of the seller, the encumbrance shall be upon the entire immovable property included in the registration existing in the name of the seller and, by making a separate registration for said portion, the encumbrance shall be limited only to the portion of the immovable property which is the subject of the contract.
(2) Irrespective of the provisions of the Transfer and Mortgage of Immovables Law, in the event that before the deposit of the contract there is already a deposited mortgage which encumbers the immovable property which is the subject of the contract, the buyer may pay to the mortgage lender, in accordance with the terms of repayment of the loan, the amount of the mortgage debt corresponding to the subject of the contract, such as the amount this is determined in the provisions of subsections (4), (5) and (6) of article 7 and the mortgage lender is obliged to accept it as an amount paid against the mortgage debt and in this case the real burden created by the deposit of the contract precedes the real burden of the pre-existing mortgage, regardless of the repayment of the entire mortgage debt:
Provided that, in relation to contracts that are deposited in the competent Provincial Land Office or that have been entered into before the date of entry into force of this Law, the provisions of this subsection shall apply provided that the written consent of the seller and the mortgagor has been obtained for this purpose.
(2A)(a) Irrespective of the provisions of the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law, in the event that prior to the filing of the contract, which is accompanied by Type A of the Annex, in accordance with the provisions of article 3A, there is already a deposited mortgage encumbering the immovable property which is the subject of the contract, the buyer and/or the seller, in the event that the latter received an advance payment from the buyer, upon signing the contract, deposits into the seller's bank account, which is specified in the written statement, the amount specified in Form A of the Appendix and the mortgage lender is obliged to accept it.
(b) Subject to the provisions of paragraph (a), any amount deposited by the buyer and/or seller into the bank account of the seller, which was specified in the written statement, including any advance received by the seller at the time of signing the contract, shall be considered a payment by the buyer under the contract, against or to repay, as the case may be, the sale price of the immovable property which is the subject of the contract contract.
(c) The mortgagor by accepting the amount specified in Type A of the Appendix gives the buyer a written confirmation of payment according to Type B of the Appendix and releases or eliminates the immovable property which is the subject of the contract from the said mortgage, in accordance with the provisions of the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law:
Provided that, the Director may impose on a mortgagor, who willfully fails to comply with the above obligation, an administrative fine which does not exceed one hundred thousand euros (€100,000) and notifies the mortgagor accordingly by registered letter, stating the reasons why the Director intends to act in this way, giving him the right to submit of performances within a period of five (5) working days from the date of receipt of the notification:
It is further provided that, a mortgage lender who has been imposed an administrative fine may, within thirty days (30) from the date of notification to him of the decision on the imposition of the administrative fine, file an appeal before the Minister for a review of the challenged decision and in such a case the Minister examines the appeal, after hearing the appellant or giving him the opportunity to support in writing the grounds on which the appeal is based, and decides on the appeal within thirty (30) days from the date of its exercise by notifying the applicant of his decision:
Provided further that, the Minister may by his decision to-
(3) The encumbrance created by the filing of the contract until the issuance of a decree for special performance of the contract, in accordance with article 7 of this Law, or payment of compensation in accordance with subsection (6) of this article, secures the respective amount that the buyer paid against the sale price or the respective amount of the value of the object of the consideration.
(4) The lien is valid until the annulment or withdrawal of the deposit of the contract by which it is created or until the expiry of the deadline for filing an appeal against a court decision ordering the deletion of the deposit and in case of filing an appeal until the issuance of a decision on the appeal.
(5) Regardless of the provisions of any other law and notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1) of this article regarding the encumbrance created by the deposit of the contract, the provisions of subsection (1) of article 29, subsection (1) of article 31, subsection (6) of article 41 and subsections (2) and (3) of article 42 of the Transfer and Real Estate Mortgage Law, as well as the provisions of any other law, which refer to a prior mortgage or to the disposition of the auction in relation to a subsequent mortgage, or to the forced sale of undivided property, or to the payment of damages due to forced expropriation or requisition, will apply as if such filing had created, mutatis mutandis, a mortgage under the aforementioned laws instead encumbrance pursuant to this Law:
It is understood that the provisions of subsection (1) of article 29 and subsection (1) of article 31 of the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law do not apply in cases where the contract has been filed before the entry into force of this Law and concerns the entire interest in the immovable property being sold, unless the buyer's consent is secured.
(6) In a case where the Court does not issue the requested decree for specific performance of a contract in accordance with the provisions of Article 7, but instead awards damages in favor of the buyer, provided that the buyer has fulfilled his obligations arising from the contract, the encumbrance created by the filing of the contract in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall operate as a mortgage, from the date of filing of the contract, in accordance with the provisions of the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law, in favor of the buyer for the amount of compensation mentioned in the Court's decision.
(7) The filing of a contract in accordance with the provisions of this Law does not act in such a way as to frustrate or delay the sale of the immovable property, which may have been ordered by the Court before the date of this filing.
(8) Irrespective of the provisions of the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law, in case of transfer of immovable property which is the subject of a contract which was filed with the competent Provincial Land Office after the date of entry into force of the Sale of Real Estate (Special Execution) (Amendment) Law of 2023 and is accompanied by Form A of the Annex, and provided that it has form B of the Appendix is issued, the transfer declaration is accepted at any competent Provincial Land Office and the immovable property is transferred regardless of any encumbrances or prohibitions which may be burdened by the immovable property or its owner and which follow the contract in priority:
Provided that, in the event that the immovable property, subject of the contract, is encumbered by a mortgage which is already registered at the time of acceptance of the contract in the competent Provincial Land Office and the buyer has paid the amount specified in Type A of the Appendix, the transfer declaration is accepted by the Director and the mortgagee is obliged to release or eliminate the immovable property, which is the subject of the contract, from the mortgage in question.
(9) In the event that the mortgagor fails to act in accordance with the provisions of this article, and provided that the purchaser has presented the payment certificate, according to Type B of the Appendix, duly signed and sealed by the mortgagor, the Director shall transfer the immovable property, subject of the contract, to the purchaser's name, regardless of any encumbrances or prohibitions which may be borne by the immovable property or its owner and which follow the contract in priority.
(10) The provisions of subsection (2) apply only in relation to a contract which has been filed with the Provincial Land Office before the date of commencement of the Sale of Real Estate (Special Execution) (Amendment) Law of 2023.
(11) In the event of a liquidation or bankruptcy order being issued against the seller, the provisions of the Companies Law and the Bankruptcy Law shall apply.

## Formulations necessary for the execution of the contract
6.-(1) Every contract is amenable to specific performance, by decree of the Court, issued pursuant to article 7 and provided that the following conditions have been met in relation to it:
(a) Subject to the provisions of subsection (2), the contract is filed in accordance with the provisions of subsection (1) of section 3 and the provisions of section 3A; and
(b) the action for a decree for specific performance of the contract is brought within the period of limitation provided in the law for the time being in force for the limitation of claims arising from breach of contract.
(2) In the event that the contract is in writing but has not been filed in accordance with the provisions of this Law or the contract is oral, the Court may order its special performance when it deems this to be fair and reasonable under the circumstances and as long as the rights of third parties resulting from prior encumbrances or prohibitions are not affected and it is satisfied that-
(a) the contract sufficiently specifies the identity of the contracting parties and the subject of the contract,
(b) there is an entry in the Land Registry of the competent Provincial Land Registry, in the name of at least one of the sellers in relation to the immovable property, which is the subject of the contract or which includes the part of the immovable property which is the subject of the contract.

## Execution of the contract
7. (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, the Court may by its decree order the special performance of the contract under any conditions it deems necessary.
(2) The decree for the special execution of a contract may also provide for an order to take all the necessary measures and steps to secure the certificates, permits or approvals required by any law in force for the separate registration of the real property in question and or the appointment of a person other than the seller to take the necessary measures or to take the necessary steps or to replace him, as well as an order for the costs involved.
(3) The decree of the Court regarding the costs required to complete the procedure for carrying out separate registration for the part of the immovable property that is the subject of the contract, is a burden as the registration of a court decision in accordance with article 53 of the Civil Procedure Law and burdens all parts of the immovable property for which separate registration is carried out.
(4) The Court may issue a decree of special execution also in the case that before the deposit of the contract there is already a deposited mortgage encumbering the immovable property which is the subject of the contract if it is satisfied that the buyer has paid against the mortgage debt the amount corresponding to the subject of the contract and in the event that this is a part of immovable property, the amount corresponding to the said part calculated on the basis of the coefficient of its value.
(4A) The Court may issue a decree of special execution in the case of a contract which has been filed with the competent Provincial Land Office in accordance with the provisions of paragraph (a) of article 3A and on which there is already a deposited mortgage, provided that it is satisfied that the buyer has paid the amount specified in the written statement which was issued according to Type A of the Appendix.
(5) For the purposes of subsection (4) the coefficient of the value of the portion of immovable property corresponds to the percentage of the value of said portion in relation to the total value of the mortgaged property and in the cases of jointly owned property does not include the value of the limited jointly owned property.
(6) The coefficient of value of each section that will result based on the urban planning permit is determined by the seller and is deposited in the Provincial Land Office before the sale of any section.
(7) In the event that the action for specific performance of the contract is brought by an assignee of collateral, the Court orders the registration of the object of the contract in the name of the buyer and the simultaneous registration of a mortgage on the new title.
(8) The provisions of subsections (4), (5) and (6) apply only in relation to a contract that was filed with the relevant District Land Office before the date of entry into force of the Sale of Real Estate (Special Execution) (Amendment) Law of 2023.

## Execution of court order
8.- (1) Any person who has secured a court decree ordering special performance of a contract must apply within one year from the date of its issuance to the competent Provincial Land Office for the registration of the immovable property, which is the subject of the contract, in the name of the person listed in the decree. The competent District Land Registry Officer, with the presentation of the decree or an official copy thereof and after the documents required for the registration in accordance with the provisions of the Transfer and Mortgage of Immovables Law, shall ensure that all the entries necessary for the implementation of the decree are made in the office's Land Registry, regardless of any encumbrances or prohibitions on the immovable property or its owner and which follow in priority the contract of which the specific performance is ordered, except for an encumbrance created by a Court order, which is issued in accordance with the provisions of subsections (2) and (3) of article 7:
Provided that, the competent District Land Registry Officer, who is required to carry out the registration of immovable property in accordance with a Court decree issued pursuant to the provisions of this Law or the person for whose benefit the decree was issued, may apply to the Court for instructions in relation to any issue that arises during the carrying out of such registration and the Court may issue such instructions as it deems necessary.
(2) In the event that there is a security assignment contract filed and the collateral assignor in whose favor a Court decree was issued ordering special performance of the contract refuses or neglects to take the necessary actions for the execution of this decree, the collateral assignee may take the said actions and address the competent Provincial Land Office as provided in subsection (1):
Provided that, the collateral assignee may apply to the Court for instructions in relation to any matter arising in relation to the above necessary actions for the execution of the decree and the Court may issue such instructions as it deems necessary.

## A right to special performance ceases if the property is not registered in the Land Registry within a certain time
9. (1) If any person, having secured a decree of a Court ordering specific performance of a contract, neglects or omits to apply for the registration of immovable property in his name and to perform all necessary acts and actions to enable the registration to be effected, within one year from the date of the decree, the right of such person to claim special performance of the contract shall cease and no registration of the property shall be effected in his name in Land Registry of the competent District Land Office, by virtue of the authorization of the decree, unless the period of validity of the decree is renewed in accordance with the provisions of subsection (2).
(2) The decree of the Court for special performance of a contract may be renewed for further periods which shall not exceed one year each, if the Court deems this just and reasonable under the circumstances:
Provided that, the application for renewal of the decree is submitted to the Court before the end of its period of validity and the Court may renew it regardless of whether the above period has already passed.

*(81(I)/2011)*

## Result of registration under a Court order for special execution
10. When immovable property is registered following the issuance of a Court order for the special performance of the contract to any person, such property, after its registration, vests in said person to the extent of the right of ownership and interest in such property by virtue of the contract and the Court, which has ordered the special performance of the contract, may order the delivery or securing of possession of the property to such person, as the Court deems proper.

*(81(I)/2011)*

## Collateral Assignment Agreement
11. (1) The transferee in a collateral assignment contract may file this contract with the Provincial Land Office of the province in which the immovable property which is the subject of the contract to which it relates is located, at any time after its signature and in case of deposit, the following provisions of this article shall apply.
(2) The deposit of the collateral assignment contract is accepted only if-
(a) the contract to which it relates is already deposited;
(b) is in writing, includes sufficient details of the identity of the contracting parties and is signed by the contracting parties whose signatures are duly certified;
(c) precisely identify the contract to which it refers; and
(d) adequately defines the monetary obligation it secures.
(3) The filing of the collateral assignment contract acts as a burden on the contract it concerns and maintains the order of priority of the encumbrance created by the filing of the contract it concerns, so that the transferee of collateral acquires the collateral burden created by the filing of the contract it concerns.
(4) In the event that the collateral assignor does not fulfill its obligations arising from the collateral assignment contract, the collateral transferee may file an action for special performance of the contract to which it relates, in accordance with the provisions of article 7, so that a decree of the Court may be issued in accordance with subsection (7) of the said article:
Provided that, the provisions of this subsection do not affect the right of the buyer, who is an assignor of collateral, to file an action for special performance of the contract under this Law.
(5) In the event that a collateral assignment contract is filed, the immovable property that is the subject of the contract to which it relates cannot be transferred pursuant to the Transfer and Mortgage of Real Estate Law, unless the deposit of the collateral assignment contract is previously withdrawn or deleted in accordance with the provisions of this article.
(6) In the event that the financial obligation secured by the collateral assignment contract is paid off or ceases to exist, the collateral assignee shall arrange for the withdrawal of its deposit, appearing before the Provincial Land Office of the province in which the immovable property which is the subject of the contract is located and bringing the relevant documents necessary for this purpose.
(7) Where the collateral transferee-
(a) refuses or neglects to proceed with the withdrawal of the deposit of the collateral assignment contract even if the secured monetary obligation has been paid or has ceased to exist, or
(b) refuses to accept payment of the amount of the monetary obligation for which the collateral assignment contract was granted, after it has become payable and to proceed with the withdrawal of the deposit, or
(c) is of unknown residence or is a company or cooperative that no longer exists or has died and the personal representative or his heirs are unknown and in these cases either the security assignor is unable for any of these reasons to pay the beneficiary the amount secured by the security assignment contract, after it has become payable, or the secured monetary obligation has been paid or has ceased to exist,
the collateral assignor may apply to the court and request the issuance of a decree ordering the deletion of the deposit of the collateral assignment agreement and the court may issue the decree in its judgment of law under the circumstances:
Provided that, the competent District Lands Officer, upon presentation to him of a decree issued pursuant to this subsection ordering the deletion of the deposit of the security assignment agreement, or an official copy thereof, ensures that the necessary actions are carried out for the implementation of the decree.
(8) In the event that a collateral assignment contract is filed, the collateral assignor may not, without the prior consent of the collateral assignee, -
(a) withdraws the deposit of the contract to which the collateral assignment contract relates,
(b) cancel the contract to which the collateral assignment contract relates, or
(c) enter into an assignment contract.
(9) In the event that a collateral assignment contract is filed and the seller terminates the contract to which the collateral assignment contract relates, he shall simultaneously inform the collateral transferee of the termination of the contract.
(10) Pursuant to this article, the consequences of the deposit of the collateral assignment agreement cease to exist upon the withdrawal of its deposit by the collateral transferee or upon the expiry of the deadline for filing an appeal against a court decision ordering its deletion and in case of filing an appeal with the issuance of a decision on the appeal.

## Power of the Court to allow the filing of a contract or the initiation of an action outside the time limit
12. Regardless of the provisions of this Law, the Court may, upon a relevant application, allow the filing of a contract or the filing of an action for special performance for contracts which remain in force and were entered into at any time, even if the time period provided for this purpose has passed, in accordance with the provisions of this Law or the deadline for their filing in accordance with the provisions of the Law on the Sale of Land (Special Execution) of the Law, as the case may be, when it deems this fair and reasonable for the protection of the buyer.

## Reservation of jurisdiction of the Court
13. Nothing contained in this Act shall be construed to deprive any Court of the right to award damages for breach of contract where the Court thinks fit, instead of ordering specific performance of the contract.

*(81(I)/2011)*

## Liability of heirs of seller
14. If any seller under a contract in relation to which the formalities specified by article 7 or 8 have been observed, dies after the conclusion of the contract, the buyer is entitled to claim from the administrator of the seller's property any remedy, as he is entitled by this Law to claim from the seller.

*(81(I)/2011)*

## Seller treatments
15. Where any purchaser of immovable property under a contract in respect of which the formalities prescribed by article 7 or 8 have been complied with refuses to pay the purchase price and accepts the property, the seller's remedy under the contract shall consist only of damages.

*(81(I)/2011)*

## Transitional provisions
16. (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of this Law, contracts which remain in force and were entered into at any time before the date of entry into force of this Law, even if the deadline for their submission provided for in the repealed Land Sale (Special Execution) Law has passed, may, subject to the provisions of article 3, be submitted within a period of six months from the date of entry into force of this Law.
(2) The provisions of this Law shall also apply in relation to contracts entered into before the date of entry into force of this Law and which are already filed with the competent Provincial Land Office before the entry into force thereof or which will be filed in accordance with the provisions of subsection (1) or article 16A regardless of whether any legal proceedings are pending in relation to them on the said date.

Without prejudice to the provisions of article 16, contracts which remain in force and were entered into at any time before the entry into force of the Sale of Real Estate (Special Execution) (Amendment) Law of 2012, even if the deadline for their submission provided for in the repealed Sale of Land (Special Execution) Law or in this Law, as the case may be, has passed, may, subject to the provisions of article 3, be filed within a period of six months from the date of entry into force of the Sale of Real Estate (Special Execution) (Amendment) Law of 2012.

## Commencement of this Law
17. The validity of this Law begins after three months have passed from the date of its publication in the Official Gazette of the Republic.

*(81(I)/2011)*

## Removal
18. From the effective date of this Law, the Land Sale (Special Execution) Law is repealed.

*(81(I)/2011)*
